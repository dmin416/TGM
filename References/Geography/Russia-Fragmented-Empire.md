## Collapse of the Romanov empire

- The **Great Russian Empire** (Romanov dynasty) **no longer exists** as a unitary state.
- A **February Revolution in 1911** ended Romanov rule (`Source/378.md`).
- **Tsar Nicholas II** and his family were tried and **executed by hanging** (not the OTL cellar killings).
- Initial revolutionary leadership was largely killed during the overthrow (**Kolchak’s** double-dealing and royalist intervention).
- **Vladimir Lenin / Ulyanov does not exist** in this timeline (`Source/048.md`). No Soviet state was established.

## Civil war landscape (January 1914 baseline)

The old empire is a **patchwork of warring factions**. Diplomats still say “Russia” by treaty habit; there is **no** single government.

| Faction | Leader | Backing | Heartland / notes |
|--------|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Western Russian Provisional National Government** (西露西亚临时国民政府) | **Kerensky** | **German / Saxon** | Western grain belt; economy and production chaotic under Kerensky; German grain imports ease Saxon unrest |
| **Kolchak** (“Supreme Autocrat”; **Universal Supreme Duchy of All Russia**, 全露西亚最高专制公国) | **Admiral Kolchak** | **Britain & France** | **Saint Petersburg** region and surrounds |
| **Denikin forces** | **General Denikin** | **Britain & France** | **Moscow** region and surrounds |
| **Lesser warlords** | various | mixed | e.g. **Black Sea Republic** (黑海共和国) and other small states between the big three |

**International Brigades** (German aligned) fight in Spain first; veterans are later **sent to Kerensky’s west** (`Source/292.md`, `144.md`).

## War arc (after general European war begins)

Britain and France escalate support to **finish** the Russian question so they can focus on Germany (`Source/292.md`).

1. **Kolchak** and **Denikin** receive heavy Entente aid; they absorb smaller warlords and **link up**.
2. They **defeat Kerensky’s western army** in a decisive battle and **advance on Kiev** to destroy the last western “rebel” holdout (`Source/142.md`, `372.md`).
3. **Kolchak** declares general mobilization for the **Universal Supreme Duchy of All Russia**; Saxon staff read this as eastern war (`Source/143.md`).
4. German **General Staff** grows **pessimistic**; Saxony may **commit a field army** east if the western provisional government falls (`Source/292.md`, `472.md`).
5. If Kolchak unifies the east under Entente patronage, Germany faces a **two-front** nightmare: a restored, **Britain-aligned Russia** on the border.

## Writer notes

- **Kerensky** is a **provisional revolutionary** leader, not a Bolshevik; there is no Leninist party or Red Army in this world.
- **Nicholas II** is dead; do not use OTL “Kaiser’s cousin the Tsar” beats unless flashback or foreign courts remember him.
- OTL **1913 imperial Russia** tables in some military refs are **background bleed**; use **this file** and [People.md](../Characters/People.md) for in-story Russia.
- Chinese upstream name: **露西亚**; Saxon/German cast often say **Russia** in English `Story/`.
