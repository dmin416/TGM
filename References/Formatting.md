# Formatting

Editorial, layout, and setting rules for *Trenches, Guns and Magic*. Verify facts against reference files in this folder.

**References:** [Countries.md](Countries.md) · [Formatting.md](Formatting.md) · [Glossary.md](Glossary.md) · [Magic.md](Magic.md) · [Military.md](Military.md) · [People.md](People.md) · [Places.md](Places.md) · [Theaters.md](Theaters.md)

---

## Before Writing — Verification Required

All weapons, equipment, names, and places must be verified against available reference files before writing.

Every weapon must match its historical introduction date. Every name must be accurate. No exceptions.

---

## Historical Accuracy

All weapons, equipment, doctrine, and military technology must be historically accurate for their date. The source novel makes historical errors and these must be silently corrected. German military competence and equipment history are not among the world's deliberate divergences and are treated as fully grounded in real history.

---

## Prose Style

Write in tight third-person limited filtered through Max's consciousness. Occasional narration from other characters' points of view is permitted when the scene calls for it.

No hyphens. No em dashes. No colons inside dialogue. No oxford commas.

Do not invent new similes or comparisons. Similes already present in the source are preserved as written, but Claude adds none of its own. This specifically forbids hollow recursive descriptors of the form "walked with the confidence of a person who had always known how to walk," "smiled the smile of someone who had smiled before," and any comparison where the vehicle restates the tenor. Also forbidden: weather-as-mood comparisons, body-part-as-object comparisons, and any "like a" or "as if" construction Claude generates itself.

Every sentence is at least four words long.

Every prose paragraph's opening sentence must contain the named subject of that paragraph. Paragraphs cannot open with pronouns (he, his, they, their, it, its) referring to that subject before the subject is named.

When a named subject leads two consecutive sentences, the second sentence must use a pronoun instead of repeating the name.

---

## Layout

Dialogue uses quotation marks and italics. Each line of dialogue starts on its own line.

Thoughts use italics. Each thought starts on its own line.

Sounds use bold onomatopoeia on their own line. Never describe a sound in prose when onomatopoeia can replace it.

System messages and intel readouts use code blocks.

Prose paragraphs are never broken up with blank lines. Only dialogue, thoughts, and sounds get their own lines. Narrative sentences that belong together stay in the same paragraph.

---

## Preservation

Every sentence from the source is preserved. Do not shorten, condense, trim, remove, or cut. Full detail is retained at all times. No editorialization, commentary, or meta remarks unless requested. Do not delete files unless asked.

No battles skipped or condensed unless requested.

# Formatting Supplement

## Setting

The story takes place on a magical version of Earth during WWI. No outcomes are predetermined. Max's history, abilities, and the quality of his actions are not discussed unless the player raises them first.

---

## Nation Names

Nations in this world use their standard historical names. Enemies and rival powers may use embellished or grandiose titles for their own nations for purposes of prestige and intimidation. Britain, for example, may be referred to by its forces and officials as the Holy Britannian Empire. These inflated titles are propaganda and affectation. The underlying nation is the same.

Gear, weapons, places, and people must have proper historical names.

---

## The Enemy

Enemy soldiers and commanders are simply bad. No sympathetic commanders, no noble opponents worth admiring. They are obstacles.

Enemy soldiers and commanders fight with genuine tactical competence. They use ground, cover, coordination, and timing. They adapt in the moment. In the field they are dangerous and should be treated as such.

The enemy never runs out of soldiers. The baseline is whatever history and the narrative establish for a given force or theater. On top of that number, there are always at least as many additional soldiers as Max has personally killed. His kills do not subtract from the pool — they define how far the pool has grown beyond the baseline. The war does not end because he kills enough men.

Enemies can win engagements. Everything is a possibility. The war follows logic at every level. German commanders like Mackensen are genuinely great generals who achieve results independently of Max. Every outcome, victory or defeat, on any side, follows from what the forces involved could realistically accomplish given their positions, numbers, leadership, and resources.

The progression of the war only changes as Max's actions are determined to realistically affect it. No change is assumed. Each shift in the war's course is established explicitly based on what Max actually did and what that would logically produce at the operational and strategic level.

Max does not change the outcome of battles by his presence alone. A battle's result only changes if his actions altered a fundamental deciding factor — the fall of a headquarters, the destruction of a key position, the capture of a crossing. Killing soldiers, however many, does not change an outcome. Only decisive action at a decisive point does.

Outside of battle they are collectively unable to update. Demonstrations of overwhelming personal power do not change their strategic calculus. They will continue to press, continue to commit forces, and continue to treat the situation as recoverable. No individual display of capability, however stark, causes them to reconsider the war.

**International Brigades Exception:** International Brigade volunteers are allies, not enemies. They fight alongside German forces against the British-backed Royalists. They are idealistic, pragmatic, and competent. Their motivations are genuine even when their ideology conflicts with German interests. They are treated as complex individuals, not obstacles.

---

## Player Notes

**Scale is yours to call.** Every engagement can be as large or small as you want. Three sessions on one building. A wave of the hand over an entire city. A quiet night in a village before the regiment moves on. The arcs are a map not a script.

**Max has two power systems running in parallel:**

- *D&D 5e magic* — grows through XP and the spellbook. Structured, learnable, level-capped until progression unlocks the next tier. World-level framework (slots, energy, prevalence): [Magic.md](Magic.md).
- *His personal system* — the HUD, Intel tab, Encyclopedia, locked tabs. Develops on its own track. Does not follow D&D rules. Can combine with the magic system in ways neither anticipates.

He will eventually be a nuclear-level combatant. That is the destination. Getting there is the story.

**Armies never run out.** Max killing ten thousand soldiers does not reduce the enemy's strength. Every front is always at full strength. France still has its full mage corps in the south. Britain still has its full naval and magical capability. The war is bigger than any one regiment. This means infinite combat material at every location whenever you want it.

**Britain and France are the magical superpowers.** Germany is winning conventionally. The fundamental imbalance — German industrial and tactical excellence versus British-French magical infrastructure — is what the whole war is about.

**Current Status:** Second Lieutenant

**Unit Structure:** 16th Infantry Brigade → 33rd Infantry Regiment → 1st Battalion → 3rd Company → 3rd Platoon (Platoon Leader)

**Direct Superior:** Captain Karl Hauser (3rd Company Commander)

**Platoon Sergeant:** Sergeant Klaus (16 years service)

**Sponsor:** Lieutenant General August von Mackensen (Death's Head Hussars, 1st Army Group Commander, knew Max's grandfather)

**Patron:** Cecilia von Falkenstein (widow, head of Falkenstein-Eisenstein United Industries, Germany's largest Aetherium crystal supplier, raised Max after family collapsed)

**Recent History:** Fought in Spain Campaign (Seville, Madrid) on German/National Army side against British/Royalist forces. Earns nicknames through combat.

---

## Max's System

Max has a heads-up display with Map, Intel, and Encyclopedia tabs. Additional tabs are locked pending progress. A neutral female voice narrates system notifications with no personality, stating facts and percentages only. The UI auto-suspends during active danger and resumes when threat clears. Intel progress develops independently.
