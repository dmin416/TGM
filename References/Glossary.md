## Locations — Spain

**Seville** — Southern Spain; opening campaign.

**Madrid** — Spanish capital; second campaign.

**Cuenca** — Spanish city.

**Valencia** — Spanish city.

**San Isidro** — Village outside Seville.

## Units and organizations

**33rd Infantry Regiment, 16th Brigade** — Max’s original unit.

**Death’s Head Hussars** — Mackensen’s historical regiment.

**Teutonic Knights** — German magical mecha order; Leonia von Fortis commands.

**Order of the Garter** — British magical mecha unit; white livery.

**Highland Mage Corps** — Britain’s premier battle mage force.

**Loire River Eye Mage Corps** — French elite mage corps.

**Northumberland Fusiliers, 4th Battalion** — British infantry; Seville campaign.

**International Brigades** — Left-wing volunteers.

**Black Hand / Unity or Death** — Serbian nationalist organization.

**3rd Bureau** — German Army intelligence section.

## Weapons — conventional

**Gewehr 98** — German bolt-action rifle; 7.92×57mm; 5 rounds.

**Luger P08** — German pistol; 9mm Parabellum; 8 rounds.

**MG08** — German heavy machine gun; 7.92×57mm; belt-fed; 250 rounds.

**MG15** — German light machine gun; 7.92×57mm; 100 rounds.

**MP14** — Submachine gun; designed by Helga; 9mm; 32 rounds.

**Stielhandgranate** — German stick grenade; 10–15m radius.

**FK 96 77mm** — German field gun.

**SMLE Mk III** — British bolt-action rifle; .303; 10 rounds.

**Vickers Gun** — British heavy machine gun; .303.

**Lewis Gun** — British light machine gun; .303; 47 or 97 round pan.

**Webley Revolver** — British service revolver; .455; 6 rounds.

**Hotchkiss M1914** — French light machine gun; 8mm Lebel.

**Stokes Mortar** — British 3-inch mortar.

**Mills Bomb** — British fragmentation grenade.

**Campo Giro** — Spanish pistol; 9mm Largo; 8 rounds.
