# Best Cartridges 1920 Onward

OTL reference. Post-1920 pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, and anti-materiel cartridge chronology with dimensions and roles. Use for Max omniscient knowledge, small arms planning, and weapons dialogue. Not story canon until cited. |---|---|---|---|---|---|
| .38 Special | 1920s standard | 9.07mm | 29mm | 9×29mmR | Standard police and civilian defense revolver, accurate and manageable |
| .357 Magnum | 1935 | 9.07mm | 33mm | 9×33mmR | High-velocity revolver, penetration and stopping power, law enforcement and hunting |
| .44 Magnum | 1955 | 10.9mm | 33mm | 10.9×33mmR | Heavy hunting and defense revolver, large game capable |

| .40 S&W | 1990 | 10.16mm | 22mm | 10×22mm | Law enforcement compromise between 9mm capacity and 10mm power, now largely obsolete |

| 5.7×28mm | 1990 | 5.7mm | 28mm | 5.7×28mm | Belgian PDW and pistol round, soft armor penetration, high velocity, low recoil, high capacity |
