# German Armored Airship Armament (L Class)

Reference for *Trenches, Guns and Magic*. **Fixed setting canon** for Imperial German **armored airships** (*Panzerluftschiff* / Zeppelin type with armored deck). This is **not** the August 1914 **Heer** divisional table ([German-Artillery-1914.md](German-Artillery-1914.md)). It is the **standard main battery** on the **L28 through L32** line, including **L30** in the Seville campaign.

**Related:** [Glossary.md](../Setting/Glossary.md) · [Military.md](Military.md) · [People.md](../Characters/People.md) (Reinhardt von Hoffmann, L30 commander)

## L Class Main Battery

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Class** | L28, L29, **L30**, L31, L32 (armored airship line) |
| **Mount** | Four **twin** turrets on the armored lower deck |
| **Tubes** | **Eight** barrels total |
| **Caliber** | **210 mm** (**21 cm**), Krupp pattern tube on airship mounting (same caliber family as the **21 cm Mörser** siege line, deck adapted) |
| **Role** | Street and column bombardment, fortress breach, point fire with magitech spotting rounds where story requires |
| **Secondary** | Deck MG08 turrets, bomb bays, loudspeakers (per scene) |

---

## Doctrine and Distinction

- **Heer divisional guns** stop at **77 mm** field guns and **10.5 cm** howitzers on the divisional table. Corps **150 mm**, foot **21 cm Mörser** siege pieces, and rare **42 cm** siege mortars are separate entries in [German-Artillery-1914.md](German-Artillery-1914.md).
- **210 mm on L30** is the **L class main battery**: eight barrels, four twin turrets, enabled by Luminite lift, armored deck, and magitech envelope shielding. **Always 210 mm in Story and References** for this class.
- Upstream **Source/** is not the edited manuscript. **Story/** and **References/** use **210 mm** only for L class batteries.
