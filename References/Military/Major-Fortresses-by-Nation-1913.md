# Major Fortresses by Nation (1913)

## Central Powers

### Germany
1. **Fortress Metz** (Lorraine): Premier border fortress system, most heavily fortified position in continental Europe. Garrison capacity: 25,000 troops. Location: Moselle River valley, 43km west of Saarbrücken.
2. **Fortress Königsberg** (East Prussia): Massive ring fortress protecting eastern approaches. Garrison capacity: 18,000 troops. Location: Pregel River, Baltic Sea coast.
3. **Fortress Strasbourg** (Alsace): Critical Rhine valley defensive anchor. Garrison capacity: 15,000 troops. Location: Rhine left bank, 4km west of Rhine bridge crossing.
4. **Fortress Thorn** (West Prussia): Vistula River crossing fortress, eastern defensive complex. Garrison capacity: 12,000 troops. Location: Vistula River bend, 180km northwest of Warsaw.
5. **Fortress Posen** (Poznań): Eastern territorial ring fortress, Polish border strongpoint. Garrison capacity: 14,000 troops. Location: Warta River valley, 280km east of Berlin.

### Austria-Hungary
1. **Fortress Przemyśl** (Galicia): Largest Habsburg fortress, third-largest in Europe by garrison capacity. Garrison capacity: 135,000 troops. Location: San River valley, 90km west of Lemberg.
2. **Fortress Cracow** (Kraków ring): Northern Galician defensive system, historic royal city fortifications. Garrison capacity: 45,000 troops. Location: Vistula River bend, Upper Silesian approaches.
3. **Fortress Lemberg** (Lwów/Lviv): Eastern Galician fortress complex. Garrison capacity: 30,000 troops. Location: Bug River headwaters, 70km from Russian frontier.
4. **Pola Naval Fortress** (Istria): Adriatic fleet base, coastal fortification system. Garrison capacity: 8,000 troops. Location: Istrian peninsula southern tip, protected harbor.
5. **Fortress Cattaro** (Kotor): Deep-water Adriatic fortress, Montenegrin border strongpoint. Garrison capacity: 6,000 troops. Location: Bocche di Cattaro fjord, southernmost Habsburg naval position.

### Spain
1. **Fortress Cádiz**: Atlantic naval base, historic harbor fortifications. Garrison capacity: 12,000 troops. Location: Andalusian coast peninsula, 100km south of Seville.
2. **Fortress Cartagena**: Mediterranean naval arsenal, coastal defense system. Garrison capacity: 10,000 troops. Location: Murcia coast, natural deep-water harbor.
3. **Fortress Ceuta** (North Africa): Strait of Gibraltar position, Moroccan enclave fortifications. Garrison capacity: 8,000 troops. Location: North African coast, 21km south of Gibraltar.
4. **San Sebastián Fortifications**: Basque coast defensive works. Garrison capacity: 5,000 troops. Location: Cantabrian coast, Bay of Biscay, French border approach.
5. **Barcelona Montjuïc**: Harbor-dominating citadel fortress. Garrison capacity: 7,000 troops. Location: Montjuïc hill, 175m elevation overlooking Barcelona harbor.

## Entente Powers

### Britain
1. **Gibraltar Fortress**: Mediterranean gateway, most strategically vital British position. Garrison capacity: 6,000 troops. Location: Iberian Peninsula southern tip, 14km strait width to Morocco.
2. **Portsmouth Naval Fortress**: Channel fleet base, extensive coastal batteries. Garrison capacity: 8,000 troops. Location: Hampshire coast, Solent approaches, 100km southwest of London.
3. **Dover Fortress**: Channel narrows defense, castle and modern battery complex. Garrison capacity: 5,000 troops. Location: Kent coast, 34km from Calais across narrowest Channel point.
4. **Plymouth Fortress**: Atlantic fleet base, Sound fortifications. Garrison capacity: 7,000 troops. Location: Devon coast, Plymouth Sound, 300km southwest of London.
5. **Malta Fortress Complex**: Mediterranean naval base, Grand Harbor fortifications. Garrison capacity: 12,000 troops. Location: Central Mediterranean, 93km south of Sicily, 288km north of Libya.

### France
1. **Fortress Verdun**: Meuse valley barrier, most famous French fortress system. Garrison capacity: 66,000 troops. Location: Meuse River, 230km east of Paris, 60km from German frontier.
2. **Fortress Belfort**: Burgundian Gate strongpoint, never captured in 1870. Garrison capacity: 35,000 troops. Location: Gap between Vosges and Jura mountains, 140km from Basel.
3. **Fortress Toul**: Moselle valley fortress, eastern border defense. Garrison capacity: 42,000 troops. Location: Moselle River, 20km west of Nancy, 280km east of Paris.
4. **Fortress Épinal**: Vosges approaches fortress. Garrison capacity: 38,000 troops. Location: Moselle River upper valley, 70km south of Nancy, Vosges foothills.
5. **Fortress Reims** (modernized medieval works): Champagne defensive position. Garrison capacity: 18,000 troops. Location: Vesle River, 130km northeast of Paris, Champagne plain.

### Russia (Tsarist fortresses, both factions inherit these)
1. **Fortress Brest-Litovsk**: Bug River fortress, western frontier anchor. Garrison capacity: 50,000 troops. Location: Bug-Mukhavets river confluence, 190km east of Warsaw.
2. **Fortress Warsaw** (ring fortifications): Vistula fortress complex, Polish administrative center. Garrison capacity: 90,000 troops. Location: Vistula River middle course, Kingdom of Poland capital.
3. **Fortress Ivangorod**: Vistula crossing fortress, Warsaw supporting position. Garrison capacity: 35,000 troops. Location: Vistula left bank, 120km south of Warsaw.
4. **Fortress Kovno** (Kaunas): Niemen River fortress, Lithuanian barrier. Garrison capacity: 40,000 troops. Location: Niemen-Viliya confluence, 100km northwest of Vilnius.
5. **Fortress Vladivostok**: Pacific strongpoint replacing lost Port Arthur. Garrison capacity: 25,000 troops. Location: Golden Horn Bay, Sea of Japan coast, terminus of Trans-Siberian Railway.

### Belgium
1. **Fortress Liège**: Meuse gateway, ring of twelve modern forts. Garrison capacity: 40,000 troops. Location: Meuse River valley, 25km from German frontier, 95km east of Brussels.
2. **Fortress Namur**: Meuse-Sambre confluence fortress. Garrison capacity: 35,000 troops. Location: River confluence, 60km southeast of Brussels, French frontier approach.
3. **Fortress Antwerp**: National reduit, largest Belgian fortified position. Garrison capacity: 100,000 troops. Location: Scheldt River estuary, 45km from North Sea, 50km north of Brussels.
4. **Fortress Huy**: Meuse valley supporting fortress. Garrison capacity: 8,000 troops. Location: Meuse River, 30km upstream from Liège.
5. **Fortress Mons** (field fortifications): Hainaut border defensive works. Garrison capacity: 12,000 troops. Location: Franco-Belgian border, 70km southwest of Brussels.

### Serbia
1. **Belgrade Fortress** (Kalemegdan): Danube-Sava confluence, historic Turkish fortifications modernized. Garrison capacity: 15,000 troops. Location: River confluence, Šumadija heights commanding both rivers.
2. **Niš Fortress**: Morava valley position. Garrison capacity: 8,000 troops. Location: South Morava River, 240km southeast of Belgrade, Bulgarian frontier approach.
3. **Užice Fortress**: Western Serbian mountain strongpoint. Garrison capacity: 5,000 troops. Location: Dinaric Alps, 180km south of Belgrade, Bosnian border region.
4. **Smederevo Fortress**: Danube fortress below Belgrade. Garrison capacity: 6,000 troops. Location: Danube right bank, 45km downstream from Belgrade.
5. **Pirot Fortress**: Bulgarian border position. Garrison capacity: 7,000 troops. Location: Nišava River valley, 75km east of Niš, frontier strongpoint.
