# Strategic Chokepoints and Railways by Nation (1913)

## Central Powers

### Germany
**Natural Chokepoints:**
1. Kiel Canal (Nord-Ostsee-Kanal): connects Baltic Sea to North Sea, critical naval passage
2. Rhine River gorge through Loreley valley: narrow passage for river traffic and rail
3. Elbe River at Königstein fortress region: controls Bohemian approaches
4. Vistula River crossings in West Prussia: gateway to Eastern territories
5. Black Forest passes (Höllental, Kinzig valley): control Baden-Württemberg mountain transit
6. Oder River crossings at Frankfurt/Küstrin: eastern defensive line
7. Brenner Pass approach from Bavaria: Alpine southern route

**Major Railways (1913):**
1. Berlin-Hamburg main line: primary northern artery
2. Rhine Valley Railway (Mannheim-Basel): industrial heartland connection
3. Berlin-Breslau-Warsaw corridor: eastern expansion route
4. Munich-Salzburg-Vienna connection: southern imperial link
5. Cologne-Frankfurt-Munich trunk line: north-south spine
6. Berlin-Königsberg East Prussia line: Baltic provincial connection
7. Saarbrücken-Metz fortified railway: western military logistics

4. Transylvania connection (Budapest-Brașov-Bucharest): Romanian border
5. Arlberg Railway (Innsbruck-Bludenz): western mountain crossing
6. Bosnian Railway (Sarajevo-Metkovic): Balkan annexation line
7. Prague-Vienna main line: Bohemian integration route

### Spain
