## Tier 8: Ocean Plants and Marine Coverage

### Marine Plant Types

- **Phytoplankton:** ~10²⁶ cells, cover all ocean surface to ~200m depth. ~10⁸ cells per liter of surface seawater. Produce ~50% of Earth's oxygen.
- **Kelp / Macroalgae:** up to 60m tall, dense forests along ~25% of world's coastlines. ~1.5 billion tonnes global biomass.
- **Seagrass:** ~300,000 km² of shallow seafloor, dense rhizome root mats, highly pressure and chemical sensitive.

### What Marine Plants Sense

- Temperature, salinity, pressure, current direction
- Chemical gradients: pollution, blood, dissolved minerals, pH, fuel traces
- Pressure waves: submarine wakes, underwater explosions, sonar pings, tectonic events
- Mechanical disturbance: divers, anchors, anything brushing kelp fronds
- Bioluminescent cascade: ocean already lights up when disturbed, acts as passive alarm system

### Network Architecture (Ocean vs Land)

- Land: fixed mycelium wire network
- Ocean: **dynamic fluid mesh**, signal routes through whichever phytoplankton cells are currently adjacent
- Ocean cross basin latency: **~500ms–2 seconds** (vs 42ms cross land) due to dynamic routing overhead
- Still faster than any human communication system for ocean events

### Strategic Additions

- **Every submarine visible:** fuel traces, pressure wake, thermal signature all detectable. Submarines lose their only strategic advantage.
- **Every ship tracked** from port departure. Chemical/pressure wake persists hours.
- **Undersea cables and pipelines:** location, condition, tampering all monitored
- **Global weather influence:** phytoplankton DMS emissions seed clouds. Mass coordination extends weather control from continental to **genuinely global**, including ocean storm systems.
- **Deep ocean indirect awareness:** pressure waves and chemical gradients propagate upward from below photic zone. Volcanic activity, tectonic movement, deep operations partially visible.

### Revised Node Count with Ocean

## Hard Limits

- **Deep ocean trenches:** below photic zone, indirect sensing only
- **Antarctic ice sheet interior:** minimal coverage
- **Bare rock / pavement:** patchy urban coverage, blind spots in city cores
- **Attention bandwidth:** 10²⁵ FLOP/s of planetary compute feeding one biological brain. The system sees everything. The druid can only consciously direct ~50 focal points at once. Filtering and prioritization is critical.
- **Node reset speed:** even magic enhanced, plant nodes fire at ~100–1,000 Hz. Not silicon.
- **Ocean latency:** 500ms–2s cross basin vs 42ms cross land due to dynamic fluid mesh routing.
