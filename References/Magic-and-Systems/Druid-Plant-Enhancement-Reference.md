# Druid Plant Enhancement Reference

Rough draft. Material enhancement rules for druid spells acting on living plant matter. Use for *Druidcraft*, *Plant Manipulation*, weapon grips, staffs, and improvised plant arms. Not story canon until cited. Distinct from engineered [Lilyvitae.md](../Biology-and-Chemistry/Lilyvitae.md) (fixed bio-graphene properties) and from cantrip **combat** scaling in [Magic.md](Magic.md) (100 J × system Level, 30 ft reach).

## The Core Idea

A druid spell achieves a fixed material enhancement multiplier at each level. That multiplier is applied to whatever plant material is used. The plant's starting properties determine the final result.

## How to Apply to Any Plant

1. Find the plant's tensile strength, compressive strength, hardness, and toughness
2. Multiply each property by the level enhancement number
3. The result is what the plant becomes after the spell acts on it

---

## Example: Blackthorn

Blackthorn stem starts at roughly 90 megapascals tensile strength and Mohs hardness 3.5. One of the traditional European weapon woods due to its natural density.

| Level | Tensile Result | Hardness Result | Equivalent Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (3×) | 270 MPa | Mohs 3 | Soft structural aluminum |
| 5 (15×) | 1350 MPa | Mohs 6 | High grade tool steel |
| 10 (30×) | 2700 MPa | Mohs 7.5 | Hardened tool steel |
| 17 (51×) | 4590 MPa | Mohs 8.5 | Approaches tungsten carbide |
| 20 (60×) | 5400 MPa | Mohs 9 | Corundum territory |

---

## Example: Hornbeam

Hornbeam starts at roughly 110 megapascals tensile strength and Mohs hardness 4. The hardest common European wood and historically used for mallets and tool handles.

| Level | Tensile Result | Hardness Result | Equivalent Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (3×) | 330 MPa | Mohs 3.5 | Functional weapon steel |
| 5 (15×) | 1650 MPa | Mohs 6.5 | High grade tool steel |
| 10 (30×) | 3300 MPa | Mohs 8 | Approaches tungsten carbide |
| 17 (51×) | 5610 MPa | Mohs 9 | Corundum territory |
| 20 (60×) | 6600 MPa | Mohs 9.5 | Near diamond hardness |

---

## Key Rule

The spell achieves the same enhancement regardless of plant. Better starting material produces a better final weapon. Hornbeam reaches functional steel at level one due to its exceptional natural density. Blackthorn reaches steel equivalence by level two.
