## Notes

Tesla's alternating current system is arguably the single most consequential European invention outside Germany and Britain. Every electrical grid on earth runs on his polyphase AC principle. Westinghouse commercialized it in America but the physics and engineering were entirely Tesla's Serbian mind working in New York. The compensation paid to him was negligible relative to the value generated.

Mendel's genetics work sat ignored for thirty five years after publication. When rediscovered in 1900 it immediately transformed biology, medicine and agriculture. The delay cost humanity an entire generation of potential medical progress.
