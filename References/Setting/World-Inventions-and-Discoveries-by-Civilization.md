# World Inventions and Discoveries by Civilization

OTL chronology of major inventions and discoveries grouped by civilization or nation. Use for Max's omniscient knowledge context, dialogue color, and patent pacing against [Alternate-History-Baseline.md](../Planning/Alternate-History-Baseline.md). Approximate dates; attributions follow conventional historical credit.

## Mesopotamia and Ancient Near East

- Agriculture and crop domestication: approximately 10000 BCE
- Wheel: approximately 3500 BCE
- Writing, cuneiform: approximately 3200 BCE
- Calendar: approximately 3000 BCE
- Beer brewing: approximately 3400 BCE
- Bronze metallurgy: approximately 3000 BCE
- Irrigation canal systems: approximately 3000 BCE

- Papyrus writing material: approximately 3000 BCE
- 365 day solar calendar: approximately 4000 BCE
- Large scale stone construction techniques: approximately 2600 BCE
- Hieroglyphic writing: approximately 3200 BCE

## Ancient India

- Indus Valley flush toilet and sewage systems: approximately 2500 BCE
