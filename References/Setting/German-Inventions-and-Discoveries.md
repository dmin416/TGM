## Medicine and Pharmaceuticals

- Morphine isolation: Friedrich Sertürner, 1804
- Cellular pathology: Rudolf Virchow, 1858
- Germ theory development: Robert Koch, 1876 onward
- Tuberculosis bacillus identification: Robert Koch, 1882
- Cholera bacillus identification: Robert Koch, 1883
- Koch's postulates: Robert Koch, 1884
- Diphtheria antitoxin: Emil von Behring, 1890
- Immunology foundations: Paul Ehrlich, 1890s
- Aspirin: Felix Hoffmann, Bayer, 1897
- Heroin: Heinrich Dreser, Bayer, 1898
- Novocain: Alfred Einhorn, 1905
- Salvarsan, first chemotherapy: Paul Ehrlich, 1909
- Phenobarbital: Bayer, 1912
- Prontosil, first true antibiotic: Gerhard Domagk, 1932
- Methadone: 1937

## Physics

- Ohm's Law: Georg Ohm, 1827
- Kirchhoff's Laws: Gustav Kirchhoff, 1845
- Radio waves proven: Heinrich Hertz, 1887
- X rays: Wilhelm Röntgen, 1895
- Quantum theory: Max Planck, 1900
- Photoelectric effect: Albert Einstein, 1905
- Special relativity: Albert Einstein, 1905
- General relativity: Albert Einstein, 1915
- Matrix mechanics: Heisenberg, Born and Jordan, 1925
- Uncertainty principle: Werner Heisenberg, 1927
- Nuclear fission: Otto Hahn and Fritz Strassmann, 1938
