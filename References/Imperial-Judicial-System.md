# Imperial Judicial System

Rough draft. What Max wants for Germany and will propose to the Emperor: a utopian covenant empire framework. Planning reference; not story canon until cited in `Story/` or `Status.md`. See [Alternate-History-Baseline.md](Alternate-History-Baseline.md), [Notes-for-the-Future.md](Notes-for-the-Future.md), [Market-Crash-Wealth-Reference.md](Market-Crash-Wealth-Reference.md).

## A Utopian Framework for a Covenant Empire

---

## The People

The citizens of this empire are, on the whole, genuinely good. Most try to live by the fruits of the spirit (love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control) because of their faith, not because of the culture they were raised in. Many are faithful to Jesus. Some are not. The degree to which a believer lives out the fruits of the spirit varies: faith is not perfection and no person is without failure. But a person of faith, however imperfect, is fundamentally different from a person of no faith and no conscience. The former has an internal compass that corrects them even when the court cannot. The latter has only self interest. The law holds both equally accountable for harm caused. The Sense Good and Evil spell reads the difference between them and the court responds accordingly. Creed is not on trial. Character is.

The law is not a cage for a corrupt people. It is a covenant that protects a good people from harm they didn't choose.

The empire's foundational goal is simple: every person under its care is provided for, and every person under its care hopes to contribute. These two things are not in tension; they are the same thing expressed from different directions. The empire provides work, housing, food, and a path upward because a people that is secure naturally wants to build. A person living by the fruits of the spirit does not need to be coerced into labor; they work because contributing to their community is an expression of who they are. Those who cannot contribute due to illness, injury, age, or circumstance are carried by the community without shame, because a people with genuine kindness and goodness does not abandon its own. Those who will not contribute when they genuinely could are the rare exception, and the judicial system handles them, not the community's basic generosity.

---

## The Emperor's Authority

The emperor is a man. Not a saint, not a symbol, not a divine figure: just the person at the top of the structure, doing the hardest job in the empire. He will have flaws. He will make mistakes. He is held to the covenant the same as anyone else, and the covenant does not grade on a curve for rank. His authority is legitimate as long as the system he oversees is just and the people under it are cared for. He does not need to be the best man in the empire. He needs to be a good one who keeps his word and doesn't confuse the throne with personal entitlement.

---

## The Foundational Distinction

Every case that comes before the court resolves around one question that the Sense Good and Evil spell answers immediately and infallibly: **who is this person inside?**

**The person of faith and conscience:** failed, knows it, already correcting. Guilt in a good person is already doing the court's reformative work before the trial begins. The court's job is to address the harm to the victim and the community and the root cause of the failure. Punishment layered on top of genuine remorse is redundant at best and crushing at worst. The court gets out of the way of a conscience that is already working and supports restoration instead.

**The person of pure self interest:** no faith, no conscience, no moral framework outside themselves. Self interest is their only compass. Remorse that appears at trial is performance, not reality, and the spell reads the difference instantly. This person has no internal corrective mechanism. The court must be that mechanism entirely because nothing else is. Full weight of the sentence applies. No assumption of internal reformation is made because none is happening.

The same act receives genuinely different treatment based on what is actually inside the person, not their rank, their wealth, their connections, or how well they present at trial. A powerful man with no conscience gets the full weight of the court. A poor man genuinely broken by what he did gets the court's support in making it right. This is not softness. It is precision.

---

## Core Judicial Principle

**Physical harm heals. Harm to the mind, will, or soul is a violation of a person's fundamental humanity and is weighted accordingly.**

A bruise from a deliberate bike collision is assault. Rape causing the same physical injury is lifetime hard labor with no rights, because the physical injury is incidental. The real crime is the destruction of another person's autonomy, safety, and inner world. The judicial system recognizes this without exception.

---

## Magical Truth Detection

All cases brought before the court are subject to the Sense Good and Evil spell administered by a state licensed mage. This is standard procedure, not an exceptional measure. The spell determines the intent behind the act (malicious, careless, or genuinely accidental) and whether genuine remorse and conscience are present. These two determinations together inform both the severity tier assigned and how the sentence is structured.

A man who caused a death through pure accident with a conscience that is already broken by it is treated entirely differently from a man who caused the same death through deliberate malice and feels nothing. The spell removes the court's need to guess at either.

Lying to the court about intent after the spell has been administered is a standalone Crimes Against Truth charge added to the original sentence. The spell does not force confession: a person may still say what they wish, but saying something the spell has already contradicted is fraud against the court and is treated as such. In a people shaped by the fruits of the spirit, most simply tell the truth.

## Severity Scale

| Tier | Name | Character | Punishment Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Minor | Negligent, accidental, or petty | Restitution, community service, public record |
| S2 | Moderate | Deliberate, harmful, no lasting damage | Labor sentence, restitution, standing demotion |
| S3 | Serious | Deliberate, lasting harm or significant loss | Long imprisonment with hard labor, full restitution |
| S4 | Severe | Grievous, life altering, or involving torture | Extended imprisonment, possible lifetime labor |
| S5 | Capital | Murder, mass harm, or crimes of total depravity | Lifetime hard labor with no rights |

Crimes Against the Weak escalate one full tier automatically. No exceptions.

## Crime Categories & Severity

---

### 1. Crimes Against the Body

Physical harm where the primary violation is bodily. Psychological violation is handled separately and compounds the charge.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Accidental harm, negligence causing injury | Restitution, community service |
| S2 | Deliberate assault, intentional injury without lasting damage | Labor sentence, restitution, public record |
| S3 | Grievous bodily harm, maiming, disfigurement | Long imprisonment, full restitution, permanent record |
| S4 | Torture: deliberate infliction of prolonged suffering | Extended imprisonment, no path to standing restoration |
| S5 | Murder: deliberate, premeditated killing | Lifetime hard labor with no rights |

---

### Consensual Combat

Fighting is not inherently criminal. The empire recognizes that people settle matters with their fists, that honor cultures exist, and that two willing men resolving a dispute physically, even to the point of grave injury, is not the court's business provided magical healing exists to address the consequences. This is not endorsed as the Christian ideal but it is recognized as a human reality and treated accordingly. Unlike the historical Wilhelmine dueling code, this is not gated by rank or standing: the same rules apply from laborer to crown prince.

**Combat is lawful when:**

- Both parties willingly entered the fight with full understanding of the risk
- No grudge is carried afterward: the matter is considered settled by both parties
- No third party is harmed
- Magical healing is available and not being withheld

**Combat becomes a crime the moment:**

- One party attempts to withdraw and the other continues: assault begins at that exact moment
- A weapon is introduced without prior mutual agreement
- Consent was engineered through coercion or manipulation: false consent is no consent
- A crowd is incited to join against one person
- Death results and the spell confirms one party intended to kill rather than resolve: that is murder prosecuted as Crimes Against Life S5

Two men who fight, settle it, and go home as something closer to equals than when they started have used a system the court recognizes as legitimate. The court's interest begins precisely where consent ends.

### 2. Crimes Against Life: Negligent & Unintended

Killing without deliberate intent. Distinct from murder in character but still permanently consequential to the victim.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Accidental death through minor negligence | Restitution to family, community service, public record |
| S2 | Negligent death: careless professional, unsafe employer | Restitution to family, labor sentence |
| S3 | Gross negligence: reckless disregard for obvious risk of death | Long imprisonment, full restitution, permanent record |
| S4 | Manslaughter: heat of passion, no premeditation but clear culpability | Extended imprisonment with hard labor, full restitution |
| S5 | Mass negligent death: systemic failure causing multiple deaths | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full asset seizure, permanent erasure from professional record |

---

### 3. Crimes Against the Mind & Soul

Any deliberate act violating another person's autonomy, identity, or inner safety. Weighted more heavily than equivalent physical crimes because the damage does not heal predictably and was inflicted against the most fundamental part of a person.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Minor manipulation, petty coercion, social pressure weaponized for gain | Restitution, public record, mandatory reformation program |
| S2 | Blackmail, sustained coercion, deliberate psychological harm | Labor sentence, restitution, supervised probation |
| S3 | Grooming, sustained psychological abuse, deliberate identity destruction | Long imprisonment with hard labor |
| S4 | Rape, enslavement of will, sustained torture of the mind | Extended imprisonment; no mitigation for lesser physical injury |
| S5 | Systematic rape, mass psychological destruction, organized enslavement of persons | Lifetime hard labor with no rights; no path to standing, no appeal on severity |

---

### 4. Crimes Against Truth

Deception that harms others or corrupts the community's ability to function and trust one another.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Petty fraud, minor deception for small gain | Doubled restitution, public record |
| S2 | Defamation, false testimony causing harm to an individual | Labor sentence, full restitution, standing demotion |
| S3 | Major fraud, sustained deception causing significant material harm | Long imprisonment, full restitution, public record |
| S4 | Corruption of officials, false accusation of a capital crime | Equivalent punishment to what the false accusation would have brought, asset seizure |
| S5 | Systematic empire scale fraud or deception causing mass harm | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full asset seizure, erasure from public record |

---

### 5. Crimes Against Community & Property

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Petty theft, minor vandalism | Full restitution plus labor equivalent |
| S2 | Significant theft, deliberate property destruction | Labor sentence, full restitution, public record |
| S3 | Arson, organized theft, sabotage of communal resources | Long imprisonment, full restitution, standing demotion |
| S4 | Deliberate destruction of critical infrastructure | Extended imprisonment, full restitution, permanent record |
| S5 | Destruction causing mass casualties or community collapse | Lifetime hard labor with no rights |

---

### 6. Crimes Against Animals

Killing an unprotected animal for food, pest control, or legitimate purpose is not a crime. The empire does not equate animals with people. Deliberate cruelty is a different matter: in a fruits of the spirit culture, a person who tortures animals is demonstrating an absence of goodness that puts people at risk.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Negligent cruelty, failure of duty of care to a working animal | Restitution, public record |
| S2 | Deliberate cruelty causing suffering | Labor sentence, barred from owning or working with animals |
| S3 | Torture of animals: sustained, deliberate infliction of suffering | Extended labor sentence, full psychological assessment, permanent monitoring |

Protected animals (designated as ecologically or strategically vital) fall under Crimes Against Community & Property if killed without state authorization.

---

### 7. Crimes of Trafficking & Slavery

Buying, selling, transporting, or holding a person against their will. Among the most serious crimes the empire recognizes because it treats a person as property and denies them everything the covenant promises.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S2 | Knowingly benefiting from trafficked labor without direct participation | Full restitution, labor sentence, public record |
| S3 | Active participation in trafficking, unlawful detention of a person | Long imprisonment, full asset seizure |
| S4 | Operating a trafficking network, holding multiple persons | Extended imprisonment, full asset seizure |
| S5 | Leading or founding a large scale trafficking or slavery operation | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full asset seizure, erasure from public record |

---

### 8. Crimes of Organized Predation

Extortion, racketeering, and sustained systems of coercion designed to feed off the community permanently.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Single act of petty extortion | Restitution, labor sentence |
| S2 | Sustained extortion of an individual | Labor sentence, restitution, public record |
| S3 | Small organized criminal operation preying on community | Long imprisonment, full asset seizure |
| S4 | Significant criminal network with multiple victims | Extended imprisonment, full asset seizure, network dismantled |
| S5 | Empire scale racketeering or criminal empire | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full dismantlement, leaders erased from public record |

---

### 9. Crimes Against the Imperial Covenant

Abuse of authority, corruption of office, treason, or deliberate undermining of the compact between ruler and people. This category applies to officials and to the emperor himself: the covenant is not graded by rank.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Minor negligence of duty, small abuse of office | Demotion, public correction, restitution |
| S2 | Bribery, favoritism, misuse of state resources | Labor sentence, demotion, restitution |

| S4 | Large scale corruption, deliberate subversion of justice | Extended imprisonment, full asset seizure, erasure from honor rolls |
| S5 | Treason, empire scale betrayal, deliberate destruction of the covenant | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full erasure from public record |

### 10. Crimes Against Decency

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Public indecency, lewd conduct | Public correction, community service |
| S2 | Distribution of obscene material to adults without consent | Labor sentence, restricted movement, public record |
| S3 | Deliberate exposure of children to corrupting content | Long imprisonment; escalated automatically under Crimes Against the Weak |
| S4 | Systematic production targeting children | Escalated to S5 under Crimes Against the Weak |

Desertion of a dependent without provision or transfer of care.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|

| S2 | Deliberate abandonment causing distress but no lasting harm | Labor sentence, loss of custody or guardianship rights |
| S3 | Deliberate abandonment causing lasting harm | Long imprisonment, permanent loss of guardianship rights |
| S4 | Abandonment resulting in serious injury or near death | Extended imprisonment, treated as grievous bodily harm by neglect |

Deliberate violation of graves, holy sites, war memorials, or the honored dead.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|

| S2 | Deliberate vandalism of a grave or memorial | Labor sentence, full restoration at offender's cost, public record |
| S3 | Targeted desecration of a community's holy site | Long imprisonment, full restoration, public record |
| S4 | Systematic desecration as hatred or political violence | Extended imprisonment, treated as Crimes Against the Covenant |

### 13. Crimes Against the Weak: Escalation Rule

This is not a standalone category. It is a modifier. Any crime whose victim is a child, the elderly, the mentally diminished, or a person in total dependency automatically escalates **one full severity tier.** A S4 crime against a child becomes S5. There is no ceiling exemption and no mitigating circumstance that removes this escalation.

---

### 14. Self Defense & Rules of Engagement

The empire recognizes the right of any person to defend themselves, their family, and those in their immediate care from harm. A person who acts in genuine self defense has committed no crime. The Sense Good and Evil spell confirms intent and the court does not punish a person for surviving.

**Self defense is lawful when:**

- The threat was real and immediate
- The response was proportionate to the threat
- The person had no reasonable opportunity to withdraw or de escalate first
- The defense stopped when the threat stopped: continuing to harm a subdued or fleeing person is assault

**The preferred response is always de escalation.** A person who talked someone down, withdrew, or found another resolution without violence has acted in the spirit of the fruits of the spirit and is commended by the community for it. The court recognizes restraint as a virtue. A man who could have fought and chose not to has done something harder and better.

**Self defense becomes a crime when:**

- The threat was not real or was manufactured to justify violence
- The response was grossly disproportionate
- The person pursued a fleeing attacker and continued to harm them
- The spell reveals the defense was used as a pretext for intended violence

---

### 15. Military Rules of Engagement

Soldiers operate under the covenant even in a riot or combat situation. Violence is a last resort, not a first response.

**Violence is lawful for soldiers when:**

- A commanding officer has assessed the situation and issued a direct order
- The crowd or enemy has produced weapons and is actively using them
- Life of soldiers or civilians is in immediate and genuine danger with no alternative
- All verbal dispersal and de escalation attempts have failed

**Violence is unlawful even under orders when:**

- The crowd is unarmed and fleeing
- A person is already subdued and no longer a threat
- Violence is directed at bystanders not participating in the threat
- A commanding officer orders atrocity: an unlawful order is not a lawful order and following it is not a defense at trial

A soldier on the ground who must make a call without an officer present is assessed by the spell at trial. Genuine fear for life is treated with consideration. Chaos used as cover for violence is not.

---

### 16. Sedition & Incitement

Distinct from treason in that the offender may not act directly but organizes, inflames, or weaponizes others toward violence or rebellion. Free grievance is not a crime: a citizen may criticize the emperor and the law through legitimate means. The line is crossed when speech becomes a tool for organizing harm.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Spreading deliberate falsehoods that cause public unrest | Restitution, public correction, public record |
| S2 | Organized agitation inciting civil disorder without violence | Labor sentence, restricted movement, public record |
| S3 | Inciting violence against individuals or groups | Long imprisonment, full asset seizure |
| S4 | Organizing armed insurrection or rebellion | Extended imprisonment, full asset seizure |
| S5 | Leading a rebellion causing mass death or collapse of civil order | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, erasure from public record |

A citizen who voices genuine grievance through legitimate channels (petition, community assembly, direct appeal to the emperor) is explicitly protected. The law distinguishes between a man who argues and a man who arms.

Non citizens do not hold this right. A foreign national, visitor, or non citizen resident who publicly criticizes or attacks the emperor's character or authority is making a hostile act against the empire itself. This is prosecuted under Crimes Against the Imperial Covenant at S4 minimum. The right to assess the emperor belongs to the people of the covenant. It is earned by membership in it, not assumed by presence within its borders. It is worth noting that such an act would be extraordinarily rare in a well governed empire where the emperor visibly upholds the covenant.

---

### 17. War Crimes & Conduct in Conquest

The empire expands. Imperial soldiers operate under the covenant even on foreign soil. The uniform does not suspend the fruits of the spirit: it represents them. Conduct that would be criminal at home is criminal abroad. A conquered people is not an animal. Soldiers who behave otherwise shame the empire and violate the covenant.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S2 | Looting private property beyond authorized seizure | Restitution, labor sentence, demotion in rank |
| S3 | Deliberate destruction of civilian property, desecration of foreign holy sites | Long imprisonment, permanent discharge, public record |
| S4 | Torture of prisoners of war, summary execution of surrendered combatants, rape in conquest | Extended imprisonment; no mitigation for context of war |
| S5 | Massacre of civilians, systematic atrocity, organized use of rape as a weapon of war | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full erasure from imperial record; commanding officers share culpability for acts carried out under their authority and knowledge |

Prisoners of war are entitled to food, shelter, and freedom from torture. They are not citizens of the empire and do not hold merit standing, but they are human beings and the covenant's values do not stop at the border.

---

### 18. Magic Crimes

Magic exists and is rare. That rarity makes its misuse particularly serious: the victim typically has no defense and no frame of reference for what was done to them. Magical violation is treated as categorically worse than equivalent non magical harm because it bypasses all natural ability to resist or escape.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Unauthorized use of minor magic causing no harm | Fine, public record, magic use restricted pending review |
| S2 | Using magic to deceive or manipulate without physical harm | Labor sentence, magic use suspended, restitution |
| S3 | Enchanting a person without consent: altering their emotions, will, or perception | Long imprisonment, permanent magic license revocation |
| S4 | Magical coercion: forcing actions against a person's will, magical torture, using healing as extortion | Extended imprisonment, permanent magic license revocation |
| S5 | Magical assassination, mass magical harm, enslaving a person's mind entirely | Lifetime hard labor with no rights; no path to standing restoration, no appeal on severity |

Withholding healing that only you can provide in order to extort compliance is an S4 magic crime regardless of whether physical harm results. The coercion is the crime.

State licensed healers and mages operate under the Imperial Covenant directly. Abuse of a magical office escalates one full tier under Crimes Against the Imperial Covenant in addition to the magic crime charge.

## Financial Crimes

Selling below true cost of production (materials, labor, overhead, and a fair margin) with intent to bankrupt competitors, corrupt a market, or undermine lawful trade is a financial crime under the imperial covenant. Charity pricing, authorized wartime rationing, and state disaster relief are not crimes when declared and recorded by proper authority.

| Severity | Description | Punishment |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Isolated below cost sales without lasting market harm | Full restitution to harmed sellers, public correction, doubled make good to affected trade guild or municipality |
| S2 | Pattern of predatory pricing in one sector or region | Asset seizure, labor sentence, loss of trade license, standing demotion |
| S3 | Empire wide or cartel coordinated dump pricing | Equivalent to S3 Crimes Against Truth: full restitution, long imprisonment |
| S4 | Sustained cartel predation causing regional economic collapse | Extended imprisonment, full asset seizure |
| S5 | Empire scale financial predation causing mass harm | Lifetime hard labor with no rights, full asset seizure, erasure from public record |

## Punishment Philosophy

**S1: Restore.**

The offender repairs what they broke, materially and socially. No imprisonment. The goal is wholeness, not humiliation.

**S2: Correct.**

The offender is held accountable with a labor sentence and restitution. A path back to full standing exists and is expected to be taken.

**S3: Remove and Reform.**

The offender is separated from the community, put to productive hard labor, and given a genuine path back, contingent on demonstrated change, not time served alone.

**S4: Severe Restriction.**

The offender has committed a grievous act. Return to normal standing is unlikely and not guaranteed. The Sense Good and Evil spell is the determining factor: a person of genuine conscience showing deep remorse receives a structured path through extended imprisonment with hard labor and a defined review period. A person showing no remorse and no conscience receives extended imprisonment with no path to standing restoration and is treated as a candidate for S5 on any subsequent offense.

**S5: Permanent Consequence.**

Lifetime hard labor with no rights. The person contributes labor to the community they violated for the remainder of their life. There is no path to standing restoration.

The empire does not execute. Death is God's jurisdiction, not the emperor's. A good person killed is sent home to God: that is no punishment. A person of pure self interest killed escapes into God's judgment before they have faced any earthly consequence. Lifetime hard labor with no rights forces a person to live inside what they chose, every day, for the rest of their life. That is a heavier burden than death and the empire knows it.

## The First Strike Principle

The empire does not assume the worst of anyone on a first offense. Even serious crimes carry the possibility of reformation where the spell shows genuine conscience and remorse. The door is not closed on a first offense unless the act itself was so systematically depraved (mass harm, organized atrocity, predatory networks) that the scale removes the question.

A person of faith and conscience who has committed a serious crime and is genuinely broken by it is already doing the hardest reformative work possible. The court addresses the victim's needs, the root cause, and any structural correction needed, then supports that person's restoration rather than crushing a conscience that is already working.

A person of pure self interest who has committed even a moderate crime and shows no remorse is treated with the full weight available at that severity because their conscience will not correct them when the court is done. The sentence has to do the work their character will not.

---

## Substance and Professional Consequences

Where a crime was committed under the influence of any substance, the personal ban on that substance comes first before any other professional consequence. The substance is the proximate cause and is addressed directly.

A professional who caused harm under the influence does not automatically lose their profession on a first offense if the spell shows genuine remorse and the character to do better. A genuinely repentant person punishes themselves harder than any court could. The medic who killed a patient through carelessness while drunk, who is broken by what he did and dedicates the rest of his career to being better, will become a finer healer than most, driven by a guilt no court sentence could manufacture. The court assigns the substance ban, the restitution, and then steps aside and lets that conscience work.

Professional bans are reserved for willful or predatory misuse of a professional role, or for repeated failure showing no capacity for correction.

---

## Restitution & Repentance

Restitution is not only financial. True repentance means the offender actively repairs what they broke in whatever way is within their power. A doctor who killed a patient through negligence does not simply pay a fine: he studies harder, improves his practice, and takes care of the family of the person he lost if they have need. A man who burned a neighbour's barn rebuilds it with his own hands where possible. The court assigns the material restitution. What a person of genuine conscience does beyond that is between them, the people they harmed, and God. The empire does not mandate the heart, but it recognizes when one is present, and so does the community.

Imprisonment never generates debt. A person serving a sentence owes nothing financial beyond restitution already assigned at trial. All imprisoned persons work. For crimes that were accidental or purely material in nature the labor is productive but not punishing: a trade, a craft, something that builds a skill. For crimes with a moral dimension the labor is hard. Not cruel, but hard. The work is the consequence. The debt to the community is paid in it.

In a people genuinely shaped by the fruits of the spirit and faith in Jesus, forgiveness is not rare. People know that others are flawed. They know they are flawed themselves. A man who has genuinely repented, made right what he could, and changed is rarely met with lasting grudges by his neighbours, because his neighbours are trying to live by the same standard he failed to meet and know how hard that is. The court handles justice. The community handles restoration. And in a people of genuine faith, restoration is usually possible.

## Crimes of Desperation

In a population that genuinely pursues the fruits of the spirit and has access to fair wages, work, and community support, a person stealing bread is a signal that something has gone wrong upstream, not evidence of a criminal character. The judicial system treats this category entirely differently from ordinary crime.

### The Displaced or Refugee

A person new to the empire, cut off from their community, without access to the systems that sustain everyone else. They stole because they had no other option and no one yet to turn to. This is not a moral failure; it is a person falling through a gap the community has not yet closed around them.

**Response:** Immediate provision of food and shelter. Assignment to a work unit matched to their existing skills. Access to trade training if needed. The theft is recorded as context, not as a criminal mark. If they integrate and contribute, the record is expunged after one year of good standing.

### The Mentally Ill or Broken

A person whose mind does not function as it should, whether from illness, trauma, or damage. They cannot be held to the same standard as someone in full possession of their faculties.

**Response:** Assessment by a state healer or physician first, judge second. If mental illness or severe trauma is confirmed, the person is placed in a care facility, not a labor camp. Work is assigned at the level they can manage. If magical healing is applicable it is considered at the physician's recommendation regardless of tier standing: one of the few exceptions to the tiered healing system. The community carries them because a good community does.

### The Willful Wastrel

A person who has had every opportunity the empire provides and has chosen to take from others rather than contribute. They are not desperate. They are not broken. The spell confirms it.

**Response:** First offense: assigned work, housing provided, trade offered. The empire gives one full chance. Second offense: the offer is made again with supervised labor and restricted movement. Third offense: reclassified as a standard property crime and sentenced accordingly. The empire is not obligated to carry indefinitely a person who has refused every hand extended to them.

The question a judge must ask is not *what did they take* but *why did they take it.* The spell answers this. The community provides context. False claims of desperation are treated as a minor fraud offense.

## Appeals & Magical Evidence Review

Standard verdicts are final. The single valid grounds for appeal is belief that magical evidence was fabricated or applied against the accused. A convicted person may appeal on these grounds once. The emperor himself or a mage of equivalent power conducts the review.

If the appeal is upheld, the original verdict is overturned and the fabricator faces Magic Crimes and Crimes Against Truth charges at S5.

If the appeal is found false, the convicted person receives an additional Crimes Against Truth charge at S4 added to their original sentence.

## Statute of Limitations

**The Served Sentence**

A person who has completed their punishment and demonstrated genuine change carries no permanent legal burden from that crime beyond what the sentence itself imposed. S1 and S2 records are expunged after a defined period of good standing. S3 records remain on file but do not follow the person into daily life once they have reintegrated. S4 and S5 sentences by their nature are lifetime labor: there is no post sentence burden because there is no post sentence life outside of supervised labor. A reformed person's past is their own, held in their conscience, their faith, and their desire to do better. The state does not wave it in their face once the covenant has been honored on both sides.

**The Fugitive**

A person who committed a crime and was never captured carries that crime indefinitely. There is no expiration. There is no statute of limitations for the uncaught. Time does not absolve. Distance does not absolve. A man who murdered at twenty and is found at seventy is still a murderer and faces the full consequence of his crime. The only path to relief is to come forward, face the court, and accept judgment, at which point the judge may consider the years lived and the character demonstrated, but is not obligated to reduce the sentence.

The distinction is simple: justice was available to both men. One faced it. One ran from it. The empire does not reward running.
