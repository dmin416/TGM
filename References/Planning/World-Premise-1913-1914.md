## The two great resources

### Aetherium

Actual magic: spells, enchantments, magical practitioners, magical constructs. Britain and France lead the world in aetherium based power. Their militaries field trained magical practitioners alongside conventional forces. Magical tradition runs deep in both nations institutionally. This is D&D style magic on page: spells, enchantments, summoning, magical artillery, enchanted equipment, magical infrastructure, armored knights (Order of the Garter, Loire Iris Corps, Highland Mage Corps).

### Luminite

The petroleum substitute. Underground petroleum stays stable until it surfaces, then **solidifies into Luminite crystal**. The crystal is mined, milled, and **refined and processed** into fuels and industrial feedstock (diesel equivalent slurry, combustion fractions, armored knight fuel lines). Handling is dangerous (dust, heat, fouling) but the material is **not** air detonation explosive. Whoever controls luminite deposits controls industrial energy.

## The major powers

### The German Empire (Wettin dynasty)

Heart of the world's technological edge. The Wettin emperors (**Emperor Albert II**) cultivate a relationship with their people that the old Hohenzollern line never managed: a Protestant empire with genuine popular legitimacy. German industry leads in precision manufacturing, chemistry, engineering, and applied science. Where Germany lacks magical dominance, it compensates with ingenuity (Luminite industry, Teutonic armored knights, conventional excellence). Germany backs the republican and revolutionary faction in the Spanish Civil War and sends the expeditionary force plus International Brigade alignment.

### Britain (Holy Britannian Empire in enemy propaganda)

One of the two great aetherium powers. Military and institutions are deeply integrated with magical tradition and practice. **Queen Victoria** rules as the "Eternal Queen" (youth preserved by magitech). Britain backs the Royalist faction in Spain, seeking a friendly monarchy on the Iberian Peninsula.

### France

The other great aetherium power. Alongside Britain, France leads in magical capability and magical technology. Entente partner since 1904. Wants Alsace-Lorraine back. **France holds Gibraltar** in this timeline (not Britain).

### The United States

**Realistic posture for 1914:** officially **non aligned** and wary of European war (Monroe Doctrine, no treaty entanglements), but **deep trade** with Germany (industrial exports, finance, Protestant cultural sympathy with the Wettin court). America is not a German military ally and does not send armies to Europe in 1914. Material and economic ties matter. When the continental war locks Germany in the west, Washington can still move **opportunistically** on Spanish colonies without treating Berlin as a friend on the battlefield. No cartoon alliance; plausible neutrality plus business.

### Spain

In crisis. Civil war from early 1912. Britain backs Royalists (**Alfonso XIII**). Germany backs Republicans, National Army loyal to **Crown Prince Alfonso** (German educated), and International Brigades. Southern **Aetherium belt** in the mountains is the strategic prize. Spain retains **Cuba, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the Philippines** (no Spanish American War yet). **Germany shares a land border with Spain** (Mediterranean coast enclave; rail crossing for relief columns). German Mediterranean Fleet bases at **Port Montana**.

**Story position (`Story/17`–`19`):** late January to February 1914, **before** Republican victory. Seville holds, Madrid has fallen to Royalists, expedition was encircled then relieved by Mackensen. Peninsular war still open.

**Arc outcome (later):** German backed side and International Brigades **win** the civil war; Britain humiliated; friendly government in Madrid; settlement may include **Alfonso XIV** as constitutional monarch under Republican government per [Glossary.md](../Setting/Glossary.md).

### Russia

Gone as a unitary empire. The Romanov state **collapsed in 1913**. Fragmented factions (Kolchak British backed, Denikin, Kerensky German aligned, warlords, ethnic breakaways) shape everything east of Germany.

### Korea

Independent Korean Empire; German military backing; Aetherium deposits; not annexed by Japan.

## Timeline of events

### 1912 — Early

Spanish Civil War begins. Britain backs Royalists. Germany backs the republican and revolutionary side. International Brigade volunteers pour in from Europe and America.

### Late January – February 1914 (`Story/17`–`19`, present)

Seville counterattack arc. German expedition encircled; **General Mackensen** crosses the border with twelve divisions; rail line reopened. Fleet posturing (Mediterranean and High Seas Fleet sortie; Royal Navy mirrors). Victoria and Albert II both posture hard while keeping diplomatic channels open. **Not** Republican victory yet.

### Spanish Civil War resolution (after story present)

Republican and German backed forces defeat Royalist and British supported forces on the peninsula. Germany's prestige rises. New Spanish government friendly to Berlin.

### June 28, 1914

Archduke **Franz Ferdinand** assassinated in **Sarajevo**. **Canon:** the plot is **Vatican led or clergy led** (Holy See agents, ordained conspirators, or church aligned mage cell). Princip may still be on the ground; upstream may keep Princip arrested before the motorcade while a **clergy backed team** completes the killing. Full chain of command and public blame remain to be written. War moral framing scrambles toward Rome as well as the Balkans.

### July–August 1914 — July Crisis and mobilization

Austria Hungary declares war. Mobilizations cascade. Germany activates the Schlieffen Plan. Germany marches west first.

### August 1914 — March through Belgium

Germany deploys along a massive rail corridor westward. Troop trains cross the Rhine at Cologne at extraordinary pace. The advance enters Belgium and Luxembourg.

**Route of march (Dresden to Paris):**

Dresden → Leipzig → Halle → Magdeburg → Hannover → Dortmund → Cologne → Aachen → Liège → Huy → Namur → Charleroi → Maubeuge → Mormal Forest → Le Cateau → Saint Quentin → Laon → Soissons → Château Thierry → Meaux → Paris

**Liège:** First great obstacle. Belgian forts enhanced with aetherium magical defenses (enchanted fortifications, magical artillery, embedded mages). Britain and France supply magical support. Breakthrough costs Germany dearly but succeeds through technological ingenuity and weight of force.

**Charleroi:** After Namur, German forces reach Charleroi on the Sambre. Command holds Charleroi and the bridge rather than overextending pursuit. Rearguard digs in against French Fifth Army counterattacks while the main body consolidates and resupplies before the push on Paris.

### The Liège breakthrough — Max's airdrop (not yet in `Story/`)

Rather than grinding through magically fortified Liège in frontal assault, **Max leads an airborne insertion** into the fortress command and magical infrastructure at the heart of the defensive network. The airdrop succeeds. Max's force lands inside the perimeter, bypasses outer walls, and neutralizes aetherium emitters, mage nodes, or command elements powering the enchantments. Outer forts go blind; enchantments fail. The main German force advances without full magical fortification weight. Liège collapses faster than OTL. The Schlieffen timetable holds.

### Max — from Liège to Paris

After Liège, Max stays with the advancing force for the entire campaign: Huy, Namur, Charleroi, Maubeuge, Le Cateau, Saint Quentin, Laon, Soissons, Château Thierry, Meaux, to the gates of Paris. Present at each major action with the vanguard.

### Christmas 1914 — Paris breached (partial capture)

German forces enter Paris by Christmas 1914. Not complete capture: Germany holds outer districts and has pushed into the city, but French government, military command, and key national resources are not secured. Leadership may have evacuated. Aetherium infrastructure and command nodes uncontrolled or destroyed before capture. Germany is inside Paris but does not yet own it politically or militarily.

1. **[World-and-Setting.md](../Setting/World-and-Setting.md)** still applies to **ungated** fights: enemies are competent, armies do not run dry, Max only flips outcomes through **decisive** action at a decisive point, personal power displays do not rewrite enemy strategy.

Future battles not named here may still be won or lost under the player framework. Do not contradict a fixed beat in `Story/` without updating this file first.

## Future storyline hooks

### The American betrayal (future; not yet in `Story/`)

While Germany is locked in the western campaign and besieged by Britain, the **United States** moves opportunistically on Spanish overseas territories (Cuba, Puerto Rico, Guam, Philippines). Republican Spain, Germany's ally, loses colonies to American expansion. Germany is too committed and overstretched to respond in the moment.

**Future war:** Germany and Spain versus the United States.

### Other long arc

- Max Protestant led holy war against corrupt Catholic institutional power (not lay believers); Teutonic Order role.
- Covenant Germany post victory judicial system.
- Colonial citizenship reforms if Germany wins.
- Korea as German aligned partner.
