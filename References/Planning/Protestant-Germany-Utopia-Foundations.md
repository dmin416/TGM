## Population Growth

Stable families and genuine prosperity produce large families naturally. Lutheran and Reformed theology frames children as divine blessing. Remove material anxiety and birth rates rise without coercion.

Infant mortality is the second lever. Roughly one in six German children died before age five in 1913. Improved sanitation, nutrition, midwifery and basic medicine cuts that fraction sharply. Population compounds fast when children survive to adulthood.

## Housing Policy

The Garden City model (Ebenezer Howard, 1898) already exists and Germany has the rail and industrial capacity to build it at scale. Every family receives a small house with a garden. Streets are wide and tree-lined. The state owns settlement land and leases plots at fixed rates, keeping speculators from inflating density.

Rail lines connect satellite towns to industrial centers. Workers live twenty kilometers from the factory without a long walk. Zoning law forbids tenement construction entirely. Any builder who wants a permit builds cottage-row or nothing.

The Krupp works in Essen had already built model worker towns on this principle. Scale it nationally and the apartment block never becomes the default.

## Theological Foundation

The family hearth is sacred in Protestant tradition. The home is the first church. A man who cannot provide his family a proper house fails a spiritual obligation. A state that makes proper houses affordable fulfills one.

## Work, Meaning and the Adamic Calling

Work is worship. Adam tended and kept the garden before sin entered the world. The utopia restores Eden. Thorns and thistles are removed. The calling remains.

Every man tends and keeps his corner of creation. He builds, farms, crafts, raises children and guards what is his. The ground cooperates. The vocation does not change.

---

## Callings

- **Procreation and family.** Still commanded and blessed.
- **Worship and rest.** Now gathered around the Lord's Supper and the resurrection day.
- **Justice and mercy.** Unchanged and intensified in the Sermon on the Mount.
- **Care for the poor and vulnerable.** Unchanged. James 1:27 restates it as pure religion.
- **Hospitality.** Unchanged and emphasized in the epistles.
- **Witness and proclamation.** Now the Great Commission with global scope.
- **Discipleship and teaching.** Now the whole church teaching the whole church across generations.
- **Peacemaking.** Unchanged and grounded in reconciliation through Christ.
- **Stewardship of resources.** Unchanged. Now framed as managing what belongs to God.
- **Governance and authority.** Unchanged. Romans 13 is post-resurrection.
- **Sanctification.** Now empowered by the Spirit rather than law alone.

Dominion over creation stays as tending and keeping. The mandate did not expire. The curse on the ground is being reversed in Christ and completed at the renewal of all things.

---

## Meaning

Every generation stands individually responsible before God. Comfort is not the goal. Faithfulness is the goal. A man with a guaranteed house and full pantry still owes his full labor to his calling.

Craft mastery is never finished. Spiritual formation is lifelong. Family and community carry genuine stakes because children can still fail and marriages still require tending.
