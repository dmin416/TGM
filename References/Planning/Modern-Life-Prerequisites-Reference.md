# Modern Life: Everything and What It Needs

Rough draft. Prerequisites reference for every major component of modern civilization: what each technology is and what it requires from a 1914 industrial baseline. Use for Max industrial planning, dependency ordering, and patent investment. Not story canon until cited. ## Medicine and Healthcare

### Antibiotics

**What:** Penicillin, sulfonamides, streptomycin, tetracyclines

**Needs:** Microbiology knowledge, sterile fermentation vessels, industrial filtration, pharmacology testing, cold chain distribution. Sulfonamides need existing dye chemistry only. Penicillin needs directed mold cultivation and extraction chemistry.

### Vaccines

**What:** Smallpox, typhoid, cholera, influenza, polio and others

**Needs:** Germ theory, sterile production facility, glass vials, refrigerated storage and transport, injection needles, adjuvant chemistry, animal testing protocols.

### Blood Transfusion System

**What:** Field and hospital transfusion with stored blood

**Needs:** ABO blood typing (Landsteiner 1901), sodium citrate anticoagulant, glass bottles, rubber tubing, refrigeration, donor registry, cross matching protocol.

### Surgical Anesthesia

**What:** Safe general and local anesthesia

**Needs:** Ether and chloroform exist but are crude. Cyclopropane needs organic synthesis. Local anesthetics need cocaine derivatives or synthetic alternatives. Dosing knowledge and monitoring are the critical gaps.

### Sterile Surgical Technique

**What:** Infection free surgery

**Needs:** Autoclave steam sterilization, rubber gloves, surgical masks, sterile draping, antiseptic solutions, gown and cap protocol, understanding of infection vector.

### X-Ray Imaging

**What:** Diagnostic bone and chest imaging

**Needs:** Vacuum tubes, high voltage transformer, photographic film or fluorescent screen, lead shielding, positioning frames. All achievable with 1914 components.

### Insulin

**What:** Diabetes treatment

**Needs:** Bovine or porcine pancreas supply, extraction chemistry using cold ethanol and acid, filtration, standardized dosing units, sterile injection protocol.

### Dialysis

**What:** Kidney failure treatment

**Needs:** Semipermeable membrane material, blood pump, anticoagulant, dialysate chemical solution, precise flow control valves, vascular access technique.

### Intravenous Therapy

**What:** Fluid resuscitation, drug delivery

**Needs:** Sterile saline and glucose solutions, glass bottles, rubber tubing, steel needles, air filter vents, flow control clamps. All available 1914.

### Ventilator

**What:** Mechanical breathing assistance

**Needs:** Bellows or rotary pump, one way valves, pressure gauge, tubing, endotracheal tube, cuff seal. Basic version achievable with 1914 engineering.

### ICU Monitoring

**What:** Continuous patient vital sign tracking

**Needs:** Electrocardiograph (Einthoven string galvanometer exists 1903), blood pressure cuffs (exists), thermometers (exists), pulse oximetry needs photoelectric cells and electronics.

## Sanitation and Water

### Municipal Water Treatment

**What:** Clean potable water at scale

**Needs:** Sand filtration beds, chlorine dosing (sodium hypochlorite chemistry), flocculation with alum, settling tanks, distribution pressure pumps. Chemistry all available 1914.

### Sewage Treatment

**What:** Safe waste disposal preventing disease

**Needs:** Screening, settling tanks, activated sludge aeration (knowledge available 1914), sludge drying beds, outfall engineering, bacteriology monitoring.

### Indoor Plumbing

**What:** Running hot and cold water in buildings

**Needs:** Cast iron or copper pipe, pressure water tower or pump station, hot water boiler, trap valves, sewage connection. All available 1914.

### Garbage Collection and Disposal

**What:** Systematic waste removal

**Needs:** Vehicles, collection schedule, landfill site engineering, basic recycling separation, composting knowledge.

## Energy and Power

### Electrical Grid

**What:** Reliable delivered electricity at scale

**Needs:** Coal or hydroelectric generation, alternating current transformers (exist), copper transmission wire, wooden or steel poles, distribution substations, metering, insulation.

### Hydroelectric Power

**What:** Clean renewable generation from water

**Needs:** Dam engineering, concrete, turbine manufacture, generator, transmission infrastructure, site survey and hydrology.

### Natural Gas Distribution

**What:** Piped fuel gas to homes and industry

**Needs:** Steel pipeline, compression stations, metering, safety valves, leak detection, distribution grid engineering. Coal gas already distributed in cities.

### Nuclear Power

**What:** High density clean baseload generation

**Needs:** Uranium enrichment, reactor vessel steel, graphite or heavy water moderator, control rod mechanism, cooling system, containment structure, radiation monitoring. Requires fission discovery first.

### Solar Panel

**What:** Photovoltaic electricity generation

**Needs:** Highly purified silicon, semiconductor doping, anti reflective coating, glass encapsulation, aluminum frame, inverter electronics. Silicon purification is the key bottleneck.

### Large Scale Battery Storage

**What:** Grid energy storage

**Needs:** Lithium mining and processing, cobalt or iron phosphate chemistry, separator membranes, electrolyte, precision cell manufacturing, battery management electronics.

## Communications

### Radio Broadcasting

**What:** Mass audio communication

**Needs:** Vacuum tube transmitter and receiver, antenna, broadcast tower, microphone, recording capability. All achievable from 1914 components with improvement.

### FM Radio

**What:** Clear static free radio signal

**Needs:** Frequency modulation circuit, wideband receiver, better vacuum tubes. Engineering problem solvable by 1916.

### Telephone Network

**What:** Universal voice communication

**Needs:** Switching exchange (manual or automatic), copper wire, handset, underground or pole mounted cable, subscriber billing system.

### Television

**What:** Moving image broadcast

**Needs:** Cathode ray tube, electron gun, magnetic deflection coils, broadcast transmitter, camera tube, synchronization electronics, antenna.

### Satellite Communication

**What:** Global radio relay

**Needs:** Rocket to orbit, solar panels, transponder electronics, ground station dishes, orbital mechanics knowledge, radiation hardened components.

### Internet

**What:** Global data network

**Needs:** Computers at nodes, fiber optic or copper wire links, packet switching protocol, DNS naming system, router hardware, server infrastructure.

### Mobile Phone

**What:** Portable wireless telephone

**Needs:** Transistors, integrated circuits, cell tower infrastructure, frequency allocation, battery, microphone and speaker, switching network.

### Smartphone

**What:** Pocket computer with communications

**Needs:** Advanced integrated circuits, touchscreen display, GPS receiver, camera sensor, high capacity battery, internet connectivity, app software ecosystem.

### GPS Navigation

**What:** Precise location anywhere on earth

**Needs:** Constellation of satellites with atomic clocks, ground control stations, receiver chip, ephemeris data broadcast, signal processing software.

## Transportation

### Paved Road Network

**What:** All weather vehicle roads

**Needs:** Portland cement or bitumen asphalt, grading machinery, drainage engineering, bridge construction, surveying. Bitumen and concrete both available 1914.

### Automobile

**What:** Personal motor transport

**Needs:** Internal combustion engine (exists), rubber tires (exists), fuel supply chain, roads, driver training, traffic law, repair infrastructure.

### Commercial Aviation Piston

**What:** Passenger air travel by propeller aircraft

**Needs:** Aluminum alloys, air cooled radial engines, navigation instruments, weather forecasting, airports, radio communication, structural engineering knowledge.

### Jet Aviation

**What:** High speed passenger and freight air travel

**Needs:** High temperature nickel chromium alloys for turbine blades, precision turbine casting, aerodynamic knowledge, pressurized fuselage, jet fuel refining.

### Container Shipping

**What:** Standardized global freight

**Needs:** Steel containers in standard dimensions, large diesel ships, purpose built cranes, port infrastructure, intermodal truck and rail links.

### High Speed Rail

**What:** Fast intercity rail travel

**Needs:** Welded continuous rail, electric overhead wire, aerodynamic train design, signaling and switching, reinforced straight line track geometry.

### Subway and Metro

**What:** Urban underground rail

**Needs:** Tunnel boring machinery or cut and cover construction, electric traction, ventilation, station construction, signaling, rolling stock.

### Helicopter

**What:** Vertical flight craft

**Needs:** Specific blade pitch control mechanism (collective and cyclic), light powerful engine, tail rotor anti torque, rotor head engineering. Achievable 1918 to 1920.

## Food and Agriculture

### Synthetic Fertilizer at Scale

**What:** Nitrogen, phosphate and potassium supply for crops

**Needs:** Haber Bosch ammonia (exists 1909), superphosphate manufacturing, potash mining, distribution infrastructure.

### Pesticides and Herbicides

**What:** Crop protection chemistry

**Needs:** Organic chemistry industry, application equipment, safety protocols. DDT and organochlorines need specific chlorination chemistry.

### Hybrid Seed Program

**What:** High yield crop varieties

**Needs:** Systematic crossbreeding program, seed bank storage, distribution network, agricultural extension service.

### Cold Chain Distribution

**What:** Refrigerated food transport from farm to consumer

**Needs:** Mechanical refrigeration units on vehicles and rail cars, insulation materials, refrigerated warehouses, temperature monitoring, fast transport network.

### Canning Industry

**What:** Long shelf life preserved food

**Needs:** Tin plate (exists), can seaming machines, pressure retort cooking vessels, food safety testing, distribution.

### Freeze Drying

**What:** Light stable preserved food

**Needs:** Vacuum chamber, refrigeration to freeze product first, vacuum pump, moisture measurement, packaging. All available 1914 with directed engineering.

### Supermarket Supply Chain

**What:** Diverse food available year round to urban consumers

**Needs:** All of the above plus trucks, roads, packaging industry, retail refrigeration, checkout systems, waste management.

## Construction and Housing

### Reinforced Concrete

**What:** Strong cheap durable structure

**Needs:** Portland cement (exists), steel rod, understanding of tension and compression behavior, form work carpentry, concrete mixing. All available 1914.

### Structural Steel Frame

**What:** Tall buildings and long span bridges

**Needs:** Steel rolling mills (exist), bolt and rivet connections (exist), structural engineering calculations, erection cranes, fire protection cladding.

### Elevator

**What:** Vertical transport in buildings

**Needs:** Electric motor (exists), steel cable, counterweight, safety governor brake, control panel, shaft construction, door interlocks.

### Float Glass

**What:** Flat distortion free window glass

**Needs:** Tin bath at precise temperature, glass chemistry, continuous casting furnace, edge trimming. Pilkington process requires knowing tin float concept.

### Central Heating

**What:** Whole building temperature control

**Needs:** Boiler (exists), cast iron radiators (exist), pipe network, thermostat bimetal strip, coal or gas fuel supply.

### Air Conditioning

**What:** Summer cooling

**Needs:** Carrier designed it 1902. Scaling to residential requires cheaper compressors, safe refrigerant chemistry (Freon 1928 or alternative), electrical supply.

### Plumbing and Fixtures

**What:** Hot and cold water in buildings

**Needs:** Copper or iron pipe, solder, water pressure supply, hot water cylinder, ceramic fixtures, waste trap design.

### Insulation Materials

**What:** Thermal and acoustic building insulation

**Needs:** Fiberglass needs glass fiber drawing technology. Rock wool needs basalt or slag melting and spinning. Both achievable from 1914 materials.

## Manufacturing and Industry

### Arc Welding

**What:** Permanent metal joining

**Needs:** Stable DC electric supply, electrode chemistry, flux coating knowledge, operator training. Achievable 1914 with existing electrical industry.

### Precision Machining

**What:** Interchangeable parts manufacture

**Needs:** Hardened tool steel, measuring instruments (micrometer exists), grinding machines, jigs and fixtures, quality control gauges.

### Aluminum at Scale

**What:** Lightweight structural metal

**Needs:** Hall Héroult electrolytic process (exists 1886), cheap electricity, bauxite ore supply, carbon anode manufacture, rolling and forming mills.

### Synthetic Rubber

**What:** Petroleum derived rubber substitute

**Needs:** German chemistry had early work. Styrene butadiene route needs petrochemical feedstocks. Chloroprene route more accessible from coal tar chemistry of 1914.

### Plastics

**What:** Lightweight mouldable materials

**Needs:** Bakelite exists 1907. Polyethylene and vinyl need petroleum feedstocks and high pressure chemistry. Cellulose acetate available from existing chemical industry.

### Precision Casting

**What:** Complex metal shapes

**Needs:** Investment casting with wax pattern, ceramic shell, controlled atmosphere furnace for high temperature alloys. Sand casting exists.

### Ball Bearing Manufacturing

**What:** Low friction rotation

**Needs:** Precision steel spheres within tight tolerance, hardened race rings, cage manufacture. SKF developed industrially 1907. Scale up straightforward.

## Consumer Products and Comfort

### Refrigerator

**What:** Home food preservation

**Needs:** Sealed compression refrigerant system, insulated cabinet, electric motor, thermostat. Sulfur dioxide or ammonia refrigerants available 1914.

### Washing Machine

**What:** Mechanical clothes cleaning

**Needs:** Electric motor, drum or agitator, water inlet and drain, timer, detergent chemistry. All available 1914.

### Electric Stove

**What:** Controllable cooking heat

**Needs:** Nichrome resistance wire (exists 1906), cast iron hob, thermostat, electrical connection. Straightforward 1914 manufacture.

### Vacuum Cleaner

**What:** Powered floor cleaning

**Needs:** Electric motor, centrifugal fan, filter bag, hose and nozzle. Hoover existed 1908. Improved models straightforward.

### Electric Iron

**What:** Fabric pressing

**Needs:** Nichrome heating element, cast sole plate, thermostat. General Electric made one 1882. Improved versions trivial.

### Ballpoint Pen

**What:** Reliable portable writing

**Needs:** Tungsten carbide ball ground to precise sphere, oil based ink with correct viscosity, brass housing, capillary feed. Bíró achieved 1938.

### Safety Razor

**What:** Safe self shaving

**Needs:** Precision thin steel blade stamping, handle design, blade disposal. Gillette patented 1904. Already exists.

### Adhesive Tape

**What:** Clear pressure sensitive tape

**Needs:** Cellulose acetate or cellophane backing, rubber resin pressure sensitive adhesive, release coating, slitting and rewinding. 3M achieved 1930.

### Zipper

**What:** Reliable fastener

**Needs:** Brass or aluminum teeth, precision stamping, slider mechanism. Sundback achieved reliable design 1913 to 1917.

### Velcro

**What:** Reusable fabric fastener

**Needs:** Nylon hook strip and loop strip, weaving machinery. De Mestral achieved 1941. Requires nylon production first.

### Nylon

**What:** Strong synthetic fiber and fabric

**Needs:** Hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid chemistry, polymerization reactor, melt spinning equipment. DuPont achieved 1935.

### Aluminum Foil

**What:** Food wrapping and cooking

**Needs:** Aluminum rolling mills to extreme thinness, annealing, slitting. Aluminum production exists 1914. Foil rolling is incremental.

### Plastic Wrap

**What:** Transparent food covering

**Needs:** Polyvinylidene chloride or polyethylene film extrusion. Requires petroleum chemistry.

### Disposable Tissue

**What:** Paper hygiene product

**Needs:** Creped tissue paper machine, packaging. Paper industry exists. Kleenex introduced 1924 from existing paper technology.

## Computing and Electronics

### Vacuum Tube Amplifier

**What:** Signal amplification for radio and communication

**Needs:** Glass envelope, tungsten filament, nickel grid and plate, hard vacuum pump, precise assembly. de Forest triode exists 1906.

### Transistor

**What:** Solid state amplifier and switch

**Needs:** Highly pure germanium or silicon, controlled doping, point contact or junction fabrication, understanding of semiconductor physics. Bell Labs achieved 1947.

### Integrated Circuit

**What:** Multiple transistors on single chip

**Needs:** Silicon wafer preparation, photolithography, chemical etching, metallization, packaging. Kilby achieved 1958.

### Electronic Computer

**What:** Programmable calculation machine

**Needs:** Vacuum tubes or transistors, memory (mercury delay line or magnetic drum), input output, programming language, cooling. Zuse achieved 1941 with relays.

### Magnetic Recording

**What:** Audio and data storage on tape

**Needs:** Iron oxide particle coating on acetate tape, recording head electromagnet, precise tape drive mechanism. AEG and BASF achieved 1935.

### Hard Drive

**What:** Large data storage

**Needs:** Magnetic disk coating, read write head floating nanometers above surface, servo motor, controller electronics. IBM achieved 1956.

## Notes on Critical Dependencies

**Electricity is the foundation of almost everything.** Without a reliable electrical grid, roughly two thirds of items listed here cannot function. Grid construction should precede most other technology investment.

**Germ theory unlocks an enormous fraction of medicine.** Once accepted and taught systematically, clean water, sterile surgery, vaccine programs and antibiotic development all flow from it. The knowledge exists by 1914 but institutional adoption is incomplete.

**Organic chemistry is the second great enabler.** Plastics, synthetic rubber, pharmaceuticals, dyes, explosives, synthetic fibers and fertilizers all derive from the same industrial organic chemistry base. Germany already has the world's best foundation.

**Aluminum and steel together build the physical world.** Both industries exist by 1914. Expanding them and improving alloy knowledge unlocks aviation, construction, vehicles and consumer goods simultaneously.

**The single most transformative near term intervention remains directed antibiotic discovery.** More human life hours have been lost to bacterial infection than to any other cause. Arriving with this knowledge in 1914 industrial Germany represents the highest return on any single action.
