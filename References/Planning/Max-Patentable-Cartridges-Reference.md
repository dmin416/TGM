# Max Patentable Cartridges Reference

Rough draft. Cartridges Max can file patents for ahead of OTL introduction, drawn from omniscient knowledge of post-1913 small-arms development. Use for patent pacing, industrial planning, and weapons dialogue. Not story canon until cited. Distinct from the **1914 program** already established in character ([German-Military-Calibers.md](../Military/German-Military-Calibers.md): 6×54mm, 7×77mm, 9×87mm, 13×99mm, improved 20×138mm). This list is the broader forward portfolio beyond that baseline.
