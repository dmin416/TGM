# Portugal — Cultural Regions

## Algarve

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Fado music evenings — traditional melancholic song performances
2. Sardine grilling festivals — summer beachside fish roasts
3. Cork harvesting demonstrations — sustainable tree bark stripping
4. Moorish chimney preservation — Arab architectural heritage
5. Cataplana cooking — copper pot seafood preparation
6. Cliff walking — dramatic Atlantic coastline hiking
7. Ceramic tile (azulejo) painting — traditional decorative art
8. Fishermen's festivals — maritime heritage celebrations
9. Almond blossom viewing — spring agricultural tourism
10. Traditional boat building — fishing vessel craft preservation

**Specialties:** Cataplana, grilled sardines, percebes (goose barnacles), octopus salad, almond cake, medronho brandy, carob products, Dom Rodrigo sweets

---

## Douro Valley

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Port wine estate tours — fortified wine production and tasting
2. Grape harvest (vindima) — traditional foot-stomping in lagares
3. Rabelo boat rides — traditional wine transport vessel tours
4. Quinta overnight stays — vineyard estate accommodations
5. Terraced vineyard hiking — ancient stone terrace exploration
6. Wine brotherhood ceremonies — traditional vintner societies
7. Schist village tours — traditional slate stone architecture
8. Fado singing — regional musical tradition performances
9. Douro railway journeys — scenic valley train tours
10. Almond and olive harvest — agricultural tradition celebrations

**Specialties:** Port wine, Douro table wine, presunto ham, alheira sausage, cabrito (roast kid), olive oil, almonds, bôla cake

---

## Alentejo

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Cork forest walks — sustainable harvesting education
2. Cante Alentejano — polyphonic work song tradition
3. Whitewashed village tours — traditional architecture preservation
4. Megalithic stone circle pilgrimages — prehistoric monument visits
5. Pork slaughter festivals (matança) — traditional meat preservation
6. Montado ecosystem tours — cork oak savanna exploration
7. Black pig raising — indigenous Iberian breed preservation
8. Pottery making — traditional earthenware craft
9. Wheat field festivals — agricultural harvest celebrations
10. Bullfighting traditions — Portuguese horseback style

**Specialties:** Açorda (bread soup), migas, pork dishes, black pork, Évora cheese, ensopado (lamb stew), sericaia dessert, olive oil

---

## Azores

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Whale watching expeditions — Atlantic cetacean observation tours
2. Cozido das Furnas — volcanic ground cooking demonstrations
3. Holy Spirit festivals (Império) — religious crown processions
4. Hydrangea viewing — island floral landscape tourism
5. Pineapple greenhouse tours — Azorean tropical fruit cultivation
6. Whaling heritage museums — historical industry preservation
7. Crater lake hiking — volcanic landscape exploration
8. Traditional bullfighting (touradas à corda) — rope bullfighting
9. Azorean folk dancing — traditional island music and dance
10. Cheese making — São Jorge cheese production demonstrations

**Specialties:** Cozido das Furnas, lapas (limpets), alcatra (beef stew), bolo lêvedo bread, queijadas, São Jorge cheese, pineapple, passion fruit

---

## Madeira

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Levada walking — irrigation channel hiking tradition
2. Madeira wine lodge tours — fortified wine aging and tasting
3. Flower Festival — spring floral parade and carpet displays
4. Carnival celebrations — elaborate costume street festivals
5. Espetada grilling — beef skewer bay laurel cooking
6. Wicker basket sledding — Monte toboggan descent tradition
7. Embroidery workshops — traditional needlework craft
8. New Year's fireworks — world-famous harbor pyrotechnics display
9. Bolo de Mel making — traditional honey cake preparation
10. Banana plantation tours — subtropical fruit cultivation

**Specialties:** Espetada, bolo do caco (garlic bread), black scabbard fish, lapas, bolo de mel, Madeira wine, passion fruit, bananas
