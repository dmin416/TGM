# Trenches, Guns and Magic — The Villain Bible
### A Grounded Antagonist Reference for an Alternate-History WWI (1914–1920)

**TL;DR**
- This bible profiles 55+ antagonist or potential-antagonist nations of an honorable Germany in the TGM setting, each grounded in real 1880–1920 atrocities, named villainous leaders, and authentic 1914 military capacities — designed so Lt. Max Friedrich's adventures feel historically loaded rather than invented.
- The strongest villain anchors are the colonial superpowers (Britain, France, Belgium, Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, the United States) whose documented horrors — Amritsar, the Algerian conquest, the Congo Free State, Italian Libya, Putumayo, Aceh, the Haiti occupation — give the British/French Aetherium oligarchy a self-evidently sinister face; the secondary tier (Japan, the Ottoman Three Pashas, Serbia's Black Hand, Mexico's Huerta, the Russian White Terror) provides crisis-driven antagonists whose villainy is contemporary to 1914–1920 rather than ancestral.
- Every profile flags both the named historical perpetrator Max can confront (Dyer, Leopold II, von Trotha, Talaat Pasha, Weyler, Liakhov, Kolchak, Arana, etc.) and the real independence movement Germany can plausibly ally with (Indian Ghadar, Irish Republicans, Vietnamese Đông Du, Egyptian Wafd, Korean Righteous Armies, Persian Jangali, Pan-Arab Sharifians), giving Max both a target and a recruit in nearly every theater.

## Key Findings

**The colonial powers are pre-villainized by their own paper trails.** Britain killed 26,000 Boer women and children and over 14,000 Black African internees in concentration camps 1900–1902 (UK National Archives, catalogue ref WO 32/8061: "It is estimated that 26,000 Boer women and children died, as well as over 14,000 black inmates, mainly as a result of disease and malnutrition"); shot ≥379 (official) to 1,000+ (Indian estimate) unarmed worshippers at Amritsar on 13 April 1919 under Brig-Gen Reginald Dyer; hanged Egyptian villagers at Denshawai in 1906 under Lord Cromer. Belgium under Leopold II oversaw the deaths of an estimated 5–10 million Congolese (Hochschild, *King Leopold's Ghost*, 1998). Italy invented aerial bombing of civilians at Ain Zara on 1 November 1911 under General Carlo Caneva. The Netherlands' Colonel Van Daalen massacred ≥2,922 Acehnese including 1,149 women and children in 1904. Portugal ran the *chibalo* forced-labor system in Angola and Mozambique into the 1960s. Use the real names and the moral weight is already loaded.

**The Ottoman Three Pashas are the war's hardest moral test for the honorable-Germany framing.** Talaat, Enver and Djemal directly ordered an Armenian Genocide that killed "at least 664,000 and possibly as many as 1.2 million" Armenians during 1915–1916 (US Holocaust Memorial Museum Encyclopedia) — plus mass killings of Assyrians and Anatolian Greeks. Historical Germany sheltered Talaat in Berlin until his assassination by Soghomon Tehlirian on 15 March 1921. In TGM, this becomes the moral knife-edge — either Germany breaks publicly with the CUP, or Max becomes the officer who refuses to look away.

**The "Russia is many monsters" framing solves the Tsar problem.** With Imperial Russia replaced by Kolchak/Denikin/"Kerensky," the Whites' real record — the 1985 UN Whitaker Report cited "100,000 to 250,000 Jews in more than 2,000 pogroms killed by a mixture of Whites, Cossacks and Ukrainian nationalists" — gives Germany a clean enemy without compromising the family-court relationship of Wilhelm II and Tsar Nicholas II.

**Germany's own historical sins need explicit, in-canon handling.** The Herero-Nama genocide killed "some 65,000 Herero (80% of the total Herero population) and 10,000 Nama (50% of the total Nama population) between 1904 and 1907" (1985 UN Whitaker Report) under Lt-Gen Lothar von Trotha's 2 October 1904 *Vernichtungsbefehl*; the Maji Maji Rebellion suppression killed by official German count 75,000 but per Tanja Bührer and Gwassa 250,000–300,000 (Oxford *Holocaust and Genocide Studies*, 2021). TGM canon should disgrace von Trotha and prosecute the worst offenders rather than ignore the record.

## Details — Antagonist Profiles by Region

### EUROPEAN GREAT POWERS AND BORDER NATIONS

**1. GREAT BRITAIN (The Aetherium Crown).** *Motive:* Maintain global colonial monopoly on Aetherium-rich territories (India, Egypt, South Africa); strangle Spanish-German continental bloc. *Real sins:* Boer concentration camps under Lord Kitchener killed 26,000 Boer women and children plus over 14,000 Black African inmates 1900–02 (UK National Archives WO 32/8061); Amritsar 13 April 1919 — Brig-Gen Reginald Dyer ordered 1,650 rounds fired into a crowd of approximately 10,000 in Jallianwala Bagh, ≥379 dead official, perhaps 1,000+ unofficial; Punjab Lt-Gov Sir Michael O'Dwyer endorsed it and imposed the "crawling order"; Denshawai hangings 28 June 1906 under Lord Cromer (four hanged publicly, eight flogged 50 lashes each); Sudan reconquest 1898 (Kitchener desecrated the Mahdi's tomb, made the skull into an inkwell); recurring Bengal famines. *Leaders:* PM Asquith then Lloyd George; First Lord Churchill; Kitchener (died at sea aboard HMS *Hampshire* June 1916 — backstory after that date); King George V (Kaiser's cousin). *Actions:* Hunger blockade — per 1914-1918-Online "between 478,500 and 800,000 [German] civilians died from diseases related to hunger and malnutrition," with the German Board of Public Health's official December 1918 figure of 763,000 and historian Jay Winter's independent estimate of 300,000 excess deaths; subsidizes Russian Whites; supports Japan vs German Pacific. *Hooks:* Max smuggles arms to Ghadar Party Sikhs; Irish Republican Brotherhood–Germany alliance (echoing OTL 1916 Easter Rising and Roger Casement's *Aud*); uncovering an Aetherium slave-mine in Bengal. *Capabilities:* Royal Navy 28+ dreadnoughts; magitech airships; BEF 150,000 regulars; Indian Army 1.5 million wartime; global finance hub.

**2. FRANCE (The Aetherium Republic).** *Motive:* *Revanche* for Alsace-Lorraine; lock down North African Aetherium. *Sins:* Algerian conquest 1830+ killed approximately one-third of pre-colonial population (Marshal Pélissier's 1845 *enfumades* smoked tribes to death in caves); Madagascar conquest under Gallieni 1896–99 killed ~100,000; Voulet-Chanoine Mission 1898–99 massacred entire villages across Niger (Birni-N'Konni, May 1899: ~10,000 killed in a day, women raped and impaled, children thrown in wells); Yên Thế insurgency crushed 1913 with execution of Hoàng Hoa Thám (Đề Thám); *Tirailleurs sénégalais* 200,000 conscripted in WWI with ~30,000 dead; Mokrani Revolt suppression 1871 killed ~250,000 Berbers. *Leaders:* President Poincaré; PM Clemenceau ("Tiger"); Marshal Joffre (Plan XVII, 27,000 dead on 22 August 1914 alone); Marshal Foch; General Nivelle (April 1917 disaster); General Sarrail (Salonika meddler). *Hooks:* Vietnamese exile Phan Bội Châu in Berlin; Algerian Emir Khaled (grandson of Abd al-Qadir) as ally; Berber Aetherium-slavery exposé. *Capabilities:* Army 1.3 million August 1914 / 3.6 million mobilizable; Schneider 75mm field gun; Sahara/Madagascar/Indochina Aetherium.

**3. ITALY (Papal State variant).** *Motive:* Recover lost Papal States; expand into Catholic North Africa. *Sins:* Italian Libya 1911 — Lt. Giulio Gavotti dropped the first aerial bomb on civilians at Ain Zara on 1 November 1911; **Sciara Sciat reprisal massacre 23–26 October 1911** killed ~4,000 Libyan civilians under General Carlo Caneva; later reconcentration camps killed ~40,000–60,000 Cyrenaican Bedouins. **General Luigi Cadorna** as WWI C-in-C executed ~750 Italian soldiers by decimation 1916–17 — more than any other belligerent. *Leaders (Papal-State variant):* fictional Pius-X-style reactionary Pope; real Cardinal Rafael Merry del Val; Gabriele D'Annunzio (decadent ultranationalist). *Hooks:* Documentary evidence of Sciara Sciat handed to neutral press; alliance with Risorgimento exiles wanting secular Italy. *Capabilities:* Army 1 million mobilizable; dreadnoughts *Dante Alighieri* and *Conte di Cavour*; weak artillery.

**4. BELGIUM.** *Motive:* TGM satellite of the Anglo-French Aetherium cartel. *Sins:* Congo Free State 1885–1908 under Leopold II — Force Publique (12,100+ men in 21 companies by 1914) enforced rubber quotas by hand-amputation, hostage-taking, village-burning; population fell by an estimated 5–10 million per the 1919 Belgian commission and Hochschild's *King Leopold's Ghost* (1998); exposed by E.D. Morel and Roger Casement 1903–04. Force Publique station chief **Léon Rom** decorated his garden with severed African heads (Conrad's Kurtz model). *Leaders:* King Albert I; Colonial Minister Jules Renkin. *Hooks:* Max liberates a Congolese rubber station; Lettow-Vorbeck's German askaris meet Force Publique deserters. *Capabilities:* Belgian Army 117,000 with Liège/Namur/Antwerp forts; Force Publique 12,100+.

**5. NETHERLANDS.** *Motive:* Neutrality but Anglo-French-leaning; protect East Indies oil/tin. *Sins:* Aceh War 1873–1914 — Colonel **Gotfried van Daalen** under Governor **J.B. van Heutsz** destroyed multiple Gayo/Alas villages in 1904, killing 2,922 Acehnese including 1,149 women and children at Kuta Reh, against just 26 Dutch losses (Wikipedia "Jo van Heutsz"); Hendrik Colijn (future Dutch PM) wrote in 1894 of ordering 9 women and 3 children gathered together and shot. *Leaders:* Queen Wilhelmina; PM Cort van der Linden; van Heutsz (Amsterdam monument); Colijn. *Hooks:* Acehnese resistance seeking German aid; Sarekat Islam contact; U-boat refueling crisis. *Capabilities:* KNIL 33,000 in Indies; ~200,000 mobilizable.

**6. SWITZERLAND.** Banker-launderer; Swiss mercenary officers historically served Leopold's Force Publique. *Leaders:* General Ulrich Wille (Germanophile army commander). *Hooks:* Zürich gold-laundering scandal. *Capabilities:* 250,000 mountain militia.

**7. PORTUGAL.** Republican regime 1910 seeking British protection. *Sins:* *Chibalo* forced labor across Angola, Mozambique, São Tomé, Guinea — officially codified 1899, amended 1911 and 1914 (Eric Allina: "slavery by any other name"); São Tomé cocoa scandal exposed 1905–07 by Henry Nevinson and John Harris; ~500,000 Angolans eventually fled forced labor; Bailundo war 1902; Gungunyane defeated 1895. *Leaders:* PM Afonso Costa; Norton de Matos (Governor-General Angola). *Actions:* Sends ~55,000 troops to Western Front and Mozambique against Germans. *Hooks:* Max liberates São Tomé cocoa plantation; allies with Mbundu rebels. *Capabilities:* Army ~30,000 ill-equipped regulars.

**8. DENMARK.** Officially neutral; pro-British trade; sold Danish West Indies to USA 1917 for $25 million. *Leaders:* King Christian X; PM Carl Theodor Zahle. *Hooks:* Schleswig pro-German Danes; Iceland nationalists; mid-war Caribbean intrigue.

**9. SWEDEN (potentially co-belligerent).** Officially neutral but effectively pro-German until 1917 under PM **Hjalmar Hammarskjöld** ("Hungerskjöld"). Activist intellectuals Sven Hedin, Otto Järte, Adrian Molin urged Central Powers entry. In 1915 German Under-Secretary Arthur Zimmermann proposed a Swedish-led "Nordic Bloc"; Sweden declined (~90% of metallurgy depended on British coal). Sweden mined the Öresund's Kogrundsrännan channel on 28 July 1916 under German pressure. Iron ore from Kiruna/Gällivare: ~7 million tons/year pre-war via Luleå and Narvik. Hammarskjöld resigned 5 March 1917 over bread riots. *Leaders:* King Gustaf V (1914 Courtyard Speech crisis ousted Liberal PM Karl Staaff); General Otto Nordenskiöld. *Hooks:* In TGM, a plausible co-belligerent option opening a Finnish front against Russia; **Finnish Jäger Battalion 27** (OTL: 1,900 Finns trained in Germany 1915–17 to fight Russia); Max in Stockholm meeting the Activists. *Capabilities:* Army 600,000 mobilizable; iron ore as strategic resource.

**10. NORWAY.** Pro-British "neutral ally"; merchant marine lost ~2,000 sailors to U-boats. King Haakon VII (wife Maud is Edward VII's daughter — direct enemy-court line).

**11. GREECE (the National Schism).** *Royalist:* King **Constantine I** (Kaiser's brother-in-law, married to Sophia of Prussia). *Venizelist:* PM **Eleftherios Venizelos** launched coup-Provisional Government in Thessaloniki on 9 October 1916. *Allied atrocities vs Greeks:* **Noemvriana, 1–3 December 1916** — French Vice-Admiral **Louis Dartige du Fournet** landed ~2,500–3,000 marines at Piraeus; battleship *Mirabeau* shelled Athens (30–40 shells near the Zappeion and Royal Palace); Royalist Epistratoi paramilitaries lynched ≥35 Venizelists; Allied 47–60 killed/134–167 wounded, Greek royalist ~82 killed (sources include Dartige du Fournet's memoir *Souvenirs de guerre d'un amiral 1914–1916*). The Allied **blockade December 1916–June 1917** starved royalist southern Greece. French Senator **Charles Jonnart** delivered a 24-hour ultimatum on 11 June 1917 forcing Constantine's abdication. *Royalist leaders:* Constantine; Col. **Ioannis Metaxas** (future 1936 fascist dictator); General Victor Dousmanis. *Hooks:* Max as German liaison to royalist Greek army; smuggling Constantine out before forced abdication; sinking the *Mirabeau*; surrender of Rupel fort to Germans (May 1916 OTL). *Capabilities:* Greek Army 250,000 mobilizable but politically split; armored cruiser *Averof*.

**12. ROMANIA.** *Motive:* Irredentist claims on Habsburg Transylvania, Bukovina, Banat (17 August 1916 Bucharest Treaty with Entente). *Sins:* **1907 Peasants' Revolt** February–April 1907; began at Flămânzi against Jewish lessor Mochi Fischer; 140,000 troops mobilized; villages shelled; deaths official 419, French Embassy estimate 10,000–20,000, western consensus ~11,000; Captain **Ion Antonescu** (future WWII dictator) ordered firing on negotiating peasants at Galați 12 March 1907 killing 12; over 10,000 arrested (Wikipedia "1907 Romanian peasants' revolt"; Eidelberg, *The Great Romanian Peasant Revolt of 1907*, Brill 1974). *Leaders:* King Carol I (1881–1914, signed secret 1883 alliance with Germany/Austria!); then Ferdinand I; PM **Ion I.C. Brătianu** (Interior Minister during the 1907 massacres); General Alexandru Averescu. British Col. **John Norton-Griffiths** ("Empire Jack") destroyed >1,500 oilwells, 70 refineries, and ~800,000 tons of crude at Ploiești 5 November–5 December 1916. *Hooks:* Boyar war-profiteering; protecting/seizing Ploiești; Transylvanian Saxons loyal to Habsburg. *Capabilities:* 650,000 men in 23 divisions, poorly equipped; pre-war Europe's #2 oil producer.

**13. BULGARIA (if antagonist).** Plausible antagonist only with a divergence (e.g., a post-1918 Vardar defection). *Sins:* Brutal Balkan Wars 1912–13 atrocities. *Leaders:* Tsar Ferdinand I; PM **Vasil Radoslavov**; General Nikola Zhekov. *Capabilities:* ~878,000 mobilized at peak — virtually every male of military age; Balkan War veterans.

**14. OTTOMAN EMPIRE (CUP regime — the moral trap).** *Sins:* **Armenian Genocide** — Talaat ordered arrests of ~250 Armenian intellectuals in Constantinople the night of 23–24 April 1915; **Temporary Law of Deportation 30 May 1915**; per the US Holocaust Memorial Museum Encyclopedia, "at least 664,000 and possibly as many as 1.2 million died during the genocide" via Syrian death marches; Talaat's documented order "the destination of the deportations is nothingness" was read into the record at the Tehlirian trial in Berlin; ~100,000–200,000 Armenian women/children forcibly Islamized. Also the genocides of Assyrians (Seyfo) and Pontic/Anatolian Greeks. **Mount Lebanon famine 1915–18** killed ~200,000 (half the Lebanese population) — Djemal's grain requisitioning + Allied blockade + locust plague. *The Three Pashas:* **Talaat** (Interior, "primary architect"); **Enver** (War, disaster at Sarıkamış January 1915 with ~60,000 Turkish troops freezing); **Djemal** (Navy/Syria, executed Beirut-Damascus Arab nationalists 6 May 1916 — "Martyrs' Day"); plus Said Halim Pasha (figurehead Grand Vizier); Dr. Bahaeddin Şakir (Special Organization). *Hooks in TGM:* Honorable Germany breaks with the CUP; supports an Armenian state; Max in Aleppo discovers death-march evidence and reports via the real German consul **Walter Rössler** (who OTL documented atrocities); protects Armenian orphans; the Musa Dagh siege; alliance with **Sharif Hussein of Mecca** flips the Arab Revolt to the German side. *Capabilities:* Army ~800,000 mobilized; controls Dardanelles, Mesopotamian oil, Hejaz; German Liman von Sanders military mission.

**15. SERBIA (Black Hand).** *Sins:* **Colonel Dragutin Dimitrijević "Apis"** led the cabal that butchered King Alexander Obrenović and Queen Draga in the Belgrade palace on the night of 10–11 June 1903; founded the **Black Hand (Ujedinjenje ili Smrt)** May 1911; supplied four FN Model 1910 pistols, six bombs, and cyanide capsules to Princip/Čabrinović/Grabež via **Major Vojislav Tankosić** for the 28 June 1914 Sarajevo assassination; PM **Nikola Pašić** knew of the plot per Christopher Clark's *The Sleepwalkers* (2012). Serbian Third Army under General Božidar Janković carried out systematic massacres of Albanian Muslims in 1912 Kosovo/Macedonia per the **Carnegie Endowment Report on the Causes and Conduct of the Balkan Wars (1914)** — executions documented in Pristina, Gjilan, Prizren, Đakovica; ~25,000 Albanians killed by early 1913; Serbian foreign-ministry records list 239,807 Albanian expulsions; Trotsky's *Kievskaya Mysl* dispatches corroborate. *Leaders:* King Peter I Karađorđević; Regent Crown Prince Alexander; PM Pašić; Apis (executed at the Salonika Trial on 26 June 1917); Vojvoda Putnik. *Capabilities:* ~350,000 mobilized; Balkan War veterans; mountainous terrain.

### RUSSIAN CIVIL WAR FACTIONS

**16. KOLCHAK ROYALISTS.** *Sins:* **Admiral Aleksandr Kolchak**, Supreme Ruler from the 18 November 1918 Omsk coup. The Cheka's 1920 trial figure recorded 25,000 executions in Yekaterinburg Governorate alone 1918–19; Russian historian Nikita Ratkovsky estimates the total White Terror at ≥500,000 excluding pogroms. Atamans **Grigory Semyonov** (Transbaikal, Japanese-funded) and **Boris Annenkov** (Semirechye) ran torture/execution trains across Siberia. Mass executions: socialists at Omsk 25–26 December 1918 under General I.N. Krasilnikov; 670 prisoners at Ufa April 1919; 350 at Chita May 1919. *Hooks:* Max on the Trans-Siberian seizing the ~500-ton Romanov gold reserve; rescue of the Romanov family from Yekaterinburg (alt-history's single best plot lever). *Capabilities:* ~400,000 men at peak with French/British/Japanese matériel; Czechoslovak Legion wildcard.

**17. DENIKIN WARLORDS (AFSR / Volunteer Army).** **General Anton Denikin** commanded the Armed Forces of South Russia 1918–20. The 1985 UN Whitaker Report cited "100,000 to 250,000 Jews in more than 2,000 pogroms killed by a mixture of Whites, Cossacks and Ukrainian nationalists"; Nahum Gergel's foundational 1928 study attributed 17.2% of all pogroms to Denikin's forces and 40% to Symon Petliura's Ukrainian forces (Wikipedia "Pogroms during the Russian Civil War"). **Fastov massacre 6–10 September 1919** — Cossack/Volunteer Army troops killed 900–1,500 Jews. **Krasnov's Don Cossack regime 1918** executed 25,000–40,000 in Don Province. Denikin's generals issued proclamations to "extirpate the evil force which lives in the hearts of Jew-communists." *Leaders:* Denikin (resigned April 1920); Wrangel (successor); Ataman Pyotr Krasnov (later WWII Nazi collaborator); General Andrei Shkuro. *Hooks:* Max rescuing a Jewish shtetl from a Cossack raid; discrediting Denikin in the Allied press. *Capabilities:* Peak 270,000 in 1919; Caucasus oil; French/British supplies.

**18. "KERENSKY COMMUNISTS"** (the TGM label is anachronistic — flag this; Kerensky was Socialist Revolutionary, not communist). Probable intended referent: Bolsheviks under Lenin/Trotsky/**Felix Dzerzhinsky** (Cheka), with Red Terror killing 50,000–140,000 1918–20 (Ronald Suny). *Hooks:* Bolsheviks infiltrate German Spartacists; Max negotiates with Kerensky's envoy in Stockholm. *Capabilities:* Red Army ~5.5 million by 1920.

### ASIA AND PACIFIC

**19. JAPAN.** *Motive:* Seize German Pacific colonies + Qingdao + Shandong; Asian hegemony before the Western powers recover. *Sins:* **Annexation of Korea, 22 August 1910** by Resident-General **Count Terauchi Masatake** with pro-Japanese PM Yi Wan-yong; Terauchi imposed gendarmerie-police "Military Rule" 1910–19; **March First Movement 1919** suppression killed ~7,500 Koreans, wounded ~16,000, burned 715 houses; **Jeam-ri church massacre 15 April 1919** burned 29 Koreans alive; **Port Arthur Massacre 1894** under General Yamaji Motoharu killed up to 20,000 Chinese civilians; **Twenty-One Demands on China 18 January 1915** under PM Ōkuma Shigenobu attempted to make China a Japanese protectorate; assassination of Korean Queen Min in Gyeongbokgung Palace 8 October 1895 directed by Minister Miura Gorō. *Leaders:* Emperor Taishō; PM Ōkuma then Terauchi (1916–18); Foreign Minister Katō Takaaki; General Tanaka Giichi; Admiral Katō Tomosaburō. *Actions vs Germany:* At war from 23 August 1914 — siege/capture of Qingdao October–November 1914; seized all German Pacific colonies. *Hooks:* Max defending Qingdao; smuggling Korean royals out of Tokyo; arming Korean Righteous Army guerrillas in Jiandao; alliance with Yoshino Sakuzō's "Taishō Democracy" liberals. *Capabilities:* Army ~300,000 regular; Navy 3rd-largest with four British-built *Kongō*-class battlecruisers; samurai-magic tradition in TGM.

**20. CHINESE WARLORDS.** **Yuan Shikai** (President 1912–16) crushed the Second Revolution 1913; assassinated rival Song Jiaoren March 1913; conceded most of Japan's Twenty-One Demands May 1915; declared himself Hongxian Emperor December 1915, forced to abdicate March 1916; died 6 June 1916. **Warlord Era** disintegration — Beiyang Army split into Anhui clique (**Duan Qirui**, Japanese-funded "Nishihara Loans"), Zhili clique (Feng Guozhang, Cao Kun, Wu Peifu), Fengtian clique (**Zhang Zuolin**, Manchurian "Old Marshal," Japanese-backed). Warlord armies grew from ~500,000 in 1916 to ~2 million by 1928, financed by looting and opium. *Hooks:* Max allies with Sun Yat-sen's Canton KMT (OTL: Sun briefly sought German aid 1916–17); arms shipments to Mongolian princes (Ungern-Sternberg backdrop). *Capabilities:* Beiyang ~250,000; warlord armies vast, low quality.

**21. BRITISH INDIA (the Raj).** *Sins:* Bengal famines (1873–74, 1896–97, 1899–1900 — each killed 1+ million through deliberate non-intervention and grain export); **Komagata Maru May–July 1914** — Punjabi Sikhs aboard a Japanese ship turned back from Vancouver, on return 19 shot dead by British troops at Budge Budge; Amritsar (see Britain); Rowlatt Act 1919 (detention without trial). *Leaders:* Viceroy Lord Hardinge then Lord Chelmsford; Secretary of State Edwin Montagu; O'Dwyer; Dyer. *Hooks:* **Ghadar Party** émigrés (OTL: February 1915 Ghadar Mutiny was German-funded — the "Hindu-German Conspiracy" / Annie Larsen affair); rescue Lala Har Dayal; supply Aetherium-detection technology to Bengali revolutionaries. *Capabilities:* Indian Army 1.5 million wartime, ~60,000 killed.

**22. KOREA (German ally under threat).** Liberation from Japan. Resistance figures: exiled Emperor Gojong (died 21 January 1919, suspected poisoning sparked March First); Crown Prince Yi Eun (held in Tokyo); An Jung-geun (assassinated Itō Hirobumi at Harbin 26 October 1909); Syngman Rhee (in US); Righteous Armies in Manchuria with ~10,000–20,000 fighters. *Hooks:* Max delivers German rifles to Jiandao guerrillas; rescue of Korean princes; German-backed government-in-exile.

**23. PERSIA (Iran).** **Anglo-Russian Convention, 31 August 1907** partitioned Persia (Russian north / British south / neutral center). Colonel **Vladimir Liakhov**'s Persian Cossack Brigade shelled the **Majlis on 23 June 1908** under Mohammad Ali Shah, killing hundreds. **Anglo-Persian Oil Company** founded 1909 on William Knox D'Arcy's 1901 concession (60-year monopoly granted by Mozaffar ad-Din Shah for £20,000 cash + 16% profit share); Britain bought 51% in June 1914 (Churchill championing). **Persian Famine 1917–19** — mainstream estimate ~2 million dead (Abrahamian, Floor, Katouzian); Mohammad Gholi Majd's contested figure is 8–10 million blaming British Dunsterforce under General Lionel Dunsterville [FLAG: contested, depends on a disputed 18–20 million baseline population]. *Hooks:* TGM Persia is a major **plausible German ally** — OTL the Jangali movement of **Mirza Kuchik Khan** in Gilan received direct German support; Max sent to advise Kuchik Khan or attack APOC oilfields at Masjed Soleyman.

**24. SIAM (Thailand).** OTL declared war on Germany 22 July 1917 under King **Vajiravudh (Rama VI)**, Sandhurst-educated; sent the Siamese Expeditionary Force (1,284 men, left Bangkok 20 June 1918, arrived Marseille 30 July 1918, occupied Neustadt an der Haardt 1918–19 — only Asian troops to occupy German territory). French sins on Siam: 1893 Paknam Crisis — French gunboats *Comète*/*Inconstant* under Rear Admiral Edgar Humann forced the Chao Phraya on 13 July 1893, blockaded Bangkok; **Franco-Siamese Treaty 3 October 1893** ceded all Laos + 3-million-franc indemnity. *Hooks:* Sabotaging the Siamese Expeditionary Force in Champagne; cultivating pro-German Princes Paribatra/Mahidol/Rangsit.

**25. VIETNAM / FRENCH INDOCHINA.** French sins: 1885 Hué incident killed ~1,500; Đề Thám executed February 1913; opium/salt/alcohol monopolies (Régie d'Opium); Michelin's Phú Riềng rubber-plantation brutality. *Hooks:* **Phan Bội Châu's Đông Du movement** pro-German exile network (OTL); arming rebels through Siam; meeting young **Nguyễn Tất Thành** (future Ho Chi Minh) in Marseille 1918.

**26. OTTOMAN ARAB TERRITORIES (Sharif Hussein's Revolt).** **Djemal Pasha** as Syrian governor 1915–17 publicly executed dozens of Arab nationalists in Beirut and Damascus on 6 May 1916 ("Martyrs' Day"); requisitioned food causing the Mount Lebanon famine. **Sharif Hussein of Mecca** revolted June 1916 with sons Faisal and Abdullah. *Hooks in TGM:* Germany allies with Sharif Hussein FIRST, before Britain — flips the entire Arab theater. Max as German "Lawrence" figure.

**27. PHILIPPINES (Spanish in TGM).** Historic backstory: Spanish Gov-Gen **Camilo de Polavieja** executed **José Rizal** by firing squad at Bagumbayan, Manila, 7:03 a.m. 30 December 1896; the Gomburza priests garrotted 17 February 1872. *TGM present:* reformed Spain; US-backed annexationists are the new villains. *Hooks:* Max in Manila stops a US filibuster expedition; elderly Aguinaldo reconciled.

### COLONIAL AFRICA

**28. BRITISH KENYA.** Land theft from Maasai (1904 and 1911 Agreements); Kipande pass system 1915; the punitive Nandi Expedition 1905–06 — **Colonel Richard Meinertzhagen** murdered the Nandi Orkoiyot **Koitalel Arap Samoei** under flag of truce on 19 October 1905. *Hooks:* Arms to Nandi and Kikuyu.

**29. BRITISH NIGERIA.** Aro Confederacy crushed 1901–02; **Satiru Massacre 10 March 1906** — British forces under Lieutenant W.B. Hillary killed ~2,000 Mahdist followers. *Hooks:* Sokoto Caliphate remnants seek Pan-Islamic German alliance.

**30. BRITISH SOUTH AFRICA.** Boer camps already cited. **Bambatha Rebellion 1906** suppression under Colonel Duncan McKenzie killed ~3,000–4,000 Zulu; **1913 Natives Land Act** confined Blacks to 7% of land; Witwatersrand mine-compound system. *Leaders:* PM Louis Botha; Defence Minister Jan Smuts (invaded German Southwest and East Africa). *Hooks:* **Maritz Rebellion 1914** — OTL real and German-supported, Boer General Manie Maritz declared a new republic on the German Southwest border with German backing; arming ANC precursor (SANNC, founded 1912).

**31. BRITISH EGYPT.** Denshawai (see Britain). 1914 Protectorate declared, Khedive Abbas II Hilmi dismissed; martial law; forced labor of fellahin for Sinai/Palestine campaigns; cotton requisitioning. *Leaders:* Sir Eldon Gorst then Sir Reginald Wingate; Sultan Hussein Kamel (British puppet). *Hooks:* **Saad Zaghlul's Wafd Party** (which leads the 1919 Revolution); **Sanussi Brotherhood** German alliance in Western Desert (OTL: Germans/Ottomans supplied the Sanussi 1915–16).

**32. ANGLO-EGYPTIAN SUDAN.** Kitchener at **Omdurman 2 September 1898** killed ~10,800 Mahdists; desecrated the Mahdi's tomb. *Hooks:* Aid Mahdist remnants under the Khalifa's heir.

**33. FRENCH ALGERIA.** Conquest + Mokrani 1871 (~250,000 dead); the *indigénat* code; 1898 anti-Jewish riots in Algiers under mayor Max Régis; massive land confiscation for *colons*. *Hooks:* Emir Khaled leads a pro-German movement; Sahara Tuareg **Kaocen Ag Mohammed**'s 1916–17 revolt (OTL Senussi/Ottoman/German-supported).

**34. FRENCH MOROCCO.** Resident-General **Hubert Lyautey**'s conquest 1907–34; 1907 Casablanca bombardment killed ~1,500 Moroccans. *Hooks:* Sultan Abd al-Hafid (abdicated 1912) restoration plot.

**35. FRENCH WEST AFRICA.** Voulet-Chanoine 1898–99 (see France). Dakar-Niger Railway corvée; head tax; *Tirailleurs* conscription. *Hooks:* Samori Touré's sons; pan-African networks.

**36. BELGIAN CONGO (post-1908).** Force Publique brutality continued; Compagnie du Kasaï and Lever Brothers' Huileries du Congo Belge replaced rubber with palm-oil/copper coercion; Union Minière du Haut-Katanga began copper extraction 1906. *Hooks:* Continued forced-labor exposé.

**37. PORTUGUESE ANGOLA.** Already cited. Conquest of Cuanhama 1906 under Captain Alves Roçadas. *Hooks:* Cross-border German Southwest Africa alliance; Ovambo king **Mandume ya Ndemufayo**.

**38. PORTUGUESE MOZAMBIQUE.** Conquest of the Gaza Empire under **Mouzinho de Albuquerque** 1895 — King Ngungunhane paraded in Lisbon, died in exile 1906; **Wenela** recruitment for South African mines. *Hooks:* **Barue Rebellion 1917** (OTL brutally suppressed) — Germans could arm it.

### AMERICAS

**39. UNITED STATES.** *Sins:* **Philippine-American War 1899–1902** killed ~250,000–1,000,000 Filipinos; 1902 Senate inquiries documented water-cure torture; General Jacob H. Smith's "Kill everyone over ten" Samar order (court-martialed but only reprimanded). **US occupation of Haiti 28 July 1915–1934** under Wilson and Admiral William B. Caperton — disbanded the Haitian Senate April 1916, revived corvée labor 1917; **Charlemagne Péralte** killed by USMC sniper Sgt. Herman Hanneken on 1 November 1919, body strapped to a door as crucifix-mockery; James Weldon Johnson's 1920 *Nation* exposé documented USMC rapes and torture; ~11,500 Haitians killed. **Dominican Republic occupation 1916–24.** **Veracruz occupation April–November 1914** killed ~150 Mexicans. Wilson segregated federal civil service 1913 and praised *Birth of a Nation*; ~50–100 Black lynchings/year; Native American boarding schools; 1916 Bisbee Deportation; the Espionage Act 1917 jailed Eugene V. Debs. Per Britannica, **J.P. Morgan Jr.** "organized more than 2,000 banks to underwrite a total of more than $1,500,000,000 in Allied bonds" and as sole purchasing agent was "buying about $3,000,000,000 worth of military and other supplies" for the Entente. *Leaders:* Wilson; Sec State Bryan then Robert Lansing; J.P. Morgan Jr.; Pershing; Caperton; **USMC Major Smedley Butler** (later admitted *"I was a high-class muscle-man for Big Business"*). *Hooks:* Max in Mexico City; Black Tom munitions depot sabotage 30 July 1916 (OTL German agents destroyed a $20 million depot in NJ); German-American Bund organizing. *Capabilities:* Tiny pre-1917 army (~150,000); huge industrial base; Navy 2nd-largest; financial weapon paramount.

**40. MEXICO (plausible German ally).** *Sins:* **President-dictator Victoriano Huerta** seized power February 1913 by murdering Madero and VP Pino Suárez in the "Decena Trágica" coup with US Ambassador Henry Lane Wilson's connivance; **Pancho Villa's** January 1916 Santa Isabel Massacre (16–17 American mining engineers murdered on a train) and 9 March 1916 Columbus, NM raid (~17 Americans killed); Carranza's repression of Yaqui Indians in Sonora; **Cananea Massacre 1906** under Porfirio Díaz; Yucatán *henequen* slavery exposed by John Kenneth Turner's *Barbarous Mexico* (1910). *Leaders:* Huerta (alcoholic killer, expelled July 1914); Venustiano Carranza (austere wealthy estate-owner); Álvaro Obregón (military genius); Pancho Villa; Emiliano Zapata (assassinated 10 April 1919); **Felipe Ángeles** (Villa's French-trained artillery commander). *TGM hooks:* Max as German military advisor to Villa's Division del Norte (peak 50,000); Aetherium smuggling across the Rio Grande; Zapatista land-reform PR alliance. The real **Zimmermann Telegram (January 1917)** offered Mexico the return of Texas, New Mexico, and Arizona.

**41. CUBA (TGM Spanish colony).** Historic backstory: General **Valeriano Weyler** "the Butcher" Captain-General 1896–97 — **reconcentration camps 16 February 1896** killed 170,000 (Spanish 1898 census) to a higher estimate of 400,000 Cubans, under PM **Antonio Cánovas del Castillo** (assassinated 8 August 1897 by anarchist Michele Angiolillo). *Hooks:* Max in Havana stops US filibuster; meets descendants of Antonio Maceo's *Mambises*.

**42. BRAZIL.** *Sins:* **Canudos War 1896–97** — Colonel Moreira César's three failed expeditions, then General Artur Oscar destroyed the community of ~25,000 *sertanejo* followers of Antônio Conselheiro in October 1897 (throats slit); **Contestado War 1912–16** killed ~10,000 caboclo rebels; **Revolt of the Lash 22 November 1910** (*Revolta da Chibata*) — Afro-Brazilian sailor **João Cândido** led a mutiny against floggings on the new dreadnought *Minas Geraes*; Amazon rubber-boom debt-peonage. Joined the Allies October 1917. *Leaders:* President Wenceslau Brás (1914–18). *Hooks:* Afro-Brazilian organizing; Amazon Aetherium scramble; *Minas Geraes* dreadnought (twelve 12-inch guns) duels a German raider. *Capabilities:* Two Minas Geraes-class dreadnoughts from Armstrong/Vickers — at commissioning briefly the most powerful warships in the world.

**43. ARGENTINA.** *Sins:* **Conquest of the Desert 1878–85** under General **Julio Argentino Roca** — killed 1,000+ Mapuche, displaced/enslaved ~15,000, seized 225,000 sq mi (Wikipedia "Conquest of the Desert"); captured women and children given as servants to Buenos Aires elites; **Tragic Week January 1919** under President Hipólito Yrigoyen — police and right-wing paramilitaries killed ~700 workers and Jewish immigrants in Buenos Aires; **Patagonia rebellion 1920–22** killed ~1,500 anarchist workers under Colonel Héctor Varela. *Hooks:* German-Argentine community at Bariloche; British wool-trade extortion exposed. *Capabilities:* Two Rivadavia-class dreadnoughts (built USA); strongest South American army on paper.

**44. CHILE.** Saltpeter monopolist — **strategic for explosives**; in 1913 Chilean nitrate accounted for over 50% of world nitrogen production; Germany imported ~900,000 tons in 1912. **Fritz Haber** and **Carl Bosch's** Haber-Bosch process (lab demonstration 1909 Karlsruhe; first industrial plant BASF Oppau 1913; full scale Leuna 1917) broke the German dependency. *Sins:* **War of the Pacific 1879–84** (took Bolivian Antofagasta, landlocking Bolivia); **occupation of Araucanía 1861–83** under Cornelio Saavedra Rodríguez; **Santa María de Iquique school massacre 21 December 1907** — General Roberto Silva Renard ordered troops to fire on striking nitrate workers and their families sheltering in a school, killing ~2,000–3,600. *Hooks:* Cutting off Allied saltpeter is the major Atlantic strategic objective — Max sabotages Tarapacá nitrate works; Haber-Bosch industrial espionage. *Capabilities:* Modern Prussian-trained army; Almirante Latorre-class dreadnoughts (Britain bought *Latorre* as HMS *Canada* — fought at Jutland; *Cochrane* became HMS *Eagle* aircraft carrier).

**45. COLOMBIA AND VENEZUELA.** US theft of Panama 1903 under Theodore Roosevelt; Venezuelan dictator **Juan Vicente Gómez** (1908–35) granted Royal Dutch Shell and Standard Oil concessions from 1914; 1902–03 Anglo-German-Italian naval blockade of Venezuela for debts (Germany involved!). *Hooks:* Gómez plays Germany off the Allies for oil rights; Colombian Panama indemnity.

**46. CARIBBEAN COLONIES.** British Caribbean (Jamaica, Trinidad, Bahamas) — indenture, Morant Bay 1865 backdrop, rising Marcus Garvey. Dutch Caribbean — Royal Dutch Shell refinery at Curaçao from 1915. French Caribbean — Mt. Pelée 1902 mismanaged; Black Antillean conscription. Danish West Indies sold to USA 1917 for $25 million. *Hooks:* Garvey's early career; U-boat refueling at neutral Dutch ports; raid on British coaling stations.

**47. **49. ## Recommendations

**For TGM's writer, in priority order:**

1. **Anchor on the Big Five Colonial Villains.** Britain, France, Belgium, Italy and the United States together provide enough named atrocities, named villainous officials, and named victims/resistance leaders to drive 80% of the plot. The most dramatically loaded pairs are **Britain–India** (Dyer/O'Dwyer at Amritsar gives a clean named villain) and **Belgium–Congo** (Leopold II's mountain of contemporary documentation).

2. **Treat the Three Pashas as a moral test, not an alliance.** Don't have honorable-Germany simply ally with them. Have Max discover Aleppo death-march evidence in 1915 and report it via the real German consul Walter Rössler, forcing a public break with Talaat. This converts Germany's real OTL moral compromise (sheltering Talaat until 1921) into a story strength.

3. **Address the Herero genocide canonically before the story starts.** Set a TGM divergence in which Kaiser Wilhelm formally rebukes von Trotha after the December 1904 telegram (he historically did rescind the order under pressure), and prosecutes him post-1907. This earns the "honorable" badge without ignoring real history.

4. **Use Persia, Mexico, and Ireland as the three flagship German ally states.** Each has a real OTL German-allied movement — the Jangali of Mirza Kuchik Khan, Villa/Carranza via the Zimmermann telegram, and the Easter Rising — plus abundant righteousness from being colonized victims.

5. **Use the White Terror, not Imperial Russia, as the eastern villain.** Kolchak's executions and Denikin's pogroms are documented, modern, contemporary to the story — and don't require Max to fight the Kaiser's cousin. The Romanov rescue from Yekaterinburg is the single best alt-history plot lever available.

6. **Escalate antagonist scaling along the arc.** Open with colonial proxies (Force Publique, Tirailleurs Sénégalais, Indian Army) and only escalate to direct great-power confrontation in the late game (US entry, Japanese expansion). If Max needs a redemption arc for a foe, Italy (D'Annunzio decadence) or Argentina (genuinely peripheral) are the best candidates.

**Benchmarks that should change these recommendations:** If TGM goes long-running, swap in the lesser candidates (Abyssinia, Siam, Sweden) as supporting cast; if it stays Western-Front-focused, the Russian factions and Ottoman test can recede. If a sequel handles the post-war (1920+), promote Argentina's Patagonia rebellion, Egyptian 1919 Revolution, and Korean March First as flashpoints.

## Caveats

- **Casualty figures are widely contested.** Persian famine deaths (mainstream ~2 million vs. Majd's 8–10 million — the latter rests on a disputed population baseline); Romanian 1907 deaths (official 419 vs. western consensus 11,000 vs. French Embassy 10,000–20,000); Cuban reconcentration deaths (170,000 documented in the Spanish 1898 census vs. 400,000 high estimate); Congo population decline (5–10 million range, with Jan Vansina's later work walking back his own earlier 10-million claim); Amritsar deaths (379 official vs. 1,000+ Indian estimate). When you use a number, the lower documented figure is more defensible academically; the higher figure is more dramatic. Pick deliberately.

- **Some atrocities listed involve OTL events Germany also committed.** The Herero-Nama genocide, Maji Maji suppression, the Cameroon plantation system, and Force Publique-style coercion in some German African colonies all need explicit handling. The TGM "honorable Germany" premise requires either pre-story divergence (a reformist Wilhelm) or in-story reckoning (a Reichstag scandal Max can reference) — pick one and commit.

- **"Kerensky communists" is anachronistic.** Kerensky was a Socialist Revolutionary, not a communist, and the Provisional Government was liberal-SR. If TGM intends the Bolsheviks, use Lenin/Trotsky/Dzerzhinsky; if it intends the moderate-left, use Kerensky's June 1917 offensive and the failed alliance with Kornilov.

- **Real Imperial Germany was the United States' covert adversary in Latin American intelligence games** (the Zimmermann telegram, the Black Tom bombing, Mexican arms shipments). These are reusable as Max's missions; but historical US neutrality 1914–17 means major US-German direct confrontation belongs to the late game.

- **Year-check your leaders carefully.** Kitchener died at sea aboard HMS *Hampshire* in June 1916 (backstory only after that); Joffre was sidelined late 1916; Cromer left Egypt 1907; Leopold II died 1909 (so his Congo atrocities are villainous *backstory*, not living-villain material for 1914+ scenes). Cross-reference every leader's name against the year of the scene.

- **Roger Casement is the cross-cutting hero of the colonial atrocity record** (exposed both the Congo Free State 1903–04 and the Putumayo 1910–12) — and was hanged for German gun-running on 3 August 1916. A "Casement parallel" or even a "Max meets Casement before the *Aud* mission" scene unifies multiple atrocity threads into one personality.

- **The Putumayo (Peruvian Amazon Company / Julio César Arana) atrocities** are not assigned a country profile above because Peru was peripheral in WWI and the company was London-registered — but they are an outstanding *embedded* horror Max can investigate inside any Latin American arc, and they thematically marry to the Congo (same Casement, same rubber-quota system, same hand-mutilations).
