## Core Mechanics

Stock wealth = collective belief. Crashes destroy paper value, not physical money. Banks fail via fractional reserve collapse. Short selling: borrow shares, sell high, buy low, return shares, keep spread. Leverage: 10:1 stable markets, 3 to 5:1 crisis. Broker failures transfer positions or void obligations.
