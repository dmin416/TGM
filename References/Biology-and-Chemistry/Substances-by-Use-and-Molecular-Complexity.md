# Substances by Use and Molecular Complexity

## Direct Lethal Toxins

### Extremely Simple (2-4 atoms)
| Substance | Formula | Atoms | Mechanism | LD₅₀ (human) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon monoxide | CO | 2 | Blocks oxygen transport | ~1000 ppm/min |
| Hydrogen cyanide | HCN | 3 | Blocks cytochrome oxidase | ~50-100 mg |
| Hydrogen sulfide | H₂S | 3 | Blocks electron transport | ~100 ppm/min |
| Phosgene | COCl₂ | 4 | Causes pulmonary edema (WWI) | ~300 ppm/min |

## Key Observations

### Complexity ≠ Danger
- **Simplest lethal**: Carbon monoxide (2 atoms) — blocks oxygen transport
- **Most complex lethal**: Botulinum toxin (150,000 atoms) — most toxic substance known
- **No correlation between molecular size and lethality**

### Therapeutic vs. Toxic: Often the Same Molecule
- **Morphine (40 atoms)**: Life-saving pain relief at proper dose, lethal addiction risk
- **Fentanyl (53 atoms)**: Essential surgical anesthetic, street epidemic killer
- **Insulin (788 atoms)**: Life-saving for diabetics, lethal in overdose
- **Prednisone (52 atoms)**: Saves lives in inflammation, destroys immune system long-term
- **The difference is dosage and context**

### Modern Drug Trends
**Legitimate medicines getting simpler:**
- Metformin (20 atoms) — most prescribed diabetes drug
- Acetaminophen (20 atoms) — most common pain reliever
- Gabapentin (29 atoms) — nerve pain treatment

**Illicit drugs getting deadlier and more complex:**
- Fentanyl (53 atoms) — 50-100× morphine potency
- Carfentanil (59 atoms) — 10,000× morphine potency (elephant tranquilizer)
- Synthetic cannabinoids (300-400 atoms) — unpredictable, seizure-inducing

### Mechanism Matters Most
Toxicity depends on **what the molecule disrupts**, not on complexity:
- **Oxygen transport blockage**: CO, cyanide (simple)
- **Nerve signal disruption**: Sarin, VX, tetrodotoxin, fentanyl (moderate to complex)
- **Cell wall disruption**: Penicillin (moderate), Ricin (complex)
- **Protein synthesis disruption**: Tetracyclines, Ricin, Botulinum (moderate to complex)
- **Metabolic hijacking**: Methamphetamine, cocaine (simple to moderate)

### Enantiomers (Mirror Molecules)
Same atoms, different 3D arrangement = completely different biological effect
- **Thalidomide**: Safe sedative vs. teratogenic (causes birth defects)
- **Limonene**: Orange scent vs. lemon scent
- **Methamphetamine**: Decongestant (legal Vicks inhaler) vs. recreational stimulant (illegal)

### Vitamins: Essential but Complex
- **Simplest**: Vitamin C (20 atoms) — antioxidant
- **Most complex**: Vitamin B12 (181 atoms) — requires cobalt, nerve function
- **Fat-soluble**: A, D, E, K — stored in body, can accumulate to toxicity
- **Water-soluble**: B, C — excreted daily, need regular intake

### Antibiotics: The Goldilocks Zone
- **Too simple** (like cyanide at 3 atoms): Would kill everything
- **Too complex** (like proteins at 10,000+ atoms): Can't mass-produce cheaply
- **Just right** (28-79 atoms): Selective targeting, manufacturable
  - Sulfonamides (28), Penicillin (41), Tetracyclines (56), Streptomycin (79)

### D&D Application
**Poison Spray (1d12 damage) could theoretically use**:
- Simple toxins: Cyanide, phosgene (3-4 atoms)
- Moderate toxins: VX, tetrodotoxin (180-300 atoms)
- Complex toxins: Ricin (9,000 atoms)
- Dispersed as **1-5 mg active toxin** across **50-500g carrier mist**
- Requires Constitution save = body's cellular resistance to mechanism

**Cure Wounds / Lesser Restoration equivalent**:
- Penicillin (41 atoms, 300-600 mg) prevents bacterial infection cascade
- Insulin (788 atoms, tiny doses) regulates glucose to prevent damage
- Antivenom (protein complexes, millions of atoms) neutralizes venom
- **Prevention ≠ healing**: Antibiotics stop future damage, not repair existing wounds
