## Mechanistic Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1: Biological Energy Storage Crystal

Elvish craft binds energy into molecular structures that do not exist naturally, a biological battery. The bread form is a delivery matrix. The actual energy source is encoded into the grain during elvish cultivation and baking at an ontological level separate from chemistry entirely.

### Hypothesis 2: Metabolic Amplification

Lembas contains compounds that trigger hyper efficient ATP production. The body extracts far more energy per gram than normal metabolic pathways allow. Bypasses the ~60% heat loss inefficiency of normal human digestion. Interfaces directly with mitochondrial function.

### Hypothesis 3: Slow Release Enchanted Matrix + Fat Mobilization

Provides real compressed calories plus:

- Magically modified slow release carbohydrate: flat blood sugar for ~20–24 hours, no spike or crash
- Hunger signal suppression
- Optimized endogenous fat mobilization: body burns stored reserves efficiently alongside lembas calories
- Fatigue neurotransmitter preservation

### Compression Mechanism

The 4× compressed form does not alter caloric content or mass. It reduces volume only, implying the energy storage lattice can be physically compacted without degrading the delivery matrix. Analogous to compressing a crystalline structure: same atoms, smaller space. Uncompressed form may simply be the consumption optimized shape (easier to bite, faster to dissolve in saliva).

**Most likely canonical answer:** Hypotheses 2 + 3 combined: magically dense real calories plus elvish metabolic optimization and slow release delivery.
