## Glucose Syrup as Feedstock

- Bio derived alternative to petroleum for chemicals
- Already depolymerized and ready for processing
- Routes to plastics, acids, alcohols, solvents via fermentation
- More expensive than petroleum, heavily oxygenated so poor for pure hydrocarbon targets
- Better than liquid coal for chemicals, worse for fuels

---

## Liquid Coal vs Glucose in a No Petroleum World

- Coal better for fuels, roads, bulk hydrocarbons
- Glucose better for plastics, fibers, pharmaceuticals
- Both needed together to cover everything petroleum currently does

## Lubricants

- Base oils from petroleum currently
- Alternatives: coal to liquids, plant esters, animal fats
- Coal to liquids best for engines
- Plant oils fine for slow cool machinery
- Jojoba naturally makes wax esters closest to synthetic lubricant
- Castor oil best natural plant lubricant

---

## Can Plants Make Synthetic Lubricants

- Yes with engineering
- Redirect fatty acid pathways
- Get plants making wax esters instead of triglycerides
- Matches decent mineral oil quality
- One or two refining steps still needed afterward

## Magical Plants Making Hydrogen and Ethanol

- Ethanol barely magical; plants almost do it already
- Redirect sugar metabolism and suppress breakdown enzymes
- Hydrogen genuinely hard even with magic
- Plants already split water in photosynthesis
- Hydrogenase enzyme poisoned by own oxygen output
- Storing gas in tissue structurally difficult
- Nature never solved hydrogen storage in ~4 billion years

## Mangroves and Water Purification

- Salt excluding species filter up to ~90% of salt at root membrane
- Other species excrete salt through leaves
- Third strategy dumps salt in falling leaves
- Not directly harvestable as fresh water source
- Excellent biological contaminant filters
- Root membrane chemistry inspires industrial desalination membranes
- Theoretical three layers for pure water but impractical at natural scale

---

## Magical Enhanced Mangroves

- -20 MPa osmotic pressure vs seawater's -2.7 MPa
- Near total salt exclusion in single pass
- +2 MPa output pressure exceeds municipal tap water
- One layer sufficient for pure water
- 14× robustness handles enormous internal pressures
- Essentially a living solar powered desalination and pumping station
- Still limited to solar energy input
- Slow trickle not a firehose; maybe drinking water for one or two people per large tree

## Belt Thickener / Gravity Drainage

- Liquid sludge poured onto moving porous belt
- Free water drains through fabric under gravity alone
- Incoming sludge 95 to 99% water
- After belt roughly 85 to 92% water
- Looks like flash flood slowing down visibly
- Free water leaves fast, bound water stays longer
- Stack with filter press, then centrifuge, then heat for progressively drier output
- Last 20% of water costs as much energy as first 80%

## Uses of Sewage Sludge

- Agricultural fertilizer most common globally
- Composting with wood chips
- Land reclamation on degraded sites
- Biogas production via anaerobic digestion
- Dried into commercial fertilizer pellets
- Controversial due to pharmaceuticals, heavy metals, microplastics, forever chemicals

## Waste Treatment in 1913

- Major cities had sewer networks, screening, sedimentation, trickling filters
- Effluent dumped to rivers with minimal treatment
- Sludge spread raw on fields or sold as fertilizer
- Chlorination just beginning around 1910 to 1915
- Activated sludge process invented literally 1913 to 1914
- Small towns essentially nothing: cesspits and outhouses
- Typhoid and cholera still genuine threats

## Germany 1913 Waste Treatment

- Berlin among world's best sewer systems
- Rieselfelder: massive sewage irrigation fields outside Berlin
- Sewage pumped to agricultural land, filtered through soil, crops grown on top
- Strong municipal government funded infrastructure properly
- Imhoff tanks combining sedimentation and digestion being built across Germany
- Karl Imhoff was German; cutting edge technology right at 1913
- Major cities world class; rural villages essentially medieval

## Methane First, Then Fertilizer

- Lose some carbon as methane and CO₂
- Lose maybe 10 to 15% nitrogen as ammonia gas
- Keep essentially all phosphorus
- Keep essentially all potassium
- Keep most nitrogen in more plant available ammonium form
- Keep all micronutrients and minerals
- Digested sludge arguably better fertilizer than raw
- Free energy out, fertilizer quality maintained, volume reduced

---

## Anaerobic Digestion Process

- Four bacterial groups working in sequence
- Hydrolysis bacteria break complex molecules down
- Acidogenic bacteria produce organic acids and alcohols
- Acetogenic bacteria produce acetic acid and hydrogen
- Methanogenic archaea produce methane and CO₂
- Needs no oxygen, correct temperature, neutral pH
- Happens naturally in swamps, lake bottoms, animal guts
- Imhoff tanks were German innovation right at 1913 timeframe

## Max Experiments: Sewage, Nitrogen Chain, and Synthetic Oil Plant

Planning note for Maximilian Friedrich engineered biology and municipal chemistry. Not story canon until cited.

### Sewage to useful products

Raw sewage screened, settled, fats skimmed, liquids biologically treated, solids digested anaerobically producing methane. Clean water, fertilizer, and biogas come out. Magical plants and bacteria make it faster and more complete.

### Nitrogen chain

Atmosphere → ammonia via nitrogen fixing bacteria → nitric acid via nitrifying bacteria → explosives or chemical reagents. Haber Bosch industrialized this in 1913, giving Germany unlimited explosives and fertilizer simultaneously.

### Magical plant for synthetic oil

Plant fixes nitrogen, makes ammonia internally, converts to nitric acid in sealed acid resistant nodules, uses acid chemistry plus biological hydrogen carriers to strip oxygen from glucose, assembles resulting carbon chains into hydrocarbon oil. Basically an algae oil producer on overdrive with an internal chemical reactor.

### Acid sources

Plants make weak acids naturally. Sulfur bacteria make sulfuric acid. Nitrifying bacteria make nitric acid. Both can be engineered into root nodules giving a plant internal acid production without self destruction.

### Ammonia sources

Nitrogen fixing bacteria in root nodules, free living soil bacteria, cyanobacteria, decomposition of protein, lightning, industrially via Haber Bosch. The enzyme nitrogenase does the actual work and hates oxygen.

### Core logic connecting everything

Sunlight powers the plant. Plant fixes atmospheric nitrogen. Nitrogen becomes ammonia. Ammonia becomes nitric acid. Acid strips oxygen from glucose. Deoxygenated carbon chains become oil. Waste goes to digester making methane. Nothing wasted.
