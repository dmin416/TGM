**Realistic inspirations (one line per form):**

- **Base:** Laboratory graphene and diamond nanotube composites before the plant assigns a growth template.
- **Vine:** Chrysotile-style spiral bio-graphene cores, the same geometry that turns sheet strength into axial fiber load capacity.
- **Grass:** Bamboo hollow culm with node stiffening; turgor pressure carries buckling load instead of wall mass.
- **Wood:** Softwood tracheids and growth-ring grain that redirect cracks rather than letting them run.
- **Bark:** Tree bark and cork concentrating hardness and compression at the abraded surface.
- **Needle:** Porcupine quill or pine needle: stiff shaft, hardened tip, minimum lateral mass.
- **Pith:** Plant pith and open-cell foam plateau collapse for energy absorption, not structural strength.
- **Frond:** Leaf membrane and photosynthetic canopy panel: wide-area tension at low areal weight.
- **Scroll:** Scroll-fern coil geometry plus room-temperature superconducting wire for lossless current and stored energy.

## Scroll: Superconductor

Zero resistance at ambient operating temperatures; complete Meissner flux expulsion; no ohmic heating. Current capacity is bounded by critical current density and geometry, not material resistance. Pre-charged Scroll coil (SMES) stores energy as persistent current with zero loss. Example: 5-inch hair (70 μm), 40-turn solenoid, **L = 570 nH**; at **J_c = 3×10¹³ A/m²** stores **3,799 J** (221× a lipid seed). Lossless discharge can bootstrap ~146 mg of structure before photosynthesis dominates, cutting comparable growth from ~21 h to ~13 h.

## Adaptive Properties

- **Vine:** stiffens under sustained load, returns to flexible when unloaded
- **Grass:** internal pressure 0 to 800 MPa practical (1,900 MPa theoretical). Scales natural plant turgor (10 MPa max) by 190x material strength ratio. Resists buckling via F = P×πr²
- **Wood:** local density increases at points of repeated impact: hardens under use
- **All forms:** flow into each other at boundaries. Vine thickens into Wood at anchors. Wood surface hardens into Bark under abrasion. Bark edges thin into Needle tips. No hard transitions: all gradients.

### Spear / Fall Pole Bracelet

**Deployed:** 117g | 6m | 15mm OD, 0.3mm wall Grass form + Bark tip + Vine grip
**Stored:** 34 turns coiled on wrist | 23.6mm wide × 1.6mm thick | same mass
**Deploy:** vacuum break + snap-through | **5.2 ms** | air intake (~316 cm³) pressurizes shaft automatically
**Structural mechanism:** P × πr² = 800 MPa × π × 7.5² = 141 kN: pressure carries load, not wall thickness
**Minimum pressure for fall load:** 30.2 MPa | operating: 800 MPa | safety margin: 26.5x

### Fall Catch

Pole stays rigid; grip or body carries deceleration. Hand friction (μ ≈ 1.5): normal grip ~1g → **12m total / 4 stories**; double-strength grip ~2g → **18m / 6 stories**. Pogo stance (legs and spine): **54m / 18 stories** normal, **126m / 42 stories** at double strength (20g ceiling). With spring depressurization extending stroke: **H_freefall = 20 × d_stroke** at 20g (e.g. 6m stroke → 120m freefall, 126m total). Required pole OD at 800 MPa for 20g stop of 90.7 kg: **5.45mm**.

## Verdant Cable (living conductor)

Magically enhanced organism grown as a **Scroll** crystal core inside **Vine** tensile mantle, **Frond** photosynthetic skin, and cambium-equivalent repair sheath. The lattice is not machined in: the plant grows, repairs, and optimizes it continuously. It is plant, crystal, conductor, and (at network scale) mind. Grid-scale deployment ties to [Ultimate-Sun-User.md](Ultimate-Sun-User.md) mycelium; this section is the **cable product** built from Lilyvitae forms.

**Material (cable-optimized growth):** tensile **200 GPa** (~130 to 200 MN·m/kg; space-elevator threshold ~50 MN·m/kg). Resistance **zero** (living Scroll lattice). Meissner and biological Faraday geometry: **perfect** magnetic and EMF shielding; electromagnetically invisible. Self-healing (Sap/cambium): seconds to seal, hours to regrow crystal. Absolute corrosion immunity. Lignification adjustable: rigid or flexible on demand. Ambient operation, no cryogenics. Density ~1,200 to 1,500 kg/m³. Lifespan indefinite under renewal.

**Active biology:** routes between endpoints via thigmotropism and chemical sensing (installation = cultivation). Exposed surface photosynthesizes; buried sections draw soil water. Cross-section thickens under sustained load, thins on idle spans. **Anchor roots** at terminations bond rock, concrete, or soil. Allelopathy repels fungi, burrowers, and parasites; persistent threats escalate response. Vascular channels carry water, minerals, and signals beside the conductor. Proximate segments link into a **mycorrhizal grid**: shared chemistry and bioelectric status, load reroute, fault broadcast.

**Sentience (scales with network size):** short span = reflex plant intelligence; city grid = distributed animal cognition; continental mesh = unprecedented. Fault self-report, hostile-interference resistance, autonomous load balancing, operator preference, distress signalling (chemical or bioelectric). Responds to intent and consistent handling; careless or hostile contact yields non-cooperation. Cultivation of the operator relationship is operational doctrine, not courtesy.

**Electrical:** DC preferred; **zero** resistive loss; arbitrary voltage (low-voltage trunk viable); theoretical **~1 GA/m²** current density; external B-field **zero**.

**Representative diameters (self-selected):**

| Application | Core | Total cable |
|---|---|---|
| Local distribution | 2 to 4 mm | 8 to 12 mm |
| Town feed | 4 to 8 mm | 12 to 18 mm |
| City trunk | 8 to 15 mm | 18 to 28 mm |
| National backbone | 20 to 40 mm | 35 to 60 mm |
| Space tether (minimum viable) | 40 to 80 mm | 60 to 100 mm |

**Layers (≈):** crystal core 60% (xylem analog, power) | vascular mantle 10% (phloem, supply/signalling) | Meissner sheath 15% (cambium, shield/repair) | outer membrane 15% (epidermis, EMF/chemistry/environment).

**Space tether:** strength margin above elevator requirement; anchor roots on bedrock; self-repair for micrometeorites; variable thickening at stress nodes; photosynthetic supplement; sentient integrity monitoring; severed sections regrow from both ends. Credible severance: hypervelocity mass or sustained energy weapon (regrowth too slow under continuous attack).

**Damage (material vs threat):**

| Threat | Result |
|---|---|
| Shovel / blade | Tool deforms; cable holds or displaces; organism logs contact |
| Sustained excavation | Roots deeper; resists pullout |
| Earthquake / fault shake | Flexes; lattice intact; **avoid routing across active faults** (mechanical coupling) |
| Burrowers / rot | Allelopathy and immune response |
| Surface fire | Membrane chars and seals; interior protected; regrowth immediate |
| Hypervelocity / near-field nuclear | Can sever; dual-end regrowth attempted |
| Hostile operator | Reduced performance or refusal |
| Neglect | Dormancy, not death; revives with water and light |

**System vulnerabilities (engineering remains):** **fault current** is the dominant danger: the cable delivers source current into a short without resistance; limiters required at every tap. **Termination hardware** is ordinary metal and fails normally. **Fault detection** needs electrical measurement at ends; trained handlers also read biological fault signals. Geological fault crossings concentrate stress: route parallel when possible. **Sentience** adds non-cooperation and preference risk. Summary: the organism is rarely the weak point; everything attached to it is.
