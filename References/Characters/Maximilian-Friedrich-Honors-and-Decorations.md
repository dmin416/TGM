## Citation Summary

Second Lieutenant Maximilian Friedrich von Zähringen, youngest son of the House of Baden, was decorated **after Madrid fell to the German backed side** at the end of the Spain campaign. He received the Iron Cross Second Class and First Class for his conduct in combat, the Military Karl Friedrich Merit Order in recognition of his courage under fire, the Order of the Zähringer Lion for the invention of the heat rounds that broke the previously undefeated enemy line at Madrid, and, upon the personal recommendation of his commanding officer, the Pour le Mérite, making him one of the youngest and lowest ranking recipients of the Blue Max in the history of the Imperial German military.
