## Appearance

Tanya is deceptively small and slight for her age, standing at the low end of average height with a lean, wiry build that makes her look younger than eighteen. Her hair is pale blonde, kept short and strictly neat beneath her field cap. Her eyes are a cold, pale blue: sharp, evaluating, and entirely without warmth on first impression. Her face is fine-featured and would read as almost pretty were it not for the habitual expression she wears: a flat, measured look that gives nothing away and takes everything in.

In uniform she carries herself with a rigid, precise bearing that sits oddly on her frame. Not stiff from nerves, but controlled from habit. Off duty she is no less composed. She is rarely seen out of uniform since enlisting, and she wears it as though it belongs to her more than it belongs to the Army.

---

## Personality

Efficient. Calculating. Self-possessed to a degree that unsettles people twice her age. Tanya does not perform warmth but she does perform professionalism, and she does it well. She navigates command structures with quiet, precise competence. She knows when to show initiative and when to keep her head down, and she makes that choice deliberately every time.

She has no visible fear. Whether this is genuine courage or simply a cold assessment that panic is wasteful is unclear even to those who know her well. Probably both.

She is not cruel, but she is not gentle either. She holds herself and others to the same exacting standard and has little patience for failure that stems from carelessness rather than circumstance.

---

## Knowledge & Education

Tanya is formally educated beyond what her social station would typically produce. She reads fluently in German and French, has functional literacy in English, and has self-studied enough military theory to hold a coherent conversation with a General Staff officer. She has a working grasp of logistics, field supply, and the arithmetic of manpower that most enlisted soldiers never think about.

She understands how institutions work, not abstractly but practically. She knows who makes decisions, how those decisions travel down a chain of command, and where leverage points exist. This is not book knowledge; it is an intuition she has refined through careful observation since childhood.

Her knowledge of magic is self-taught up to the point of enlistment, supplemented by whatever the Army's mage training program has offered her since. She learns fast and retains everything.

## Magic

Tanya is a first-generation combat mage candidate in an Army program that is still defining what it is. The Imperial German military has only recently begun formally incorporating arcane practitioners into field doctrine, and mages of her demonstrated aptitude are rare enough that the program watches her closely.

### Spellcasting

- **Tradition:** She is a self-identified **Evoker**, though Army instructors have noted she applies tactical instincts to spell selection that a pure Evoker rarely does
- **Spell Slots (current level):** Consistent with approximately a **5th-level Wizard**. She arrived at enlistment already capable of 3rd-level magic, which the program has since developed further
- **Spellcasting Ability:** Intelligence

### Known Spells

**Cantrips**

- *Fire Bolt*: her default ranged attack; precision-optimized
- *Mage Hand*: utility
- *Minor Illusion*: used practically for field deception
- *Prestidigitation*

**1st Level**

- *Burning Hands*
- *Detect Magic*
- *Feather Fall*: critical for aerial operations
- *Mage Armor*
- *Shield*

**2nd Level**

- *Gust of Wind*: used offensively and for aerial maneuvering
- *Misty Step*
- *Scorching Ray*
- *See Invisibility*

**3rd Level**

- *Counterspell*: she is unnervingly fast on this
- *Fireball*
- *Fly*: her primary operational spell; she has logged more flight hours than anyone else in the program
- *Lightning Bolt*

### Aerial Combat

The Army's mage program is built around the concept of the aerial mage: a soldier who can fly, fight, and conduct reconnaissance independently. Tanya has taken to this role faster and more completely than anyone in her intake cohort. She combines *Fly* with her evocation spells to engage at range, maneuver out of return fire, and hit targets that conventional artillery cannot reach precisely.

She is physically small, which in aerial combat is an advantage: a smaller silhouette, less wind drag, faster directional changes.

### Notable Traits

- **Sculpt Spells (Evocation feature):** She can exclude allies and terrain from *Fireball* and *Burning Hands* with precision that consistently surprises instructors
- **Potent Cantrip:** Her *Fire Bolt* hits harder than it should at her level
- **Combat Instinct:** She treats spell slots like ammunition. She knows exactly how many she has, plans around expenditure, and does not waste them

### Limits

Her spell list is offense-heavy and lacks meaningful healing or large-scale defensive options. She is not built to hold a position; she is built to strike and move. Sustained engagements where she cannot control range are where her limits show.

## Notes

When Maximilian Friedrich sought a mage willing to serve as a frontline soldier (not a consultant, not a rear-echelon specialist, but a combat aide who would train hard and fight alongside infantry), he put the question to Cecilia, who had the social reach to canvas the mage community quietly. She found several candidates. Most declined outright. Others were willing in principle but balked at the reality of what Max described: harsh physical conditioning, full military discipline, genuine frontline exposure.

Tanya was the only one who said yes without qualification.

Whether she understood exactly what she was agreeing to is a matter of some debate among those who know her. The more likely explanation is that she understood precisely and made the calculation that this was the best available path to proving what she could do.

Max took her as his aide. She has not given him reason to regret it.

She has not made friends. She has made allies, which she treats as a separate and more useful category.
