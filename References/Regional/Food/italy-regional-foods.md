# Italy — Regional Foods and Drinks

## Tuscany

### Foods
1. Bistecca alla fiorentina (T-bone steak, rare, olive oil)
2. Ribollita (bread and vegetable soup)
3. Pappa al pomodoro (tomato and bread soup)
4. Panzanella (bread salad with tomatoes, cucumbers)
5. Crostini toscani (chicken liver pâté on toast)
6. Pappardelle al cinghiale (wide pasta with wild boar)
7. Cantucci (almond biscotti with vin santo)
8. Schiacciata (flatbread with olive oil, salt)
9. Pecorino toscano (sheep cheese, firm)

### Drinks
- Chianti wine (Sangiovese red, DOCG)
- Brunello di Montalcino (powerful red wine)
- Vin Santo (sweet dessert wine)

## Lombardy

### Foods
1. Risotto alla milanese (saffron risotto)
2. Ossobuco (braised veal shanks with gremolata)
3. Cotoletta alla milanese (breaded veal cutlet)
4. Pizzoccheri (buckwheat pasta with cabbage, cheese)
5. Polenta (cornmeal porridge, various preparations)
6. Cassoeula (pork and cabbage stew)
7. Panettone (sweet bread with candied fruit, Christmas)
8. Gorgonzola cheese (creamy blue cheese)
9. Bresaola (air-dried salted beef, Valtellina)
10. Tortelli di zucca (pumpkin-filled pasta)

### Drinks
- Franciacorta (sparkling wine, DOCG)
- Valtellina red wine (Nebbiolo, mountain terraces)
- Campari (bitter aperitif, invented Milan)

## Veneto

### Foods
1. Risotto al nero di seppia (squid ink risotto)
2. Sarde in saor (sweet and sour sardines)
3. Fegato alla veneziana (liver with onions)
4. Baccalà mantecato (whipped salt cod)
5. Risi e bisi (rice and peas)
6. Polenta e schie (polenta with tiny shrimp)
7. Tiramisù (coffee-soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone)
8. Bigoli in salsa (thick pasta with anchovy sauce)
9. Radicchio di Treviso (bitter red chicory)
10. Pandoro (sweet bread, star-shaped, Christmas)

### Drinks
- Prosecco (sparkling wine, Valdobbiadene)
- Valpolicella (red wine, Amarone style)
- Spritz (Aperol or Campari with prosecco, soda)

## Sicily

### Foods
1. Arancini (fried rice balls with ragù, cheese)
2. Pasta alla Norma (eggplant, tomato, ricotta salata)
3. Caponata (sweet and sour eggplant relish)
4. Cannoli (fried pastry tubes with ricotta filling)
5. Panelle (chickpea fritters)
6. Pasta con le sarde (pasta with sardines, fennel)
7. Cassata (sponge cake with ricotta, marzipan)
8. Swordfish (grilled or in pasta)
9. Granita (semi-frozen dessert, almond or lemon)
10. Sfincione (Sicilian thick-crust pizza)

### Drinks
- Nero d'Avola (Sicilian red wine)
- Marsala (fortified wine, sweet or dry)
- Limoncello (lemon liqueur)

## Campania

### Foods
1. Pizza Margherita (tomato, mozzarella, basil, Naples)
2. Spaghetti alle vongole (pasta with clams)
3. Mozzarella di bufala (buffalo milk cheese, Campania)
4. Sfogliatella (layered pastry with ricotta)
5. Parmigiana di melanzane (eggplant, tomato, cheese layers)
6. Insalata caprese (tomato, mozzarella, basil)
7. Pasta e fagioli (pasta and bean soup)
8. Babà (rum-soaked sponge cake)
9. Limoncello (lemon liqueur, Amalfi coast)
10. Paccheri (large tube pasta with seafood)

### Drinks
- Aglianico wine (Taurasi red, powerful)
- Lacryma Christi (wine from Vesuvius slopes)
- Limoncello (sweet lemon liqueur)

## Piedmont

### Foods
1. Tajarin (thin egg pasta with butter, truffles)
2. Brasato al Barolo (beef braised in Barolo wine)
3. Vitello tonnato (cold veal with tuna sauce)
4. Bagna cauda (hot anchovy garlic dip)
5. Agnolotti del plin (meat-filled pasta, pinched)
6. Gianduiotto (chocolate hazelnut candy)
7. Tartufo bianco (white truffle, Alba)
8. Bollito misto (mixed boiled meats)
9. Panissa (risotto with beans, salami)
10. Grissini (thin breadsticks, Turin)

### Drinks
- Barolo wine (Nebbiolo red, king of wines)
- Barbaresco wine (elegant Nebbiolo)
- Vermouth (Turin, sweet and dry)

## Lazio

### Foods
1. Carbonara (pasta with guanciale, egg, pecorino)
2. Amatriciana (pasta with guanciale, tomato, pecorino)
3. Cacio e pepe (pasta with pecorino, black pepper)
4. Saltimbocca alla romana (veal with prosciutto, sage)
5. Carciofi alla giudia (fried artichokes, Jewish style)
6. Supplì (fried rice balls with mozzarella)
7. Porchetta (roast pork with herbs)
8. Maritozzo (sweet bun with whipped cream)
9. Pecorino romano (sheep cheese, sharp)
10. Abbacchio (roast milk-fed lamb)

### Drinks
- Frascati (white wine, Castelli Romani)
- Est! Est!! Est!!! (white wine, Montefiascone)
- Sambuca (anise liqueur)

## Emilia-Romagna

### Foods
1. Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese (aged hard cheese, Parma)
2. Prosciutto di Parma (dry-cured ham)
3. Tagliatelle al ragù (pasta with meat sauce, Bologna)
4. Tortellini in brodo (stuffed pasta in broth)
5. Lasagne alla bolognese (layered pasta, ragù, béchamel)
6. Cotechino (pork sausage, served with lentils)
7. Mortadella (large pork sausage with pistachios)
8. Aceto balsamico (balsamic vinegar, Modena)
9. Piadina (flatbread, Romagna)
10. Zampone (stuffed pig's trotter)

### Drinks
- Lambrusco (sparkling red wine, Modena)
- Sangiovese (red wine, Romagna)
- Nocino (walnut liqueur)

## Liguria

### Foods
1. Pesto alla genovese (basil, pine nuts, garlic, cheese)
2. Focaccia (flatbread with olive oil, salt)
3. Trofie al pesto (twisted pasta with pesto)
4. Farinata (chickpea flour flatbread)
5. Pansotti (pasta filled with greens, walnut sauce)
6. Cappon magro (layered seafood and vegetable salad)
7. Acciughe sotto sale (salt-cured anchovies)
8. Torta pasqualina (Easter vegetable pie)
9. Pandolce (sweet bread with candied fruit)
10. Sciacchetrà (sweet wine, Cinque Terre)

### Drinks
- Vermentino (white wine, coastal)
- Pigato (aromatic white wine)
- Rossese di Dolceacqua (red wine)

## Puglia

### Foods
1. Orecchiette con cime di rapa (ear-shaped pasta with turnip greens)
2. Burrata (cream-filled mozzarella)
3. Focaccia barese (flatbread with tomatoes, olives)
4. Tiella (rice, mussels, potato bake)
5. Bombette (meat rolls with cheese, grilled)
6. Panzerotti (fried calzone with mozzarella, tomato)
7. Taralli (ring-shaped crackers, fennel or pepper)
8. Pasticciotto (custard-filled pastry)
9. Caciocavallo (stretched curd cheese, aged)
10. Friselle (twice-baked bread, soaked)

### Drinks
- Primitivo (red wine, rich and fruity)
- Negroamaro (red wine, dark and tannic)
- Rosé del Salento (rosé wine)
