# Russia — Regional Foods and Drinks

## Siberia

### Foods
1. Pelmeni (meat dumplings, served with sour cream)
2. Omul fish (smoked Lake Baikal whitefish)
3. Stroganina (frozen raw fish or meat, shaved thin)
4. Borsch (beet soup with meat, cabbage)
5. Shangi (open-faced potato or berry pies)
6. Pozharsky cutlet (breaded chicken cutlet)
7. Siberian salmon (fresh, smoked, or grilled)
8. Pine nut dishes (salads, desserts, roasted)
9. Venison stew (wild game with root vegetables)
10. Cloudberry jam (rare Arctic berry preserve)

### Drinks
- Taiga tea (wild herbs, berries, Ivan-chai)
- Sbiten (hot honey drink with spices)
- Siberian vodka (crystal-clear, strong)

## Ural Mountains

### Foods
1. Kurnik (layered meat and rice pie)
2. Pelmeni (Ural-style with three meats)
3. Shchi (cabbage soup with meat)
4. Ural fish pie (river fish in pastry)
5. Kystyby (flatbread with mashed potato filling)
6. Kulebyaka (layered fish, rice, egg pie)
7. Kvass soup (cold soup with kvass, vegetables)
8. Wild mushroom dishes (porcini, chanterelles)
9. Ural honey cake (medovik layers)
10. Ural berry kissel (thickened fruit drink)

### Drinks
- Ural kvass (fermented rye bread drink)
- Mountain herb tea (wild-gathered)
- Ural vodka (regional distilleries)

## Caucasus (Russian Caucasus)

### Foods
1. Khachapuri (cheese-filled bread, Georgian influence)
2. Shashlik (marinated grilled meat skewers)
3. Khinkali (Georgian soup dumplings)
4. Adjarian khachapuri (boat-shaped bread with egg, cheese)
5. Lobio (red bean stew with herbs)
6. Satsivi (walnut sauce with chicken)
7. Churchkhela (walnut sausage in grape must)
8. Pkhali (vegetable and walnut pâté)
9. Lavash (thin flatbread)
10. Caucasian cheese (bryndza, suluguni)

### Drinks
- Georgian wine (Saperavi, Rkatsiteli)
- Chacha (grape pomace brandy)
- Tarkhuna (tarragon soda, bright green)

## Volga Region

### Foods
1. Volga fish soup (sterlet, sturgeon, pike)
2. Karavai (ceremonial wedding bread)
3. Tatar echpochmak (triangular meat pies)
4. Chak-chak (fried dough with honey, Tatar)
5. Volga sturgeon caviar (black caviar)
6. Beluga fish (smoked or fresh)
7. Tatar gubadiya (layered meat and rice pie)
8. Plov (rice pilaf with meat, Tatar style)
9. Kystyby (Tatar potato flatbread)
10. Tatar baursak (fried dough balls)

### Drinks
- Kvass (fermented rye bread drink)
- Tatar kumis (fermented mare's milk)
- Volga vodka (regional grain spirit)

## Russian Far East

### Foods
1. Kamchatka king crab (steamed or grilled)
2. Red caviar (salmon roe, ikura)
3. Ukha (fish soup with salmon, vegetables)
4. Sakhalin scallops (fresh or grilled)
5. Far Eastern salmon (grilled, smoked, cured)
6. Sea urchin (uni, fresh from Pacific)
7. Squid dishes (dried, grilled, stir-fried)
8. Vladivostok salad (seafood, vegetables)
9. Pickled ferns (ostrich fern, taiga vegetable)
10. Medovik (honey cake, regional variation)

### Drinks
- Primorsky tea (wild herbs, pine needles)
- Pacific vodka (regional distilleries)
- Birch sap (fresh spring harvest)

## Golden Ring (Central Russia)

### Foods
1. Suzdal pickles (cucumber, cabbage, garlic)
2. Rostov enamel cookies (decorated gingerbread)
3. Pozharsky cutlets (Torzhok chicken cutlets)
4. Suzdal honey (wildflower, buckwheat)
5. Yaroslavl cheese (Russian Swiss-style)
6. Tula pryanik (spiced honey cake with filling)
7. Murom kalach (twisted white bread)
8. Kostroma cheese (firm cow's milk)
9. Ivanovo syrniki (cheese pancakes)
10. Vladimir cherry varenye (cherry preserves)

### Drinks
- Suzdal medovukha (honey mead, fermented)
- Sbiten (hot spiced honey drink)
- Ivan-chai (fireweed tea, traditional)

## Karelia

### Foods
1. Karelian pasty (rye crust with rice or potato)
2. Kalitka (open-faced rye pies)
3. Karelian fish soup (salmon, perch, whitefish)
4. Loimulohi (salmon cooked on planks by fire)
5. Sultsina (rye pastry with barley porridge)
6. Karelian stew (lamb, pork, beef layered in pot)
7. Berry pies (lingonberry, bilberry, cloudberry)
8. Rye bread (dark, dense, sourdough)
9. Pickled mushrooms (forest harvest)
10. Northern berries (lingonberry, cloudberry preserves)

### Drinks
- Karelian kvass (rye bread fermentation)
- Birch sap (spring harvest, fresh or fermented)
- Berry kompot (stewed fruit drink)

## Moscow Region

### Foods
1. Beef Stroganoff (sautéed beef with sour cream sauce)
2. Olivier salad (potato, egg, peas, mayo, meat)
3. Blini (thin pancakes with various fillings)
4. Borscht (beet soup with beef, sour cream)
5. Shchi (cabbage soup with meat)
6. Pirozhki (stuffed buns with meat, cabbage, egg)
7. Syrniki (cottage cheese pancakes)
8. Kasha (buckwheat porridge)
9. Smetana (sour cream, used in many dishes)
10. Medovik (layered honey cake)

### Drinks
- Russian vodka (Stolichnaya, Beluga brands)
- Kvass (fermented rye bread drink)
- Kompot (stewed fruit beverage)

## St. Petersburg Region

### Foods
1. Beef Stroganoff (originated here, named for Count Stroganov)
2. Ukha (clear fish soup with potatoes)
3. Pelmeni (Siberian dumplings, popular here)
4. Herring under fur coat (layered salad with herring, beets)
5. Smelt (koryushka, fried small fish, spring delicacy)
6. Pirozhki (small filled pastries)
7. Vatrushka (sweet cheese pastry)
8. Syrniki (cheese pancakes)
9. Ptichye moloko (bird's milk cake, chocolate mousse)
10. Baltic sprats (smoked small fish)

### Drinks
- Russian Imperial Stout (strong dark beer)
- Baltic Porter (dark, sweet beer)
- Petersburg vodka (premium brands)

## Don Cossack Region (Southern Russia)

### Foods
1. Don fish soup (carp, pike, perch, tomatoes)
2. Cossack borscht (rich beet soup with pork)
3. Don crayfish (boiled with dill)
4. Shashlik (grilled marinated lamb or pork)
5. Kuban lard (salo, cured pork fat)
6. Cossack kulesh (millet, pork, vegetables)
7. Don river fish (fried, smoked)
8. Varenyky (stuffed dumplings, Ukrainian influence)
9. Mazurek (holiday cake with nuts)
10. Sunflower halva (pressed sunflower seed sweet)

### Drinks
- Don wine (local Tsimlyansk reds)
- Cossack vodka (wheat spirit)
- Kumis (fermented mare's milk, steppe tradition)
