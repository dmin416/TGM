# France — Regional Foods and Drinks

## Normandy

### Foods
1. Camembert cheese (soft, bloomy rind, creamy)
2. Calvados (apple brandy, used in cooking)
3. Moules marinières (mussels in white wine)
4. Sole à la normande (sole with cream, mushrooms)
5. Tarte Tatin (upside-down caramelized apple tart)
6. Livarot cheese (washed rind, pungent, tied with sedge)
7. Pont-l'Évêque cheese (soft, square, orange rind)
8. Boudin noir (blood sausage with apples)

### Drinks
- Calvados (apple brandy, aged in oak)
- Normandy cider (still, sparkling, traditional)
- Pommeau (apple juice and Calvados blend)

## Brittany

### Foods
1. Crêpes (thin wheat pancakes, savory or sweet)
2. Galettes (buckwheat crêpes, savory fillings)
3. Kouign-amann (layered butter and sugar pastry)
4. Far breton (prune custard flan)
5. Cotriade (fish stew with potatoes)
6. Andouille de Guémené (smoked pork chitterling sausage)
7. Kig ha farz (meat and buckwheat dumpling stew)
8. Galette-saucisse (sausage in buckwheat crêpe)
9. Palets bretons (thick butter cookies)
10. Cancale oysters (flat native oysters)

### Drinks
- Chouchen (Breton mead, honey wine)
- Breton cider (traditional, artisanal)
- Lambig (Breton apple brandy)

## Provence

### Foods
1. Bouillabaisse (saffron fish stew, Marseille)
2. Ratatouille (eggplant, zucchini, tomato, peppers)
3. Tapenade (olive, caper, anchovy paste)
4. Pissaladière (onion, anchovy, olive tart)
5. Socca (chickpea flour pancake, Nice)
6. Salade Niçoise (tuna, egg, anchovy, vegetables)
7. Aioli (garlic mayonnaise with vegetables, cod)
8. Daube provençale (beef braised in red wine)
9. Calisson d'Aix (almond, candied melon confection)
10. Navettes (boat-shaped orange blossom cookies)

### Drinks
- Rosé wine (Bandol, Côtes de Provence)
- Pastis (anise-flavored aperitif, Ricard)
- Provençal red wine (Châteauneuf-du-Pape)

## Alsace

### Foods
1. Choucroute garnie (sauerkraut with sausages, pork)
2. Tarte flambée (thin crust, crème fraîche, onions, bacon)
3. Baeckeoffe (meat, potato, white wine casserole)
4. Kugelhopf (yeasted bundt cake with almonds)
5. Munster cheese (soft, washed rind, pungent)
6. Bretzel (soft pretzel with coarse salt)
7. Foie gras d'Alsace (goose liver pâté)
8. Spaetzle (egg noodles, butter)
9. Tarte à l'oignon (onion tart)
10. Bredele (Christmas cookies, various types)

### Drinks
- Alsace Riesling (dry white wine)
- Gewürztraminer (aromatic white wine)
- Alsace beer (Kronenbourg, Fischer)

## Burgundy

### Foods
1. Boeuf bourguignon (beef braised in red wine)
2. Coq au vin (chicken braised in red wine)
3. Escargots de Bourgogne (snails with garlic butter)
4. Oeufs en meurette (poached eggs in red wine sauce)
5. Gougères (cheese puffs)
6. Jambon persillé (ham and parsley terrine)
7. Epoisses cheese (washed rind, Marc de Bourgogne)
8. Pain d'épices (spiced honey cake, Dijon)
9. Dijon mustard (smooth, sharp condiment)
10. Cassis de Dijon (blackcurrant liqueur)

### Drinks
- Burgundy red wine (Pinot Noir, grands crus)
- Burgundy white wine (Chardonnay, Chablis)
- Kir (white wine with cassis)

## Loire Valley

### Foods
1. Rillettes du Mans (pork confit spread)
2. Rillauds (pork belly chunks, crispy)
3. Tarte Tatin (invented here, upside-down apple tart)
4. Pithiviers (almond cream puff pastry)
5. Poulet en barbouille (chicken in blood sauce)
6. Chavignol goat cheese (Crottin de Chavignol, hard)
7. Beurre blanc (butter, shallot, white wine sauce)
8. Sandre au beurre blanc (pike-perch with butter sauce)
9. Tarte aux pruneaux (prune tart)

### Drinks
- Sancerre (Sauvignon Blanc white wine)
- Vouvray (Chenin Blanc, still or sparkling)
- Muscadet (crisp white wine, Atlantic coast)

## French Riviera (Côte d'Azur)

### Foods
1. Pan bagnat (Niçoise salad in bread roll)
2. Socca (chickpea flour pancake)
3. Pissaladière (onion, anchovy, olive flatbread)
4. Salade Niçoise (tuna, egg, anchovy, tomato)
5. Daube niçoise (beef stew with tomatoes, olives)
6. Tourte de blettes (Swiss chard, raisin, pine nut pie)
7. Farcis niçois (stuffed vegetables)
8. Beignets de fleurs de courgettes (zucchini flower fritters)
9. Barbajuans (fried ravioli with chard, ricotta)
10. Fougasse (flatbread with olives, herbs)

### Drinks
- Rosé wine (Provence, Bandol)
- Pastis (anise aperitif)
- Bellet wine (rare Nice appellation)

## Champagne

### Foods
1. Biscuits roses de Reims (pink twice-baked cookies)
2. Chaource cheese (soft, creamy, bloomy rind)
3. Langres cheese (washed rind, orange, sunken top)
4. Potée champenoise (pork, cabbage, vegetable stew)
5. Pied de cochon à la Sainte-Menehould (breaded pig's trotter)
6. Jambon de Reims (ham in pastry crust)
7. Gougères champenoises (cheese puffs)
8. Boudin blanc de Rethel (white pork sausage)
9. Tarte au sucre (sugar tart)

### Drinks
- Champagne (sparkling wine, méthode champenoise)
- Ratafia (fortified grape must aperitif)
- Marc de Champagne (pomace brandy)

## Bordeaux

### Foods
1. Entrecôte à la bordelaise (rib steak with red wine sauce)
2. Lamproie à la bordelaise (lamprey eel in red wine)
3. Cannelés (caramelized rum and vanilla cakes)
4. Huîtres d'Arcachon (Arcachon Bay oysters)
5. Cèpes (porcini mushrooms, forests)
6. Foie gras (duck or goose liver)
7. Grenier médocain (sweet bread with vanilla)
8. Mouclade (mussels in curry cream sauce)
9. Pibales (elver eels, baby eels)
10. Macarons de Saint-Émilion (almond macaroons)

### Drinks
- Bordeaux red wine (Médoc, Saint-Émilion, Pauillac)
- Sauternes (sweet white wine)
- Bordeaux Clairet (rosé-style light red)

## Dordogne

### Foods
1. Foie gras (duck and goose liver, Périgord)
2. Confit de canard (duck preserved in fat)
3. Magret de canard (duck breast, grilled)
4. Truffes noires (black Périgord truffles)
5. Cèpes (porcini mushrooms)
6. Noix du Périgord (walnuts, oil, cakes)
7. Pommes sarladaises (potatoes fried in duck fat)
8. Tourin (garlic soup with egg)
9. Croustade aux pommes (thin apple pastry)
10. Cabécou (small round goat cheese)

### Drinks
- Bergerac red wine (Malbec, Merlot)
- Monbazillac (sweet white wine)
- Vin de noix (walnut aperitif wine)
