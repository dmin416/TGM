# Germany — Regional Foods and Drinks

## Bavaria

### Foods
1. Weißwurst (white veal sausage with parsley, lemon)
2. Schweinshaxe (roasted pork knuckle, crispy skin)
3. Leberkäse (baked meatloaf, served sliced)
4. Obatzda (camembert cheese spread with paprika, onions)
5. Brezn (soft pretzel with coarse salt)
6. Knödel (potato or bread dumplings)
7. Schweinsbraten (roast pork with crackling)
8. Apfelstrudel (apple strudel with cinnamon)
9. Dampfnudel (steamed sweet dumpling)
10. Auszogne (fried dough pastry, stretched thin)

### Drinks
- Bavarian wheat beer (Weißbier, Hefeweizen)
- Helles (pale Munich lager)
- Dunkles (dark Munich lager)

## Black Forest (Schwarzwald)

### Foods
1. Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte (Black Forest cake with cherries, cream)
2. Schwarzwälder Schinken (Black Forest ham, smoked)
3. Flammkuchen (thin crust with crème fraîche, onions, bacon)
4. Maultaschen (large ravioli with meat, spinach)
5. Spätzle (egg noodles, often with cheese)
6. Bollenhut cake (traditional hat-shaped pastry)
7. Kirschwasser (cherry brandy, clear)
8. Bauernbrot (dark rye bread)
9. Zwetschgenkuchen (plum cake)
10. Käsespätzle (cheese-covered spätzle)

### Drinks
- Kirschwasser (cherry schnapps)
- Black Forest beer (Rothaus Tannenzäpfle)
- Baden wine (Riesling, Pinot Noir)

## Rhine Valley

### Foods
1. Sauerbraten (marinated pot roast with raisins, gingersnaps)
2. Himmel und Erde (apple, potato, blood sausage dish)
3. Reibekuchen (potato pancakes with applesauce)
4. Halve Hahn (rye roll with Gouda, mustard, onions)
5. Rheinischer Döbbekuchen (potato cake)
6. Muscheln Rheinische Art (Rhine-style mussels)
7. Flönz (blood sausage, Cologne)
8. Kölsch Kaviar (blood sausage with onions, rye bread)
9. Printen (spiced gingerbread, Aachen)
10. Schnippelbohnensuppe (green bean soup)

### Drinks
- Kölsch (light ale, Cologne)
- Riesling (white wine, Mosel, Rheingau)
- Altbier (dark ale, Düsseldorf)

## Saxony

### Foods
1. Dresdner Stollen (Christmas fruit bread with marzipan)
2. Sauerbraten (Saxon-style marinated roast)
3. Leipziger Allerlei (mixed vegetables with crayfish, morels)
4. Eierschecke (layered cheesecake with vanilla custard)
5. Quarkkäulchen (fried quark potato cakes)
6. Pulsnitzer Pfefferkuchen (gingerbread)
7. Sächsische Kartoffelsuppe (Saxon potato soup)
8. Quarkkeulchen (sweet curd cheese fritters)
9. Christstollen (marzipan-filled Christmas bread)
10. Bienenstich (bee sting cake with custard, almonds)

### Drinks
- Saxon wine (Elbe valley, Müller-Thurgau)
- Radeberger Pilsner (crisp lager)
- Fassbrause (malt-based soft drink)

## Harz Mountains

### Foods
1. Harzer Käse (sour milk cheese, strong, low fat)
2. Harzer Schmorwurst (smoked sausage)
3. Forelle Müllerin Art (trout with butter, almonds)
4. Wildgulasch (game stew with mushrooms)
5. Baumkuchen (tree cake, layered on spit)
6. Harzer Wildbraten (wild game roast)
7. Harzer Schmalzkuchen (fried dough cake)
8. Harzer Leberwurst (liver sausage)
9. Harzer Knackwurst (snapping sausage)
10. Buchweizenpfannkuchen (buckwheat pancakes)

### Drinks
- Hasseröder Pilsner (Harz lager)
- Schierker Feuerstein (herbal liqueur, 56 proof)
- Harz mountain herb tea (wild herbs)

## Thuringia

### Foods
1. Thüringer Rostbratwurst (grilled pork sausage)
2. Thüringer Klöße (potato dumplings, half raw, half cooked)
3. Thüringer Leberwurst (liver sausage)
4. Rostbrätel (marinated pork steak, grilled)
5. Mutzbraten (pork shoulder, marinated, grilled)
6. Schmandkuchen (sour cream cake)
7. Thüringer Mett (raw minced pork with spices)
8. Schmalkuchen (fried dough pastry)
9. Eichsfelder Feldgieker (smoked raw sausage)
10. Altenburger Ziegenkäse (goat cheese)

### Drinks
- Köstritzer Schwarzbier (dark lager)
- Thüringer beer (regional breweries)
- Schnapps (fruit brandies, plum, pear)

## Brandenburg

### Foods
1. Spreewälder Gurken (Spreewald pickles, protected origin)
2. Teltower Rübchen (small turnips, historical delicacy)
3. Beelitzer Spargel (white asparagus)
4. Kartoffelpuffer (potato pancakes)
5. Quarkkeulchen (curd cheese potato cakes)
6. Eisbein (boiled ham hock)
7. Leinöl mit Pellkartoffeln (linseed oil with boiled potatoes)
8. Grützwurst (groats sausage)
9. Böhmische Knödel (Bohemian dumplings)
10. Streuselkuchen (crumb cake)

### Drinks
- Pilsner (German-style lager)
- Korn (grain spirit, clear)
- Sanddorn juice (sea buckthorn, vitamin C)

## Hamburg and North

### Foods
1. Labskaus (corned beef, potato, beetroot hash with herring)
2. Fischbrötchen (fish sandwich, herring, mackerel)
3. Matjes (young herring, lightly salted)
4. Finkenwerder Scholle (plaice with bacon, shrimps)
5. Aalsuppe (eel soup with vegetables, fruit)
6. Birnen, Bohnen und Speck (pears, green beans, bacon)
7. Franzbrötchen (cinnamon pastry roll)
8. Rote Grütze (red berry pudding with cream)
9. Schwarzsauer (duck or goose blood soup)
10. Rundstück warm (warm meat in bread roll)

### Drinks
- Alsterwasser (beer with lemon soda)
- Korn (clear grain spirit)
- Astra beer (Hamburg pilsner)

## Westphalia

### Foods
1. Westfälischer Schinken (Westphalian ham, juniper-smoked)
2. Pumpernickel (dark rye bread, steamed)
3. Panhas (pork, buckwheat, blood loaf)
4. Pfefferpotthast (beef stew with pepper, onions)
5. Pickert (potato pancake with raisins)
6. Westfälischer Schmorbraten (pot roast)
7. Wurstebrot (sausage bread)
8. Streuselkuchen (crumb cake)
9. Münsterländer Stuten (sweet bread)
10. Töttchen (veal ragout in cream sauce)

### Drinks
- Steinhäger (juniper schnapps)
- Westphalian beer (regional pilsners)
- Warsteiner Pilsner (crisp lager)
