# Belgium — Regional Foods and Drinks

## Flanders

### Foods
1. Moules-frites (mussels steamed with white wine, served with fries)
2. Waterzooi (creamy chicken or fish stew)
3. Stoofvlees (beef stewed in beer, Flemish carbonnade)
4. Gentse waterzooi (Ghent-style chicken or fish in cream broth)
5. Belgian frites (double-fried potatoes, thick cut)
6. Paling in 't groen (eel in green herb sauce)
7. Mattentaart (cheesecake tart, Geraardsbergen)
8. Speculoos (spiced shortcrust biscuits)
9. Gentse mokken (cone-shaped raspberry-filled candies)
10. Vlaamse karbonaden (Flemish beef and beer stew)

### Drinks
- Belgian beer (Trappist ales, abbey beers, lambic)
- Jenever (juniper-flavored spirit, Belgian gin)
- Duvel (strong golden ale)

## Wallonia

### Foods
1. Boulets à la liégeoise (meatballs in sweet-sour sauce with syrup)
2. Lapin à la tournaisienne (rabbit cooked in beer, prunes)
3. Gaufres de Liège (pearl sugar waffles, caramelized)
4. Tarte al djote (chard and cheese tart, Nivelles)
5. Couque de Dinant (hard honey biscuit)
6. Pâté gaumais (meat pie with eggs)
7. Escavèche (fried fish in vinegar marinade)
8. Boulettes sauce tomate (meatballs in tomato sauce)
9. Flamiche aux poireaux (leek tart)
10. Salade liégeoise (warm potato, bacon, green bean salad)

### Drinks
- Orval (Trappist ale, Ardennes)
- Peket (juniper spirit, Liège)
- Chimay (Trappist beer, red, blue, white)

## Brussels-Capital Region

### Foods
1. Gaufres de Bruxelles (Brussels waffles, rectangular, light, crispy)
2. Choux de Bruxelles (Brussels sprouts, often with bacon)
3. Filet américain (raw minced beef with capers, onions)
4. Croquettes aux crevettes (shrimp croquettes in béchamel)
5. Vol-au-vent (puff pastry with chicken, mushrooms, cream)
6. Américain-frites (raw beef with fries)
7. Stoemp (mashed potato with vegetables, sausage)
8. Pistolets (small round bread rolls)
9. Brussels sprouts (roasted or steamed)
10. Belgian chocolate pralines (filled chocolates, Neuhaus, Godiva)

### Drinks
- Gueuze (spontaneously fermented lambic beer)
- Kriek (cherry lambic beer)
- Cantillon (traditional lambic brewery)

## Pan-Belgian Specialties

### Foods
1. Carbonnade flamande (beef and beer stew with mustard)
2. Belgian waffles (Liège with pearl sugar, Brussels rectangular)
3. Belgian chocolate (pralines, truffles, Leonidas, Godiva)
4. Witloof (Belgian endive, braised with ham)
5. Fricadelle (deep-fried minced meat sausage)
6. Cuberdons (cone-shaped raspberry gum candies)
7. Dame blanche (vanilla ice cream with hot chocolate sauce)
8. Speculoos spread (cookie butter)
9. Belgian fries with various sauces (mayo, andalouse, samurai)
10. Smoutebollen (deep-fried dough balls, powdered sugar)

### Drinks
- Trappist beers (Westvleteren, Chimay, Orval, Rochefort, Westmalle, Achel)
- Abbey beers (Leffe, Grimbergen, Affligem)
- Lambic varieties (gueuze, kriek, framboise)
