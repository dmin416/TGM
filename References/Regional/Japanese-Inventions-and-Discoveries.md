# Japanese Inventions and Discoveries

OTL chronology of major Japanese inventions and discoveries from traditional arts through digital technology. Use for Max omniscient knowledge context, Japan/Korea alignment scenes, and industrial comparison. ## Traditional Arts and Crafts

- Urushi lacquerware techniques: ancient, refined through Jomon period onward
- Bonsai cultivation as distinct Japanese art form: adapted from China, approximately 800 CE onward
- Noh theater: Zeami Motokiyo, 1300s to 1400s
- Ikebana flower arrangement: Muromachi period, approximately 1400s
- Katana laminated steel forging technique (tamahagane): medieval period, refined through 1300s
- Origami: traditional, codified and systematized through Edo period

---

## Philosophy and Religion

- Zen Buddhism systematization in Japan: Eisai introduced Rinzai, 1191
- Soto Zen school: Dogen Zenji, 1227
- Bushido warrior code formalized: Edo period, approximately 1600s

---

## Medicine and Pharmaceuticals

- Adrenaline isolation: Jokichi Takamine, 1901
- Umami taste identification: Kikunae Ikeda, 1908
- Vitamin B1 oryzanin isolation: Umetaro Suzuki, 1910
- Induced pluripotent stem cells: Shinya Yamanaka, 2006

## Notes

Japan's most consequential single contribution to modern civilization may be the Toyota Production System. Lean manufacturing and just in time methodology transformed global industrial practice across every sector. Every modern factory on earth operates on principles Japan developed and exported without receiving royalties proportional to that contribution.

Fujio Masuoka's flash memory invention at Toshiba underpins essentially all modern digital storage. Every smartphone, solid state drive, USB device and cloud data center runs on his work. Toshiba failed to patent and commercialize it aggressively. American firms Intel and others dominated the commercial returns.
