# Switzerland — Cultural Regions

## Bernese Oberland

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Alphorn playing — traditional mountain horn performances
2. Alpine wrestling (Schwingen) — sawdust ring grappling competitions
3. Yodeling festivals — traditional mountain singing competitions
4. Cheese making demonstrations — Alpine dairy production tours
5. Mountain railway journeys — Jungfraujoch cogwheel train traditions
6. Fasnacht carnival — Basel masked procession celebrations
7. Swiss flag throwing — traditional Fahneschwingen acrobatics
8. Chalet building preservation — wooden architecture conservation
9. Mountain hut (berghaus) culture — Alpine refuge hospitality
10. Traditional costume (tracht) wearing — regional dress preservation

**Specialties:** Raclette, rösti, Emmental cheese, Berner platte, meringue with cream, cherry brandy, Älplermagronen, Berner Haselnusslebkuchen

## Valais

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Cow fighting festivals (combats de reines) — traditional cattle competitions
2. Glacier hiking — Aletsch ice field exploration tours
3. Matterhorn pilgrimages — iconic mountain viewing traditions
4. Fendant wine tasting — local white wine vineyard tours
5. Raclette du Valais making — traditional melted cheese preparation
6. Irrigation channel (bisse) walking — historic water system hiking
7. Traditional costume festivals — regional dress celebrations
8. Zermatt car-free culture — electric vehicle village preservation
9. Mountain guide training — Alpine climbing instruction heritage
10. Apricot harvest festivals — Valais fruit growing celebrations

**Specialties:** Raclette du Valais, Fendant wine, apricots, rye bread, air-dried meat (viande séchée), Walliser wine, cheese fondue

3. Engadine house painting — traditional sgraffito wall decoration
4. Chalandamarz spring festivals — cowbell processions and singing
5. Capuns making — Swiss chard wrapped dumpling tradition
6. Via Ferrata climbing — assisted mountain route hiking
7. Glacier Express journeys — scenic mountain railway tours
8. Heidi heritage tours — Johanna Spyri literary landscape visits
9. Bundner Herrschaft wine route — Alpine vineyard touring
10. Traditional hay barn preservation — architectural heritage conservation

**Specialties:** Capuns, Bundner Nusstorte (nut cake), Bundnerfleisch (dried meat), Pizzoccheri, Bundner Gerstensuppe, barley soup, Maluns
