# Spain — Cultural Regions

## Andalusia

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Flamenco dancing and guitar — passionate gypsy art form
2. Holy Week processions — elaborate religious pasos and penitents
3. April Fair celebrations — Seville horse parades and dancing
4. Bullfighting traditions — corrida ritual and matador culture
5. White village tours — pueblo blanco architectural preservation
6. Moorish heritage festivals — Islamic architectural celebration
7. Sherry bodega tours — fortified wine aging traditions
8. Olive oil mill demonstrations — traditional pressing methods
9. Feria de Abril horse shows — Andalusian breed exhibitions
10. Gypsy cave dwelling tours — Sacromonte neighborhood Granada

**Specialties:** Gazpacho, jamón ibérico, sherry, pescaíto frito (fried fish), salmorejo, tortilla española, churros, gazpacho

---

## Catalonia

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Human tower building (castells) — competitive tower formations
2. Sardana dancing — circular folk dance in public squares
3. Sant Jordi Day celebrations — book and rose exchange tradition
4. Caganer nativity figurines — traditional Christmas scene characters
5. La Mercè festivals — Barcelona patron saint celebrations
6. Catalan language preservation — regional linguistic independence
7. Modernist architecture tours — Gaudí and Art Nouveau heritage
8. Correfoc fire runs — devils dancing with fireworks
9. Christmas log (Caga Tió) — gift-giving log beating tradition
10. Costa Brava coastal hiking — rugged Mediterranean cliff walks

**Specialties:** Pan con tomate, butifarra sausage, crema catalana, escalivada, fideuà, cava sparkling wine, calçots, Manchego cheese

---

## Basque Country

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Basque language (Euskara) preservation — ancient language education
2. Pintxos bar hopping — small plate tapas culture
3. Pelota basque ball game — traditional court sport
4. Stone lifting competitions (harri jasotzaileak) — strength contests
5. Txalaparta percussion — traditional wooden plank drumming
6. Bertsolari improvised poetry — competitive verse singing
7. Basque rowing regattas (traineras) — coastal boat racing
8. Rural sports festivals — log chopping and weight carrying
9. Cider house (sagardotegi) rituals — barrel tapping ceremonies
10. Traditional fishermen's auctions — coastal marketplace customs

**Specialties:** Pintxos, bacalao pil pil (cod), marmitako stew, Idiazabal cheese, txakoli wine, piperade, gâteau basque, chorizo

---

## Galicia

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Santiago de Compostela pilgrimage — Camino walking tradition
2. Galician bagpipe (gaita) music — Celtic musical heritage
3. Queimada ritual — flaming liquor preparation ceremony
4. Rapa das Bestas — wild horse roundup festivals
5. Galician language preservation — regional Celtic linguistic heritage
6. Seafood festivals (marisquiño) — coastal harvest celebrations
7. Hórreo granary preservation — raised stone storage buildings
8. Celtic cross pilgrimages — ancient stone monument visits
9. Mussel raft farming — coastal aquaculture traditions
10. Rain processions (rogas) — agricultural blessing ceremonies

**Specialties:** Pulpo a la gallega (octopus), empanada, lacón con grelos, percebes (goose barnacles), Albariño wine, tetilla cheese, tarta de Santiago

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Las Fallas festivals — giant papier-mâché burning ceremonies
2. Paella cooking competitions — traditional rice dish contests
3. Horchata drinking traditions — tiger nut milk beverage culture
4. Albufera lake boat tours — traditional fishing demonstrations
5. Holy Week maritime processions — religious boat parades
6. Orange blossom festivals — agricultural harvest celebrations
7. Water Tribunal meetings — medieval irrigation court sessions
8. Silk Exchange heritage — Gothic architecture preservation
9. Moors and Christians festivals — historical reenactment battles
10. Buñuelos making — pumpkin fritter preparation traditions

**Specialties:** Paella, horchata, fideuà, fartons pastries, turrones nougat, oranges, all i pebre (eel stew), buñuelos

## Castile

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Don Quixote windmill tours — literary heritage site visits
2. Castilian language preservation — pure Spanish dialect maintenance
3. Medieval castle circuit — fortification architecture exploration
4. Semana Santa processions — solemn religious Holy Week parades
5. Roast lamb traditions — wood oven preparation methods
6. Bull running festivals — encierro street chasing traditions
7. Cochinillo asado feasts — roast suckling pig banquets
8. La Mancha wine route — vineyard touring traditions
9. Corpus Christi flower carpets — street petal art displays
10. Castilian folk music — jota dance and dulzaina pipe traditions

**Specialties:** Cochinillo asado (roast suckling pig), cordero asado (roast lamb), manchego cheese, marzipan, chorizo, jamón serrano, sopa castellana

## Asturias

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Cider pouring (escanciado) — theatrical high pour tradition
2. Asturian bagpipe music — Celtic gaita performances
3. Picos de Europa hiking — mountain pilgrimage traditions
4. Coal mining heritage tours — industrial history preservation
5. Asturian language revival — regional dialect education
6. Covadonga pilgrimage — religious cave shrine visits
7. Cider house (sidrería) culture — communal barrel drinking
8. Descent of the Sella canoe race — annual river competition
9. Traditional granary (hórreo) preservation — raised storage buildings
10. Cheese cave aging tours — Cabrales blue cheese production

**Specialties:** Fabada asturiana (bean stew), cachopo (breaded veal), Cabrales cheese, cider, chorizo a la sidra, arroz con leche, frixuelos
