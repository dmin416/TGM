# Germany — Cultural Regions

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Oktoberfest — world's largest beer festival in Munich
2. Lederhosen and dirndl wearing — traditional Bavarian costume culture
3. Alpine horn playing — mountain shepherd musical tradition
4. Maypole raising ceremonies — village spring celebration customs
5. Schuhplattler dancing — traditional slap dance performances
6. Christmas market traditions — Christkindlmarkt in Nuremberg
7. Passion Play performances — Oberammergau religious theater
8. Beer garden culture — outdoor communal drinking traditions
9. Mountain hiking and hut culture — Alpine refuge system
10. Neuschwanstein Castle tours — fairy tale architecture visits

**Specialties:** Weisswurst sausage, pretzels, Bavarian beer, Schweinshaxe (pork knuckle), Leberkäse, Obatzda cheese, apple strudel, Dampfnudel
