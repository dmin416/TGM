# Belgium — Cultural Regions

## Flanders

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Ommegang pageant — historical procession reenactment in Brussels
2. Carillon bell tower concerts — traditional bell ringing performances
3. Flemish painting restoration — medieval art preservation workshops
4. Beer brewing traditions — abbey and Trappist monastery methods
5. Procession of the Holy Blood — religious relic parade in Bruges
6. Lace making demonstrations — Bruges and Brussels bobbin lace craft
7. Carnival celebrations — pre-Lenten street festivals with costumes
8. Chocolate making workshops — praline and truffle craft traditions
9. Begijnhof preservation — medieval women's community conservation
10. Bicycle culture — daily cycling and velodrome racing traditions

**Specialties:** Belgian waffles, chocolate pralines, Flemish stew (carbonnade), moules-frites, speculoos cookies, jenever gin, witloof (endive), Trappist beer

## Wallonia

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Walloon folk music festivals — traditional bagpipe and hurdy-gurdy
2. Coal mining heritage tours — industrial revolution site visits
3. Ardennes forest hunting traditions — wild boar and deer hunting
4. French language theater — Walloon dramatic arts performances
5. Spa town thermal bath culture — historic wellness tourism
6. Liège waffle making — pearl sugar waffle tradition
7. Marionette puppet theater — traditional Tchantchès character shows
8. Battle of the Bulge commemorations — WWII remembrance events
9. Cheese cave aging tours — Herve and Chimay production visits
10. Meuse river valley cycling — historical waterway exploration

**Specialties:** Liège waffles, Herve cheese, Ardennes pâté, boulets à la Liégeoise (meatballs), sirop de Liège, game meats, Orval beer, Brussels sprouts

## Brussels-Capital Region

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Grand Place flower carpet — biennial floral display event
2. Manneken Pis costume ceremonies — statue dressing traditions
3. Art Nouveau architecture tours — Horta and Hankar building visits
4. Comic strip wall murals — Tintin and Smurfs street art culture
5. European Quarter institutional tours — EU parliament and commission visits
6. Belgian beer café culture — hundreds of beer varieties sampling
7. Atomium exhibitions — 1958 World's Fair structure events
8. Royal Galleries shopping — 19th century covered arcade tradition
9. Marolles flea market — antique and vintage goods trading
10. Jazz club culture — historical music venue scene

**Specialties:** Moules-frites, Brussels sprouts, waterzooi stew, Belgian fries, speculoos, Brussels waffles, lambic beer, pralines
