# Austria — Cultural Regions

## Tyrol

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Almabtrieb cattle drives — decorated cow descent from Alpine pastures
2. Tyrolean evening shows — folk music and dancing performances
3. Schuhplattler dancing — traditional slap dance demonstrations
4. Krampus processions — demon mask parades during Advent
5. Alpine horn playing — mountain shepherd musical tradition
6. Traditional costume wearing — lederhosen and dirndl preservation
7. Ski resort culture — Alpine winter sports traditions
8. Mountain hut (hütte) overnight stays — Alpine refuge hospitality
8. Herz-Jesu Feuer — Sacred Heart bonfire tradition on mountaintops
10. Tyrolean wrestling (Ranggeln) — regional grappling sport

**Specialties:** Tiroler Gröstl (fried potatoes), Speckknödel (bacon dumplings), Kaiserschmarrn, apple strudel, Tyrolean bacon, Schlutzkrapfen
