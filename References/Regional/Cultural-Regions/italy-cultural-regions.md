2. Chianti wine estate tours — vineyard and castle tasting traditions
3. Renaissance art restoration — Florentine painting preservation workshops
4. Truffle hunting — trained dog foraging expeditions
5. Calcio Storico — violent historical football reenactment
6. Marble quarrying tours — Carrara white stone extraction heritage
7. Dante recitation festivals — Divine Comedy poetry performances
8. Cypress-lined road cycling — iconic Tuscan landscape touring
9. Medieval tower climbing — San Gimignano skyline preservation
10. Leather crafting demonstrations — Florentine artisan traditions

**Specialties:** Bistecca alla Fiorentina, ribollita soup, pappa al pomodoro, panzanella, Chianti wine, pecorino cheese, cantuccini biscuits, panforte
