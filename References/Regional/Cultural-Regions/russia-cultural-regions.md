# Russia — Cultural Regions

## Siberia

**Cultural Practices:**
1. Banya (bathhouse) rituals — extreme heat and ice plunge traditions
2. Shamanic ceremonies — indigenous spiritual healing practices
3. Reindeer herding migrations — nomadic Evenki and Nenets traditions
4. Lake Baikal ice walking — winter pilgrimage and ice fishing
5. Throat singing performances — Tuvan overtone musical tradition
6. Taiga survival skills — wilderness hunting and foraging education
7. Winter solstice festivals — indigenous new year celebrations
8. Mammoth tusk carving — ancient ivory craft traditions
9. Dog sledding expeditions — traditional transportation preservation
10. Old Believer communities — religious exile settlement heritage

**Specialties:** Pelmeni dumplings, omul fish, wild game, pine nut dishes, taiga berries, stroganina (frozen fish), venison, wild mushrooms
