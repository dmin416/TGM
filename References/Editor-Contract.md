# Editor Contract

Numbered master inventory for writing and editing *Trenches, Guns and Magic*. Use before any change to `Story/` or `Status.md`.

**Detail and examples:** [Prose-Mechanics.md](Prose-Mechanics.md) · **World and enemy logic:** [World-and-Setting.md](World-and-Setting.md) · **Magic on page:** [Magic.md](Magic.md) · **Max HUD and spells:** [MaxSystem.md](MaxSystem.md)

---

1. Verify every weapon, name and place against `References/` before writing.
2. Weapons match historical introduction dates.
3. Correct source novel errors silently in `Story/`.
4. German military competence and equipment follow real history.
5. Plain, direct English; match register of nearest paragraph.
6. No ornate or lyrical padding.
7. No new similes; keep source similes as written.
8. No editor-generated "like a" or "as if" lines.
9. Forbidden: recursive descriptors, weather as mood, body part as object.
10. No strategy-game UI jargon in narrative prose.
11. No hyphens or em dashes.
12. No colons inside dialogue.
13. No Oxford commas.
14. No comma before *and*, *but* or *or* in parallel constructions.
15. Every sentence is at least four words.
16. Each paragraph opens with its named subject, not a pronoun.
17. Two consecutive sentences on the same subject: second uses a pronoun.
18. Prepositional phrase over compressed noun stacks.
19. Dialogue: quotation marks plus italics, one line per speech.
20. Thoughts: italics only, one line.
21. Sounds: bold onomatopoeia on its own line.
22. System readouts: fenced code blocks.
23. No blank lines between prose paragraphs.
24. Preserve every source sentence; do not cut or condense unless asked.
25. Do not skip or condense battles unless asked.
26. Do not delete files unless asked.
27. Tight third person through Max; other POV only when the scene requires it.
28. Enemy soldiers are simply bad; no sympathetic commanders.
29. Enemies fight with genuine tactical competence.
30. Enemy armies never run out of soldiers.
31. Max changes outcomes only through decisive action at a decisive point.
32. Mass kills alone do not flip battle outcomes.
33. Enemy strategy does not update from displays of personal power.
34. HUD voice is neutral female; facts and percentages only, no personality.
35. UI suspends during active danger and resumes when clear.
36. Magic belief by common soldiers is hugely exaggerated; write rumor as rumor.
37. Use `Magic.md` tables for what named mages actually do on page.
38. Chapter header: `# Chapter N`, Arc, Date, `---`.
39. Canonical system readout format is fenced code blocks, not blockquotes.
40. Edits default to cursor line minus 1 through plus 5 unless a wider range is named.
41. No drive-by cleanup outside the named edit window.
