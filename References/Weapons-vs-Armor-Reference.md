# Weapons vs Armor Reference

Combat reference for *Trenches, Guns and Magic*. Muzzle energy figures are approximate. Penetration labels: Scratch, Dent, Damage, Penetrates, Destroys, Lethal, Stun, Heavy Stun, Inconvenience, Nothing.

## Armor Targets

- **Magical Human:** Indestructible rigid plate; blunt trauma only. No penetration possible except Panzerfaust (normal penetration rules apply to Panzerfaust).
- **Truck:** 1 to 3mm mild steel cab, 20 to 30mm cast iron engine block
- **Renault FT:** 6 to 16mm mild steel | **Mark V:** 6 to 12mm mild steel | **A7V:** 20 to 30mm mild steel
- **Mech:** 70mm mild steel WWI quality (~45 to 50mm RHA equivalent). Joints and vision slits are weak points.
- **Aircraft:** Fabric/wood frame = no armor; aluminum skin = 1 to 2mm; armored pilot seat = 6mm mild steel (late WWI only)
- **Destroyer:** 6 to 25mm mild steel | **Light Cruiser:** 25 to 75mm Harvey/Krupp steel | **Battlecruiser:** 150 to 230mm Krupp cemented | **Dreadnought:** 200 to 305mm belt / 25 to 75mm deck, Krupp cemented

## Magical Armor: Blunt Trauma Rules

Applies to **Magical Human** targets (enchanted plate infantry, Northumberland style full armor, and similar). Panzerfaust and shaped charge hits use normal penetration rules, not blunt trauma only.

### Assumptions

- Armor is **indestructible and perfectly rigid**. It does not deform, crack, or penetrate under any hit listed below unless stated.
- All damage is transmitted purely as blunt force / pressure wave through the armor to the body beneath.
- Rules apply to **single shots** unless otherwise noted.
- **Multiple shots** means repeated impacts to the same area in quick succession.

### Weapon Classes (circa 1913)

#### Light Pistol

**Examples:** .32 ACP, .380 ACP  
**Muzzle energy:** ~170 to 260 J

| Target | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Head (helmet) | Minor inconvenience: ringing, brief disorientation at most |
| Torso (plate) | Nothing: target may not even feel it meaningfully |

#### Heavy Pistol

**Examples:** 9mm Parabellum, .45 ACP, Webley Mk VI (.455)  
**Muzzle energy:** ~480 to 570 J

| Target | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Head (helmet) | **Stun:** concussion, knockdown, temporary incapacitation |
| Torso (plate) | Nothing: bruising at most, not combat relevant |

#### Battle Rifle

**Examples:** SMLE No.1 Mk III (.303 British), Gewehr 98 (7.92x57mm Mauser)  
**Muzzle energy:** ~3,300 to 4,000 J

| Target | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Head (helmet) | **Heavy stun:** severe concussion, likely unconsciousness, possible fatal TBI on repeat hits |
| Torso (plate) | **Stun:** serious blunt trauma, cracked ribs, breath knockoff, temporary incapacitation |

**Multiple shots (same area):** Cumulative trauma can escalate one level.

- Torso: stun escalates toward serious internal injury / incapacitation
- Head: heavy stun escalates toward lethal TBI
- Sufficient repeated impacts to the same point may eventually compromise even magical armor at the editor's discretion

#### .50 BMG

**Examples:** Early heavy machine gun / anti materiel use  
**Muzzle energy:** ~18,000 J

| Target | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Head (helmet) | **Lethal:** fatal traumatic brain injury regardless of penetration |
| Torso (plate) | **Heavy stun:** massive blunt trauma, likely unconsciousness, serious internal injury, not immediately lethal but severely incapacitating |

#### 20mm Autocannon

**Muzzle energy:** ~50,000+ J

| Target | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Head (helmet) | **Lethal:** fatal regardless of penetration |
| Torso (plate) | **Penetrates and kills:** sufficient focused energy to defeat even magical armor; lethal regardless of penetration |

#### Artillery, Naval Guns, Panzerfaust

| Target | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Head or torso | **Lethal** (77mm field gun and above, all naval calibers) |
| Head or torso, Panzerfaust | **Penetrates, lethal** (shaped charge; normal penetration rules) |

### Effect Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nothing** | No meaningful combat effect |
| **Inconvenience** | Ringing, minor disorientation, does not remove from action |
| **Stun** | Concussion, knockdown, temporarily out of action |
| **Heavy stun** | Severe concussion, unconsciousness, extended incapacitation |
| **Lethal** | Fatal injury; death within seconds to minutes |
| **Penetrates, lethal** | Armor is defeated; direct fatal wound |
