# Chapter 8

Arc: Seville

Date: Late January 1914, past midnight

"*Charge!*"

Max was the first to leap from behind cover without the slightest hesitation. He held a rifle with bayonet fixed and led his remaining 20 odd soldiers in a counter charge against the enemy down the hillside.

Max roared again when the distance between the two sides closed to about 50 meters.

"*Grenadiers, throw grenades!*"

Several soldiers carrying ball grenades immediately threw these deadly iron spheres with all their strength. Max threw his own grenade alongside them. The grenades flew much farther than usual taking advantage of their terrain. The grenades landed precisely in the front ranks of the Northumberland Fusilier Regiment.

**BOOM BOOM BOOM**

A series of explosions rang out. Max's grenade struck a Fusilier directly and detonated. The heavily armored soldiers at the forefront along with the infantry behind them were instantly blown to pieces.

Their heavy armor protected them from most of the shrapnel. They were not truly indestructible however. Sharp fragments still managed to penetrate through areas not protected by the armor. The massive impact and noise from the close range explosion caused these armored units to experience a brief period of dizziness and confusion.

Max and the volunteers of the International Brigades plunged fiercely into the Northumberland Fusiliers' formation from two directions like two sharp daggers.

The soldiers of the 1st Battalion who had been struggling to hold out also saw the battle situation here at the top of the hill. Major Thomas drew his pistol and let out a deafening roar when the soldiers of the Royal Army rushed to within 100 meters of the position.

"*German warriors! For the glory of the Empire! Attack!*"

The remaining gray military uniforms converged into a resolute torrent and surged down from the high ground.

The gray tide and the black tide collided violently.

The mixed colors and a small patch of field gray were also mixed with a large clump of khaki tide on the other side.

A fierce and bloody hand to hand battle was about to begin.

The commander of the 1st Battalion, 2nd Regiment of the International Brigades' Eastern European Brigade clearly noticed the charge of Max and his small group. They quickly moved in this direction and joined forces to prevent Max and his group from being overwhelmed in an instant therefore.

Max had never seen what units clad in heavy armor and wielding melee weapons could do on a battlefield where bullets were flying previously. However now he knew.

Max stared at the British armored units ignoring the bullets and charging straight into the International Brigades' formation. The armored soldiers wore steel plate covering their torsos, shoulders and thighs. Their helmets enclosed their heads completely. The armor left gaps at the joints for movement. The armored soldiers knocked the front line soldiers to their knees. The greatswords they wielded were clearly far more powerful in close combat than the bayonets on rifles.

Heads and limbs flew off one after another as the swords flashed. No one could stop these armored units it seemed.

Max charged into the enemy ranks. He felt as if his blood was on fire. The splattering blood and shrill screams around him seemed to be his most effective stimulant. They made him more and more courageous in battle.

The bayonet drills from the German Military Academy guided his charge.

A soldier from the Royal Army charged at him with a gun, yelling, his bayonet pointed directly at his chest.

Max's own experience took over.

Max stepped to the side and angled his bayonet forward in one fluid movement. The soldier's momentum carried him straight onto the blade. The sharp steel plunged deep into his chest.

Max kicked the corpse aside without a moment's hesitation. He drew his bayonet and aimed and fired at an enemy soldier not far away.

**BANG**

The enemy toppled to the ground.

Two more enemies rushed Max's position. Max snorted and dropped his rifle. He drew his P08 and Webley and fired into the enemies, dropping them to the ground.

Unfortunately Max's little show attracted the attention of a more troublesome enemy.

A heavily armored soldier wielding a two handed greatsword roared. His heavy footsteps caused the ground to tremble slightly as he charged straight toward Max.

Max raised his hand and fired several shots. The rounds struck uselessly against the soldier's helmet and chest plate.

Max did not have time to appreciate the durability of the armor. He ducked beneath a wide slash, picking up his rifle as he tumbled away.

Max turned around and fired at the armored helm. The round left a dent and bounced off. The shot did not kill the enemy but it stunned him. Max juked around him and got a round off into the man's calf, blowing a hole into it.

The wound did not stop the man for long. He muscled through the pain and closed the distance by force. The heavily armored greatswordsman began his attack. His slashes were wide and powerful. Each strike came with a whistling sound. The heavy equipment severely hampered his speed.

The bayonet was no match for the two handed greatsword's strikes. Max gritted his teeth and kept dodging, searching for an opening in his opponent's defenses.

His opponent's powerful sweeping attack missed. Max seized the fleeting opportunity.

Max lunged forward. His bayonet flashed like a hawk, slipping precisely into the gap under the opponent's arm.

The sound of a bayonet piercing flesh was audible.

The heavily armored soldier let out a painful groan and froze.

Max took another step forward without hesitation. He pressed the muzzle of the Gewehr 98 against the wound and pulled the trigger.

**BANG**

Point blank rifle fire is muffled yet deadly.

The 7.92mm bullet pierced the heavily armored soldier's body without hindrance, following the wound torn open by the bayonet.

The massive body trembled violently before crashing to the ground with a deafening thud. The heavy armor slammed into the earth.

Max twisted the bayonet to make sure the Fusilier did not get up. He grabbed his sidearms before rejoining the fray.

The battle situation on the high ground underwent a decisive change meanwhile. The Royal Army soldiers whose morale had been shattered by Max and his men's machine guns were already on their last legs. They faced the 1st Battalion soldiers charging down from the high ground with high morale. The Royal Army soldiers collapsed at the first touch. They were no match for these well trained German soldiers in close combat and were quickly routed. The Royal Army lost their armor and weapons and suffered another defeat.

Major Thomas defeated the enemy in front of him. He noticed that Max and his men were in a tough fight.

"*The entire 1st Battalion, follow me! Go support the left flank!*"

Major Thomas made a decisive move. He gathered his troops and immediately charged toward the flank of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment.

The main force of the 1st Battalion added their strength. The pressure on Max and the others was greatly reduced. They had originally been at a significant disadvantage in terms of numbers. The numerical gap between the two sides was quickly narrowed.

The fighting tenacity of the Northumberland rifle soldiers far exceeded everyone's expectations. These elite soldiers from Britain did not break down even when surrounded on three sides.

The Northumberland Fusiliers quickly tightened their formation. They formed small circular defensive arrays centered around the heavily armored soldiers. The Fusiliers stubbornly resisted attacks from all directions.

Bayonets clashed with longswords. Rifle butts struck shields. The sounds of rifle fire were interspersed.

The battlefield turned into a giant bloody meat grinder, frantically devouring the lives of soldiers on both sides.

Every second someone fell.

Max was letting himself go in the melee.

Max gripped his rifle tightly with both hands. He used all his strength with each thrust. He aimed straight for the enemy's vitals.

Sergeant Klaus and Sergeant Jonah were equally fearless. They repeatedly thrust their bayonets into the enemy's body with the simplest and most direct stabbing maneuvers.

The battle reached a strange stalemate.

The Germans and International Brigades gradually gained the upper hand in numbers. They were never able to completely defeat this tenacious British force.

Time passed second by second. Max felt his strength rapidly draining away. His arms became incredibly sore from the repeated blocking and thrusting. Even with his recent upgrades and the energy he could feel coming from the ground and with every breath, it was not enough to match the level of exertion.

Max did not understand why the brigade headquarters had clearly dispatched two Armored Knights to follow the 1st Battalion. These elite units had not come up to provide support at this time.

*What are they waiting for?*

A rapid and dense sound of hooves came from the other side of the high ground just then. The sound rolled like thunder.

Max instinctively looked in that direction.

A German black eagle flag was fluttering in the wind on the other side of the high ground.

A dark mass of cavalrymen stood under the military flag.

They wore field gray cavalry uniforms and metal tipped cavalry helmets. They held 3 meter long lances aloft.

The 52nd Cavalry Regiment of the German Empire Army.

The cavalry commander took the lead. He pressed his lance down so that the tip drew a dazzling arc in the air.

"*Charge!*"

Hundreds of cavalrymen launched a simultaneous charge upon receiving the order.

The earth trembled under their iron hooves. Their overwhelming force left everyone breathless.

The 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment was entangled with the German infantry and the International Brigades. Their rear was exposed to this torrent without any defense.

The tenacity and resolve on the faces of these British soldiers were finally replaced by terror and despair at this moment.

The British soldiers wanted to turn around and reorganize their defenses. It was too late.

The cavalry arrived in an instant like a tidal wave.

The cavalrymen at the forefront had already lowered their spears.

The sharp spearheads gleamed with a deadly cold light in the morning sun. The spears easily pierced the bodies of the British soldiers.

The enormous impact sent them flying high into the air before slamming them heavily to the ground.

The cavalry following behind brandished their sabers. They looked down upon the enemy and took lives in the blink of an eye.

Many heavily armored soldiers wielded their greatswords and cut down the German cavalrymen and their horses. They could only shatter the part in front of them like rocks in a tide. They were unable to stop the spread of the entire tide.

The cavalry completed a charge along an arc shaped tangent. The 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers was caught off guard. A shark had suddenly swum in and bitten them. A gap appeared in their ranks.

The final straw that broke the camel's back finally appeared.

The line of the 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment collapsed instantly under the charge of the cavalry.

---

Hundreds of meters away from this blood soaked hill, a pair of eyes silently observed everything happening on the battlefield through binoculars. The observer stood in a wooded area.

Ludwig stood on the shoulder of an Armored Knight kneeling on one knee. This steel behemoth was painted with the black cross emblem of the Teutonic Knights. This was one of the same two that Max had seen earlier.

"*Ludwig, if we do not act soon, this infantry battalion will be wiped out.*"

Wilhelm stood beside him on another Armored Knight. He lowered his binoculars. His tone was tinged with anxiety.

"*Their casualties can bring greater value.*"

Ludwig spoke calmly. His gaze never left his binoculars.

"*If we rush out now, we will just get tangled up with the infantry and become passive. If we want to complete this hunt, patience is the best weapon.*"

"*Then how long do we have to wait?*"

Wilhelm was somewhat puzzled. He wondered if he could remain as calm as his comrade.

"*Not much longer.*"

"*Stop keeping me in suspense and just tell me.*"

Ludwig heard his comrade's anxious tone. He put down his binoculars.

"*The 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment is still half intact. The British commander cannot stand by and watch this meritorious unit be wiped out. He will definitely send someone to rescue them. Whether it is the Highland Mages, their Armored Knights, or another full strength battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment, those will be the prey worth our attention.*"

Ludwig raised the telescope again after saying that.

The battle situation changed suddenly not long afterward as if to prove his words.

The 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers was about to be wiped out by the siege of Max and the others and the repeated attacks of the cavalry.

Four elegantly designed all white Armored Knights appeared behind them.

Twelve knight attendants wearing powered armor surrounded these Armored Knights.

The elite forces of Britain. The Order of the Garter.

Ludwig's eyes lit up instantly. He put down his binoculars. His eyes filled with barely suppressed excitement.

"*We have hit the jackpot! All hands prepare for battle! Attendant, fire three red signal flares!*"

Ludwig and Wilhelm deftly flipped over and slipped into the cockpits of their respective Armored Knights upon receiving the order.

The heavy front armor closed again. Ludwig and Wilhelm activated the Armored Knights by feel alone in the darkness before the lights turned on.

Several magical pathways were connected beneath the armor plates. The magical muscle bundles embedded in the torso and limbs were also activated.

All the magical pathways completed a self check. The view in front of Ludwig and Wilhelm changed from the dark interior of the cockpit to an external view of about 180 degrees.

They completed the final startup steps.

The internal combustion engines that powered the two steel behemoths emitted a deep roar in the silent woods. Thick black smoke billowed from the exhaust pipes on the knights' backs.

Six knight attendants dressed in the Teutonic Knights' standard powered armor stood up around them. Three of them quickly raised their signal lances and pulled the triggers into the sky.

**FWOOSH FWOOSH FWOOSH**

Three dazzling red orbs rose into the sky. The flares stood out prominently in the empty spaces of the battlefield.

---

Max was engaged in fierce fighting in the meat grinder. He noticed the newly appeared white Armored Knights and the enemies around them who were better equipped than the heavily armored soldiers.

A red soldier icon and detailed information popped up on the system map.

```
Affiliation: British Empire â€” Order of the Garter

Saint George Type 3 Armored Knight x4

Broadsword Type 2 Powered Armor (Knight Attendant) x12

Anti-Infantry, Anti-Armor, High-Threat Unit
```

"*Wait, Armored Knights are one thing, but powered armor too? Is this even in the same style as our equipment?*"

The information provided by the system completely stunned Max. This was a unit that infantry could absolutely not fight against.

Max and his men had only a few unused grenades. They had no other high powered weapons. They had no anti armor weapons.

Rifle bullets were barely a tickle to those heavily armored infantrymen. These seemingly incredibly well defended behemoths were far worse.

The remaining soldiers of the 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment erupted in thunderous cheers upon seeing reinforcements arrive. Their morale soared instantly.

They were like they had been injected with adrenaline. The Fusiliers began a frantic counterattack. The German infantry and the International Brigades had just been in the lead. They suddenly showed signs of collapse.

Two sharp whistling sounds rang out at this critical moment.

**BOOM BOOM**

Two shells landed precisely in the ranks of the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment's counterattack. The violent explosions threw up a cloud of blood and mud.

Max instinctively turned around to look.

Two black and white Armored Knights appeared on the slope not far behind him. They were Armored Knights belonging to the Teutonic Knights of the German Empire.

One of the knights knelt on the ground. He held a weapon that looked like a rifle but was actually about the same caliber as a field cannon. Smoke rose from the muzzle.

The other knight had just finished loading. The knight skillfully pulled the straight pull bolt. He ejected a huge bronze cartridge case before aiming the muzzle at the enemy below the hillside once again.

The two Armored Knights finished reloading. They fired several more shots into the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment's formation. The shots shattered their hard won formation once again.

This direct fire support from heavy units instantly crushed the morale that the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment had just begun to build.

The four Armored Knights of the Order of the Garter saw this scene down the hillside. They stopped their rescue efforts. The knights sped up and charged toward the two Teutonic Knights Armored Knights that had opened fire.

The atmosphere on the battlefield became incredibly eerie at that moment.

The infantrymen on both sides were engaged in a fierce battle. They seemed to receive some silent command and tacitly disengaged.

They did not even have time to collect their comrades' bodies before retreating to the rear.

The German cavalry made their final charge. They turned their horses around without hesitation and galloped off in another direction. The cavalry left the battlefield.

Max looked in the direction they had gone. He found that it was the location of the hidden magic crystal cannon position that he had marked earlier.

Max could not care less about the movements of the cavalry at the moment. He commanded his troops to retreat to the higher ground.

"*Quick! Follow us! Let us retreat back to higher ground!*"

Max would not forget the allies of the International Brigades. Max saw that his 3rd Platoon had begun to retreat. He immediately waved his hand and shouted loudly to the equally bewildered International Brigades soldiers. They retreated to the top of the hill together.

The remaining 600 men of the 1st Battalion and the International Brigades withdrew to the high ground. A bearded man who appeared to be the commander of the International Brigades took the initiative to shake hands with Max.

Everyone obediently lay down on the high ground. They watched the duel between the Armored Knights below with the same tension as spectators.

The four Armored Knights of the Order of the Garter were preparing to charge and take out the two annoying bugs in one fell swoop. They suddenly stopped in their tracks.

Max and the others had retreated to the high ground. They watched this scene with some confusion. The answer was revealed.

More Teutonic Knights Armored Knights emerged from their hiding places in several other directions of this battle zone.

The dark cannon muzzles pointed at the four Armored Knights of the Order of the Garter from all directions. The Teutonic Knights formed a loose encirclement.
