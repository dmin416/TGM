# Chapter 14

Arc: Seville Counterattack

Date: Late January 1914

---

*"All Highland mages, follow me!"*

Eldridge's cold voice carried across the camp.

Two other Highland mages and two mage apprentices followed at once without delay.

Eldridge looked at the two young apprentices and thought again of the apprentice killed by an enemy trap in the small building.

All three apprentices were seedlings he had chosen himself and trained himself.

He believed that with their talent they could in time reach his own level of achievement and widen his faction within the mage corps.

This deployment to the Kingdom of Aragon was also meant to temper his apprentices and add overseas service to their records.

That helped Highland mage apprentices win promotion.

Before this they had not even seen the enemy when one apprentice died in a blast.

For Eldridge, who had invested so much in them, that loss was severe.

He turned his head slightly toward his two remaining apprentices and spoke a warning.

*"Stay alert and stay careful. Do not grow careless like Brad. In an emergency you may withdraw from the fight. Do you understand?"*

*"Yes, Instructor."*

*"Yes, Instructor."*

As the five Highland mages prepared to move out the newly replenished Northumberland Fusiliers battalion also went forward on Colonel Henry's order.

The British expedition meant to crush the resistance inside Seville with absolute numbers.

Max was studying the distribution of both sides on his system map and preparing to chew off a British platoon through tight control when the map for the first time flashed a warning.

```
Warning! High threat unit approaching! Count: 5!
```

He shrank the map at once and saw five special markers for spellcasters appear among the red enemy placards and close in.

One icon carried a purple high risk mark.

*Damn it. Highland mages! Have they come to the front line?*

Max's worst fear had come to pass.

The British had clearly been driven to desperation by the string of losses and had sent their casters forward.

Before he could react the five icons reached Seville's urban districts at a speed beyond reason.

The high risk mark for Highland Mage Corps senior instructor Eldridge drove in a near straight line into the interior of the city.

Max guessed at once that Eldridge was using the same flight spell Max had seen outside the walls before.

*What does he mean to do?*

Max stared at the map and tried to read the man's next move.

A second later Eldridge's icon halted near the tallest church in the city center.

Then a squad sized friendly placard on the church side turned grey.

A violent explosion and a tremor through the ground followed from far away.

**Boom rumble!**

A lookout soon reached Max with terror on his face.

*"Platoon leader! The church bell tower! A huge fireball fell from the sky and leveled it!"*

Max had expected as much.

That bell tower was the most important height and observation post in the city. It overlooked most of the districts and gave the garrison precious battlefield information.

Now it was gone.

The senior mage had meant to tear out the defenders' eyes and ears first.

*His next step is likely a decapitation strike.*

Max understood the intent at once.

Without the high ground the command post would lose much of its sense of what was happening inside the city.

A flying senior mage could cross line after line of defense with ease and deliver a devastating blow against brigade headquarters.

*"Run to brigade headquarters! Find a way to warn them that enemy senior mages are attempting a decapitation strike! Tell them to relocate at once!"*

Max shouted to the messenger at his side.

*"Yes, sir!"*

The messenger turned and ran without delay.

On the map the other two Highland mages and the two apprentices split apart and dropped onto different axes of advance.

They clearly meant to use their magic to support the ground advance.

That did not match the usual practice of the Highland Mage Corps but it was happening all the same.

Fighting inside Seville turned fiercer than it had been at any point so far.

On a street held by the International Brigades a Highland mage descended from the sky into the midst of Northumberland Fusiliers soldiers.

He did not even seek cover. He stood in the center of the street with open arrogance.

*"Open fire! Kill the robed one!"*

International Brigade fighters in the buildings on both sides already knew British Highland mages were not invincible. They opened fire on him with bold haste.

Every bullet stopped half a meter from his body against a translucent blue shield and threw ripples across its surface.

*"Damn it! That shield again!"*

Andrei saw the scene through binoculars and struck the window frame in fury.

The mage ignored the incoming fire. He only lifted his hand toward the building pouring the heaviest fire and chanted a few syllables.

A fireball the size of a human head formed before his palm and screamed away.

**Boom!**

The windows of a three story building burst open at once. Blast and flame swallowed the room.

A squad of International Brigade soldiers inside had no time to cry out before the swelling fire took them.

The same scene played out on another street held by German soldiers.

Two mage apprentices became the core of the attacking force.

Defenders kept firing from inside the buildings and even threw hand grenades and firebombs to concentrate fire on the two casters.

Though the infantry around them kept falling the two apprentices remained unharmed.

Rifle bullets, grenade fragments and the flames of firebombs all broke against the same seemingly fragile blue shield.

In street fighting it was hard to reach the density of hundreds of rifles firing together so Mage Armor was harder to break than it had been before.

One Highland mage apprentice cast *Life Sense* on a building whose windows still flashed with fire. In a flat voice he reported the defenders' positions to the British officer beside him.

*"Six on the second floor of the building on the left. Five on the third."*

That most basic spell, first developed in ancient times for exploration, had become a deadly advantage in the streets.

*"Riflemen suppress! Heavy armor, prepare to assault!"*

The British officer gave the order at once with precise intelligence in hand.

Northumberland Fusiliers soldiers began to pour fire into the windows of that building.

These elite men shot far better than the Royal Army soldiers who had come before them.

Though they all carried bolt action rifles their rate of fire was very fast. Dense bullet rain pinned the defenders behind the windows and left them no chance to answer.

**"Aaaaaah!"**

*"I want to go back."*

Bullets came through the windows and threw plaster dust into the air. The crackle of impacts filled the room.

German soldiers caught under that fire had never faced anything so terrible. Several dropped to the floor in fear and tried to crawl toward the door.

Heavy soldiers in full plate armor took the chance. They strode forward through scattered fire with greatswords in hand.

Their two handed blades smashed through sealed doors and windows. Infantry behind them poured into the building.

Soon fierce gunfire and screams sounded inside.

Other heavy soldiers advanced straight into fire from other directions and used their tools to tear down barbed wire and street obstacles.

At that sight a 77 millimeter field gun hidden in rubble at a street corner was pushed out by the artillery team posted there.

The crew sheltered behind the gun shield from incoming bullets. The men loaded a high explosive shell with nervous speed and fired by direct lay at once. The burst covered the whole area.

Several heavy soldiers working the obstacle line fell at once and did not rise again.

Their deaths did not frighten the rest.

More heavy soldiers with cold eyes came up from behind and continued the work.

They moved like war machines without feeling and cleared the path ahead with their own flesh and blood.

For British soldiers who had once broken other imperial armies on the Thin Red Line skill and will both stood far above the Royal Army troops who had fought here earlier.

Pressure on the defenders rose at once.

On his map Max saw several friendly positions near 3rd Platoon forced to give ground.

The line was tearing open bit by bit.

*"Platoon leader! The International Brigades on the left cannot hold! They request support!"*

*"2nd Platoon on the right reports a Highland mage on their front. Casualties are heavy!"*

Messengers kept bringing bad news and Max's face grew darker.

He looked at the map and saw red enemy arrows closing on 3rd Platoon's sector from both sides in a pincer.

If he did not act soon 3rd Platoon's position would become a salient and draw encirclement.

*"We cannot wait any longer!"*

Max decided at once.

He seized his rifle and shouted to the two squads of reserves still with him. *"Follow me! We are going to put out the fire!"*

He led fewer than twenty men out of the temporary hideout at once. They moved through the buildings toward the hardest pressed ground on the left.
