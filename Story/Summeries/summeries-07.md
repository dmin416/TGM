# Summary 7 (Chapter 7)

Arc: Seville. Late January 1914, past midnight into dawn, assault on Seville outskirts.

* Max wakes after midnight to pea soup with tinned beef; Hans reports break camp at 4:30 and that Hauser ordered Max not be disturbed; Max insists messengers wake him anyway.
* At 4:30 third platoon joins the night march; dawn brings German 105mm howitzers bracketing and destroying Royal mana crystal cannon positions using observation balloons and vehicles, while two divisions close on Seville.
* General Sanjurjo learns most crystal guns are gone and orders defense as messengers report a full attack on the southeastern line; Hauser leads third company in the brigade assault.
* First battalion takes the smashed crystal hill with little resistance; Thomas orders the battalion to hold the high ground as an observation post.
* Max disperses his platoon twenty meters apart, warns Hauser the hill will be shelled, and Hauser asks Thomas to spread the battalion.
* Three British style airburst magic crystal shells devastate the concentrated first and second companies; third company and HQ are hit; second squad is wiped out and other squads take losses.
* A hidden farm battery, guided by a Highland mage apprentice, fires again on hill 125; Max sends coordinates to German artillery and reorganizes defense as Royal troops counterattack.
* Max flanks with two squads and the captured Maxim, leaving Baumann and others on the ridge; enfilade fire breaks the Royal charge with heavy casualties.
* Max relocates the gun under cover, notes doctrine and gear limits, and sees German gains on the map but no sign yet of British elites he expects.
* Khaki Northumberland Fusiliers 4th Battalion with armored knights advance to the Grenadier March; Max turns the last belt on the British, rifles on the Royal Army, and finds armor shrugs many hits.
* International Brigade 2nd Regiment hits the British flank; the Maxim runs dry, Max orders bayonets and charges rather than abandon the allies.
