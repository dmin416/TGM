# Summary 9 (Chapter 9)

Arc: Seville Counterattack. Late January 1914, armored duel and entry into Seville.

* Infantry fall silent as twelve Teutonic knights bombard four Order of the Garter white knights with 30mm arm guns; the British charge under rippling mage shields until the fire breaks them and melee armor fighting begins.
* Teutonic hybrid knights trade four fallen machines and heavy squire losses to destroy Garter squires and two knights; two British knights try to break out.
* Highland instructor Eldridge casts fifth ring Transmute Rock, turning ground to mud and trapping the pursuers; the surviving Garter knights withdraw toward Seville.
* Max orders the first battalion, third platoon and allies to volley Eldridge at three hundred meters; Mage Armor cracks under mass rifle fire and he breaks off when white retreat flares rise from the city.
* Soldiers cheer; Ludwig salutes the hill; messengers report cavalry hit the hidden mana crystal position; stuck knights are dug out while crystal fire stays silent.
* Regimental orders pursue a full enemy retreat; International Brigade and Teutonic knights join the battered first battalion into Seville with almost no resistance and the city abandoned.
* Max warns of a trap; Thomas and Ludwig argue tactical withdrawal and counterattack because Seville cannot be given up; allies mop up and camp outside.
* Third platoon is down to forty eight men; Sergeant Jonah is dead; Max sends wounded to hospital, Klaus to rearm especially grenades, Baumann to compile casualty lists, then feeds the platoon fried Thuringian sausage and coffee.
* Max, Klaus and Hans climb the cathedral bell tower; Ludwig shares the view, gossip about party Max versus battlefield Max, amnesia beatings in Seville, Cecilia's telegram to Mackensen, and sister Patricia's rejection.
* Seville civilians celebrate National Army and International Brigade; second battalion Germans loot a house after heavy officer losses and hunger, nearly clash guns with allies.
* Max uses Wild Shape pressure, strips rifles, shames the looters in German and apologizes in Spanish to allies and civilians; Thomas and Ludwig back discipline and alliance; Ludwig leaves on armored knight technical duty.
