# Summary 12 (Chapter 12)

Arc: Seville Counterattack. Late January 1914, street fighting and counter battery.

* Royal conscript Daniel and the first assault hit interlocking fire from buildings, sealed ground floors, an MG08 down Sun Avenue, and grenades dropped through chimneys into packed houses; Daniel dies in the street.
* The attack collapses within a hundred meters; Sanjurjo rages at heavy losses, unfamiliar building defense, and British Northumberland troops pulling back without fighting.
* He orders a second wave and reckless magic crystal high explosive shelling of northern Seville, collapsing blocks while most garrison waits in deep cover and cellars per Max's doctrine; observers in the cathedral bell tower spot the hidden battery.
* Whistles recall defenders to second line positions through interior passages; Royal troops advance through ruins thinking the bombardment killed the defenders.
* Colonel Braun's 52nd Cavalry locates the firing position, brings up twelve mobile 77mm guns, destroys the immobile crystal battery, then charges with two battalions.
* Highland instructor Eldridge arrives with two mages, lifts the lead horsemen into the air and slams them into the ground, killing Braun and breaking the cavalry strike.
