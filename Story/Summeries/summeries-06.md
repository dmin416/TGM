# Summary 6 (Chapter 6)

Arc: Seville. Late January 1914, afternoon through night, recon return and brigade decision.

* The recon team reaches their hidden bicycles; Max and Baumann climb the hill again and spot Royal officers and robed figures at the mana crystal gun line.
* The system identifies General José Sanjurjo, Highland Mage instructor Eldridge, apprentices and staff; Max considers sniping them at four hundred meters but shooters and mage protection make it too risky.
* Eldridge senses killing intent and looks toward the hill; Max orders a fast bicycle withdrawal.
* At Juan's booby trapped building Royal soldiers trigger a stairwell grenade; a mage apprentice casts Life Perception, finds dead upstairs, then Mage Armor before opening doors.
* Separating Juan's body from another officer trips seven grenades; the apprentice dies and a second blast alarms Eldridge and Sanjurjo outside.
* Sanjurjo orders the city sealed and searched, which diverts troops and eases Max's escape.
* At battalion HQ Max reports no ambush on the road but admits going into Seville; Thomas and Hauser are stunned until Max presents Juan's ID, revolver and detailed intelligence on defenses, the 24th Division, British garrison, mages and mana crystal cannons.
* Max marks the map from copied notes to hide the system source and urges delaying the assault; Thomas agrees but cannot decide alone and takes Max to regiment then brigade HQ.
* At the 16th Brigade, Paul praises Max's work, notes Mackensen's observer assignment, and still orders the Seville attack unchanged after liaison officers from the National Army and International Brigade demand action and staff argue artillery can outrange the crystal guns.
* Mackensen has already left by train; attack positions tonight, assault tomorrow morning.
* Alone, Paul shows his chief of staff a General Staff telegram: take Seville regardless of cost and commit Zeppelin armored airship L30 after resupply, driven by the Aetherium vein under the city.
* Max returns to company, gets told to sleep, and opens queued system notices: level 1, cantrips and first ring locked without a spellbook, XP for Juan, six soldiers and the mage apprentice, Druid Circle of the Land Natural Recovery passive, and L30 arriving in about fifteen hours; he falls asleep as recovery eases his aches.
