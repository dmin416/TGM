# Summary 5 (Chapter 5)

Arc: San Isidro. Late January 1914, afternoon, recon toward Seville.

* Max's six man bicycle recon team leaves San Isidro; a system minimap opens and fog of war clears as friendly sight feeds it.
* They hide bikes near Alcolea and enter on foot; the village looks peaceful with no sign of war.
* Baumann's Spanish fails with a farmer; Max discovers automatic translation and recovered memories of the original owner's fluent Spanish, French and other salon languages.
* The farmer reports a few hundred Royal Army soldiers in black passed east toward Seville about an hour ago.
* Following obvious march litter on the road they confirm the column went straight back without an ambush on the route.
* From a hill two kilometers out they see Seville and a resting Royal half battalion on high ground with lax security and British Comet Type I mana crystal artillery behind them.
* The system map shows more enemy formations southeast; the team has ridden deep inside a suburban defensive ring around the city.
* Max decides not to withdraw yet and uses the map to slip past patrols on foot into Seville's outer residential belt.
* They witness undisciplined Royal troops looting cafes and abusing civilians; officers drag three girls into a requisitioned building.
* Max leads a silent bayonet raid through the back; two officers die quickly, one battalion commander is captured alive.
* Interrogation in Spanish: Juan de Rivera, bought rank as lieutenant colonel of 1st Battalion, 126th Regiment, 24th Division; whole division in Seville with British Northumberland Fusiliers (two or three thousand) and more than a dozen robed mages.
* Max kills Juan after the answers, rescues and blankets the girls, and rigs grenade booby traps in the building.
* Max tells the girls to flee the city with their families, escorts them to a safer neighborhood, and the team slips out.
* A girl thanks him as a good person; Max tells Baumann that label is personal, not political, and deflects Baumann's questions about war aims with soldier's duty talk while privately accepting ongoing conflict.
