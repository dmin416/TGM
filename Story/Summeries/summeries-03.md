# Summary 3 (Chapter 3)

Arc: Battle 1. February 1914, pre dawn, assault on San Isidro.

* Twelve 77mm field guns bombard San Isidro; Max's intel map clears fog of war over the town as shells fall.
* On the whistle 1st Company advances in three skirmish lines; 2nd Company follows in dense column for a bayonet push once a breach opens.
* The assault crosses open ground with little resistance until rifle fire from the village edge drops men near two hundred meters out.
* Volley fire by the book slows the defenders and the attack seems to continue.
* A hidden Royal Army Maxim machine gun opens up and wipes 1st Company's lead platoon in seconds, then sweeps the rest of 1st and packed 2nd Company; Hauser is stunned.
* The regiment has few machine guns and did not bring them forward; Germans underestimated the weapon Britannia supplied to the Royal Army.
* Max spots the gun in a two story farmhouse on the map and through binoculars; artillery help is at least ten minutes away.
* A messenger orders 3rd Company, the reserve, into the same frontal meat grinder.
* Max asks Hauser to let 3rd Platoon flank left through a dry riverbed and brush to hit the gun from the blind side; another platoon leader backs the idea.
* Hauser agrees under threat of court martial if Max fails to rejoin the main force.
* Max leads the platoon in dispersed movement: first squad covers from a slope, second squad under Klaus holds the riverbed, Max takes two squads along the bed toward the farmhouse flank.
* Contact from the village is suppressed by the covering squads; crossfire from Klaus's position stops Royalist troops rushing Max's assault team.
* Corporal Jonah's fourth squad suppresses the farmhouse windows while Max's third squad grenades the second floor and the machine gun port; the Maxim icon goes dark and fire stops.
* Hauser and the survivors charge; 1st and 2nd Company remnants join a spread skirmish assault into the village.
* Max sends a messenger to pull Klaus and the other squads forward, warns him to stay low, then clears the farmhouse with door crack grenades, a volley, and room to room work upstairs.
* Dead gun crew and guards; the Maxim is largely intact and Max orders it moved downstairs as a platoon trophy with care not to be shot by friendlies.
* Squads reunite and celebrate around the farmhouse; Max fears casualties if the enemy counterattacks or artillery lands on the crowded group.
