# Summary 13 (Chapter 13)

Arc: Seville Counterattack. Late January 1914, British led assault and armored knight duel.

* The Royal Army second assault stalls on every axis with heavy losses on Sun Avenue and elsewhere; rubble and second line defenses still hold wire, traps and crossfire, including direct fire 77mm guns that rout attackers again.
* Eldridge confronts Sanjurjo for ordering the last magic crystal battery to fire unguarded, destroying it to cavalry; British Colonel Henry insults Spanish troops and takes command of the next attack.
* Eight Order of the Garter knights lead Northumberland infantry while Royal troops trail; Max orders defenders not to fight armor and to fall back for Teutonic knights.
* Rifle and 105mm fire barely scratches the white knights; one kicks through a barricade, another smashes Andrei's International Brigade building with a war hammer.
* Six Teutonic Siegfried knights appear; Colonel Marcus leads eight Garter Type 3 machines confident in technology and narrow streets.
* Ludwig duels Marcus with Max's shaped charge lance at twenty centimeters, penetrates the cockpit and drops him; similar kills remove four Garter knights while two German rounds misfire then one pilot uses a spare to finish a side shot.
* A fresh Garter knight disables a Teutonic knight after its shaped charges are spent, leaving four German machines against three British survivors who pull back.
* Seven Garter Type 3 knights are lost in the day including two from earlier; rear command is stunned; Henry refuses retreat and commits reserves while Eldridge heads to the front with magic.
