# Summary 14 (Chapter 14)

Arc: Seville Counterattack. Late January 1914, mages in the streets and Max's raid.

* Eldridge leads five Highland mages and a replenished Northumberland battalion forward; Max's map warns of five high threat casters approaching Seville.
* Eldridge destroys the cathedral bell tower observation post, then splits mages to support infantry; apprentices use Life Sense and Mage Armor while heavy infantry and accurate rifle fire breach German and International Brigade positions.
* Max sends a runner to warn brigade HQ of a decapitation strike, leads reserves to snipe British officers and briefly relieves the left flank, then orders Klaus to pull the platoon to the third defensive line while he takes twenty nine men to infiltrate behind the enemy.
* Max sets an L shaped ambush on an isolated British rear guard, repeats the trick on a relief column, and slows the advance while Klaus retreats with thirty six men left in the reinforced platoon.
* Klaus returns with a squad as Max ambushes a search party; mage apprentices detect the hideout with Life Sense, a building fight kills many British but two apprentices burn the first floor with fireballs, wiping twelve Germans.
* Out of mana, the apprentices flee; Max and Baumann bayonet both, loot spell books and reagents, and escape toward third platoon as system notes spell tab unlocked and Zeppelin L30 is about one hour out.
