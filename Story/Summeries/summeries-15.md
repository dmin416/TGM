# Summary 15 (Chapter 15)

Arc: Seville Counterattack. Late January 1914, last pocket and city hall fight.

* Max gathers stragglers through the map, rejoins Klaus at third platoon lines using password Weißwurst, and learns Royal soldiers in stolen German coats try to flee south because officers shoot retreaters.
* Friendly strength is below twelve thousand in isolated pockets; Max's sector holds brigade HQ at city hall with about seventy men left in third company after earlier platoon losses.
* Hauser names Max co commander; officers adopt urban crossfire plans while Max posts a National Army rear guard and moves third platoon forward.
* British assault hits city hall square; three howitzer and field gun salvos stall the column until the 105mm breech explodes and kills the crews, leaving three Garter knights shielding infantry in a wedge.
* Ludwig's two Siegfried knights ambush from a side lane with shaped charges and destroy the outer white machines; Max flanks the exposed British with rifles, grenades and firebombs in sealed buildings.
* Teutonic knights surround the last Garter knight; room fighting and firebombs burn British entries while Wilhelm's axe cripples the survivor's arm.
* Eldridge returns after finding his apprentices bayoneted, casts a massive fireball into Max's building, then uses a seventh ring Mordenkainen's Sword scroll to kill one Siegfried and shear off Ludwig's legs after splitting his shield.
* Wilhelm jumps his knight into Eldridge in the air, smashing him into British troops; riflemen cannot stop the last damaged Garter knight advancing on Ludwig's cockpit.
* Captain Hauser, grievously wounded, shoves Max the last shaped charge round and charges with two veterans; they detonate it on the knight at point blank, killing the pilot but Hauser loses an arm and dies with both veterans in the blast.
