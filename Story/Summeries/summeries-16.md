# Summary 16 (Chapter 16)

Arc: Seville Counterattack. Late January 1914, L30 arrives and aftermath.

* Wilhelm's last Siegfried charges to kill wounded Eldridge; Eldridge burns a sixth ring Disintegrate scroll and vaporizes the knight, then rallies British and Royal troops to storm city hall again.
* Zeppelin armored airship L30 breaks through the clouds; Max pulls Ludwig from his wrecked cockpit and fires Ludwig's Teutonic attack marker flare over the thickest British mass.
* L30's four twin 210mm turrets, bombs and deck MG08s shatter the charge and break enemy will; German counterattacks retake streets as the airship circles with searchlights and red flare coordination for street MG fire.
* Max's party finds half dead Eldridge; Max holds his failed antidote vial and bayonets him, loots advanced training manual, rings, potions, signet and apprentice style salvage while Klaus bags enchanted debris.
* Stretcher party carries Ludwig and barely alive Hauser; eleven of twelve Teutonic knights are dead; brigade radio confirms enemy outside the walls has broken.
* At night Max learns orderly Hans died; he opens Hauser's bundle (watch and farewell note) and recalls Hauser refusing Eldridge's green potion at the hospital before dying.
* Max reports to wounded Major Thomas as sole surviving third company officer with a reinforced platoon sized remnant; alone he opens the spell tab: caster capability at level 7 after killing apprentices and Eldridge, Spell Plague from mage slaying already cleared, third ring possible via two basic manuals which he uses at once.
