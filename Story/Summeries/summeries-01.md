# Summary 1 (Chapter 1)

Arc: Capture. Late January 1914, evening, Seville.

* Max wakes tied to a chair in a cellar while a Britannic officer beats and interrogates him about a German military attaché delegation in Seville.
* Past and present memories blur; he does not know where he is or why he is here.
* A neutral system voice announces intel collection and brings up a fogged map of Europe with Seville clear, plus tabs for Map, Intel, Encyclopedia and locked entries.
* Encyclopedia entries name Seville as capital of Andalusia in the Spanish Empire and the German Empire as a Wettin monarchy in central Europe.
* Intel states the attaché delegation was attacked and he is held in a Seville cellar; memories fill in as Maximilian Friedrich von Zähringen, new second lieutenant in the Royal Baden Cadet Corps, Karlsruhe, rank ten days old, diverted from reporting to his unit.
* Further blows suspend the HUD; he is kicked and questioned again about Germany and the Spanish civil war.
* An older, better dressed German prisoner speaks up: August von Mackensen, Imperial Army general.
* Mackensen claims diplomatic immunity and an invitation from Crown Prince Alfonso of the Spanish National Government; the Britannics reject that authority in Andalusia and demand why Germans are present.
* Argument escalates over the Holy Britannic Empire versus Germany, both empires having forces in Spain without open war between them.
* Mackensen and Max are beaten; pistols are held to their groins for a final demand about German entry into the civil war and deployment details.
* Max claims amnesia and low rank; the interrogator notes he was found with officers as low as major and a lieutenant general, which Max does not understand.
* A runner reports blockades under fire and German plus unknown allies advancing; prisoners are ordered moved to the rear.
* An explosion opens the cellar; armored German soldiers descend, kill the runner, block revolver fire with shields and cut down the three Britannic interrogators.
* Max asks them to help Mackensen first; both are freed, Mackensen carried because he cannot walk.
* Max loots IDs, papers, a revolver and ammunition from the dead before leaving.
* The house above is wrecked with many enemy dead; night fighting continues nearby.
* The rescue column includes plate armored troops with rifles and swords, ordinary German infantry and irregular allied fighters.
* Word comes that Britannic forces are breaking the feint and closing in; the captain orders withdrawal while a rear guard holds.
* Timed artillery destroys the house and the whole block, showing the mission would sacrifice the prisoners rather than let them be taken.
* The captain thanks the irregular leader, named in dialogue as the International Brigade, to cover the German retreat.
* Max reflects on the strange alliance and timeline as trucks, bicycles and horses evacuate wounded from the city outskirts.
* On the truck Mackensen recognizes Max from a Dresden ball, recalls a respected grandfather and a fading Zähringen line, and praises Max for staying silent under torture.
* Mackensen reveals a well connected woman telegrammed asking to keep Max in the rear; he refuses and assigns Max to a front line assault unit in the first wave.
* Max notices large silhouettes in the sky that may guide the shelling.
* The column reaches a wooded forward German camp with tents and artillery; Mackensen goes to the main command tent on his own while Max gets only brief medical attention.
