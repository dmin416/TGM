# Chapter 1

Arc: Capture

Date: Late January 1914, evening

**Bam!**

*"What is your military attaché delegation really doing in Seville?"*

Max came to when a fist landed on his face.

His head swam lightly as memories of his past and present life mixed together, though he noticed many gaps in his current life. As his eyes blinked open his vision cleared bit by bit. A tall Caucasian male stood in front of him wearing an old timey war uniform and looked very angry.

He thought to himself. *Where the hell am I this time? Did I die in my sleep this time? Where the hell am I now?*

The thoughts flashed through his mind as the pain flared across his face from what he assumed were several other blows to the face before he had awoken. He could tell from the pain that this wasn't a dream.

The angry man yelled at Max in British English that initially sounded hard to understand but quickly became clearer and clearer. Interestingly they didn't translate into English but into German in his head.

Max felt confused for a brief moment as he considered the possibilities.

*I've been many ranks but I've never started at second lieutenant before. If we are starting this high I might as well be a five star general. And what the hell is Seville. I've never heard of it before.*

As the name Seville crossed his mind, Max heard a neutral female voice speaking in his head.

> [Intel collection progress: 1%]
> [Intel management system: online]

The world seemed to pause for a brief moment and flicker in Max's vision as a game style display overlayed itself in his vision.

He could see a large map in the shape of Europe that was covered with a dense fog.

The only area that was clear was a single city to the west with a label matching what the man had said: Seville.

According to his knowledge this area should be a part of Spain.

Zooming in all the way, Max could see individual streets and buildings with himself in the center indicated by a small star.

To the left of the display was a set of tabs, this one labeled as Map.

Underneath the Map tab were an Intel tab and Encyclopedia and a few additional tabs with question marks on them.

Focusing on one of those mystery tabs, Max received a notification that his level of intel was too low to access them. Opening up the Encyclopedia instead, the map switched to a new nearly empty screen with only a few entries.

> [Seville, capital of Andalusia, population of 110,000. Fourth largest city in the Spanish Empire.]
> [German Empire, ruler of Saxony. A monarchy of central Europe ruled by the Wettin dynasty.]

Max raised his eyebrows.

*Oh boy. I'm dealing with the Spanish Empire and the Kaiserreich.*

He opened the Intel tab next which only had a single entry.

> [Your military attaché delegation was attacked. You were taken as prisoners. You are being held in a cellar in a home in Seville.]

Reading the entry unlocked a memory in Max's mind. This body's name was Maximilian Friedrich Zähringen. He was a soldier of Germany, which bordered Spain.

He was a brand new junior officer of the Royal Baden Cadet Corps in Karlsruhe and had gotten his rank ten days ago.

He was supposed to report to his new unit but things hadn't gone according to plan.

> [Intel collection progress: 7%]

Max was still processing the new memories when he was punched squarely in the face, knocking him sideways along with the chair he was tied to.

**Thump!**

The HUD disappeared from his vision and his focus returned to the room around him.

> [Danger detected. System UI suspended.]

Max thought to himself. *Oh boy, this thing doesn't come with a pause button it seems.*

**Bam**

A boot drove straight into his stomach, causing Max to curl up against his restraints.

He had been running through the jungles of Pandora a moment ago but now here he was tied to a chair getting his stuffing stomped on.

Perhaps he had been having too much relaxing times. He smiled wryly.

The man Max identified as an officer dragged the chair back upright and walked over to a table to wipe the sweat off his face with a towel which gave Max some time to look around.

The room looked like its appearance in the map. It had a low ceiling, no windows and a few oil lamps. Further back was a wooden staircase leading up to a hatch.

Two more soldiers in matching uniforms sat behind the table and watched him.

To his left, Max saw another officer tied up. He was much older with a uniform that looked similar to his own but much nicer.

The enemy officer at the table threw down the towel and the crimson stains stood out under the lamp light.

He walked back over to Max. His breath stank of tobacco and sweat.

**Bam**

He hooked another punch into Max's face from the other side. His ears rang and his mouth tasted like metal.

*"I will ask again. What is a German military attaché delegation doing here in Seville?"*

The officer gripped Max's hair and wrenched his head to the side and shouted in Max's ear.

*"Are you Germans trying to tear up the treaty? Do you really intend to interfere in our civil war?"*

Max gritted his teeth from the pain. This body was not used to taking a beating.

He stopped himself from rolling his eyes as he thought to himself.

*I don't even know who you are. What do you even want me to say here.*

Max couldn't find any useful information in his memories. He only knew that if Germany and Spain were neighbors, this world was vastly different from the ones that he had been in in the past.

He knew that explaining as such would probably only earn him another beating so he shut his mouth and played dead.

The officers behind the table smoked cigarettes without any appearance of concern.

*"Picking on a second lieutenant. You are truly brave."*

Max heard a rough voice from the side that spoke loudly.

*"If you have a problem, you take it up with me."*

Though it hurt to move, Max turned his head to look at the man.

The officer was bleeding and bruised but his back was still straight. He didn't look at all like a prisoner.

The enemy officer let go of Max's hair and smiled a twisted smile.

*"It's the first time anyone has told me that."*

He walked over to the older officer and hit him in the face.

**Smack** **Smack** **Smack**

The chair scraped the floor with a creak that set Max's teeth on edge as the man rained blows on the officer.

The older man's head swung from side to side, blood pouring from his mouth but he didn't make a single sound.

The enemy officer raised his hand for another but the two officers at the table put out their cigarettes and stood up.

The more impressive looking one stopped the man from his blow and observed the old officer.

*"August von Mackensen."*

He spoke calmly and much slower than the junior officer.

*"An Imperial Army general. I didn't expect to see someone of your stature stuck in a pit like this."*

Max raised a brow. This old officer was a general.

Max had several thoughts going through his mind.

*No wonder he was so confident.*

*Why is that name familiar to me?*

*Why is a second lieutenant being held with a general?*

Mackensen lifted his head. His face was a mess but his pride was unbroken.

He drew a thin smile over his bloodied teeth.

*"You know who I am. Then you should know what a military attaché delegation is."*

His voice was even.

*"We were invited by Crown Prince Alfonso, the rightful voice of the Spanish National Government. We have diplomatic immunity. You attacked us in the open, a direct insult to the German Empire. You could say it is an act of war."*

*"Diplomatic immunity."*

The other officer nearly laughed.

*"General Mackensen, this is Andalusia, not Madrid. The prince does not speak for the king. With Spain the way it is, whatever authority he claims does not reach here."*

He paused before speaking more softly.

*"We are not trying to fight Germany. But you and your people showed up where they shouldn't have. We want to know why."*

He spread his hands as if to say Mackensen was the one in the wrong.

Mackensen stared at him for a long time before giving a short laugh.

*"So you say we are where we don't belong. Then why are there Holy Britannic troops here too?"*

*"General Mackensen, we have treated you with respect. I expect the same from you."*

The middle aged officer glared at Mackensen and spoke with an edge.

*"When you name my country you will use her full formal title."*

Mackensen sneered right back across the dim cellar.

*"Full title? Holy Britannic Empire? What part of that is holy?"*

*"Neither holy nor much of an empire."*

The two were quite obstinate and argued with each other but their fight fed Max some data and the voice in his head gave a notification.

> [Intel collection progress: 9%]
> [The Holy Britannic Empire rules from the Britannic Isles. It holds vast colonies around the world and is an autocracy run by the emperor's bloodline.]

He wondered for a moment if it had anything to do with a classic anime but he hoped not.

But he could tell that they did not get along with Germany.

Both empires had forces in Spain but had kept it from devolving into all out war.

These soldiers had also earned their boy scout badges in ambushing. They had completely captured this attaché delegation.

Mackensen's mouth earned him plenty of friendly responses.

**Wack** **Wack** **Wack**

After a few solid punches Mackensen and his chair fell backwards.

But the three officers did not forget about Max and made sure he got his fair share too, leaving him on his side pretty quickly.

The three heaved for breath before two of them drew their pistols and pressed them against Mackensen's and Max's groins.

*"Our patience is limited. For the last time, is Germany set on entering the Spanish Civil War? What is your deployment?"*

Max shrugged his shoulders and looked the man in the eyes.

*"You might as well kill me. I don't remember anything."*

Max spoke calmly. He really didn't know why he was deployed here and he didn't really care to be beaten any further. He certainly didn't want to be shot through his nuts.

The officer in front of him holstered his pistol with a cold smile and hoisted him back upright.

*"Good, loyalty to your country earns my respect. But that only makes me want to break you more."*

Max almost laughed, clearing his throat and spitting blood to the side.

*"Believe me, I'm a second lieutenant. I don't have any information you would be interested in."*

The man yelled back.

*"Because you are only a second lieutenant everything about you is wrong!"*

*"If you are nobody important with nothing important to hide why were you in an officer group where the lowest rank was major and in the same car as a lieutenant general?"*

Max thought to himself. *Wait, is that where they found me? Why was I there?*

The man drove another boot into Max's stomach, causing his vision to narrow.

*"Keep up the act. When you reach the real interrogation centers at the rear, I will make sure to take care of you personally. I hope you are still as stubborn then."*

He grinned a wide and evil smile.

Before he could say any more, the cellar opened and a young soldier clambered down in a panic.

*"Major, the blockades we set up are taking fire. Germans and an unidentified group are advancing on our position."*

The man's voice trembled and dull blasts and gunshots could be heard through the open entrance.

The entire mood shifted in the cellar.

The ranking officer went deathly pale before kicking Max's chair and yelling to the others.

*"Move them to the rear right now. Hurry."*

*"Yes sir, right away."*

The soldier rushed back to the stairs to get help when a boom shook the entire house.

The cellar shuddered and dust fell everywhere.

The soldier fell backwards and rolled down the stairs onto his back.

Before he could get up, a shadow fell from the opening and stomped on his chest.

**Crackcrackcrack**

His ribs shattered but before he could even scream a rifle barrel leveled at his head.

**BANG!**

The blast hurt the ears of everyone in the cellar.

The round blew open the soldier's head, splattering red and grey matter everywhere.

Three more shadows quickly descended the stairs as well.

The Britannic officers raised their revolvers towards the stairs and fired wildly.

The bullets flew across the room, plinking uselessly away from the shadows, sparks lighting up the silhouettes. The three new shadows rushed across the room faster than Max could follow.

They carried round shields that blocked all the bullets before reaching the officers.

**Shing** **Shing** **Shing**

Their swords cut through cloth and flesh and the three officers folded to the ground without a sound.

The major who promised Max a world of suffering twitched at his feet, his eyes wide open in fear staring into Max's eyes.

Max didn't linger on the sight for long, fixing his gaze upon the newcomers.

They had finished the fight so quickly it was surprising and by the light of the lamps Max saw imperial gray German uniforms with polished metal armor over it. Along with their full head armor, it all looked well worn and incredibly heavy.

Max watched as one of them pulled a sword from a body, wiping it on the clothes before sheathing it.

They had bolt action rifles, plate armor and swords.

Max gave a wry grin and wondered what sort of crazy universe he had found himself in. His rescue party was armored like medieval knights with guns.

But he didn't have time to get thoughtful. Clearing some blood from his mouth, he flicked his head to the side.

*"I'm alright, please help the general."*

There were four soldiers so it wasn't like they needed to take turns being rescued but Mackensen still appreciated the loyal gesture.

One of the four came over and sliced through his bonds with a knife and lifted him up onto his feet. On the other side another soldier did the same for General Mackensen, lifting him onto his shoulders.

Mackensen didn't make a sound. Though he was prideful, he was too badly beaten to walk straight.

Max took a few steps, a little light headed but steady enough. He quickly ignored the soldiers who were already leaving and moved to the bodies on the ground, taking their IDs and any documents he could find before grabbing a revolver and some ammunition and following the men quickly up the stairs before he could be grabbed.

One of the soldiers looked pretty impatient.

But compared to what Max had experienced, the outside world looked even worse.

The entire ground floor of the building looked like it had been cleared with grenades, smashed into splinters by the blasts. Debris and rubble mixed together along with dust in the air.

The bodies of over a dozen enemy soldiers littered the ground in separate pieces, the smell filling his nose.

Max had to keep himself from gagging at the unexpected smell as he followed behind the soldiers.

It was already nighttime and the street lamps were out. The only light came from the cold and distant moon and from flashes of gunfire in the distance.

Max could barely make out the layout of the street.

Besides the armored soldiers who cleared the cellar, another squad of heavy infantry held the perimeter from the shadows with rifles slung over their backs.

It was clear that these soldiers were stronger than normal. They wore full plate armor and carried a full set of sword and shield along with rifles on their backs.

One of the men even carried a full MG 08 machine gun platform.

The German soldiers wearing just gray field pattern uniforms looked normal in comparison. There were about 30 of them carrying bolt action rifles and looking very uncomfortable standing next to the buildings.

Besides these two groups, there was yet another group of soldiers.

This group bore a mixture of old uniforms, working clothes and plain clothes along with an equally random assortment of weapons.

They made Max recall the enemy soldier recounting unknown assailants.

From the other side of the street a German soldier ran over, bent so low he was nearly crawling. He rushed over to the commanding officer and spoke quietly.

*"Captain, the Britannians are moving up. They are breaking down the feint on the flank and are moving their main forces toward us. Our comrades holding them back are almost pinned down."*

As he finished, the sound of gunfire could be heard getting closer.

Nearby soldiers aimed their rifles in the direction of the sounds, very much on edge.

*"Understood, send a message to the line. They must hang on until we can withdraw from this location and then they must retreat along their route. If the Britannians want to reach this house, they will ignore the feint."*

Max soon found himself picked up by one of the knights and rushed along with the column towards an open field. He passed by a number of soldiers being carried on stretchers. Perhaps survivors of other interrogation sites or from the battle.

They had moved several blocks when a whistling shriek broke the silence.

**Whsssssew** **BOOM**

He saw the destruction at a distance behind him as they all continued moving. A moment later a second explosion detonated closer to where he had been held.

*They are adjusting fire. That means they are targeting. But from where?*

**Boom**

He watched as a third shell destroyed the house that he had been held in. Then an entire salvo of artillery destroyed the entire block.

The commander looked at his watch and spoke coldly.

*"The artillery are on time for once."*

The hairs on the back of Max's neck stood up. It was clear that if this group failed to extract the general, then they would selflessly ensure that the general died for the country.

Max blinked a few times.

*They didn't hesitate to write off the general entirely.*

But his thoughts didn't slow the column down at all as it kept moving to withdraw.

The captain of the unit moved towards the irregular unit, addressing their leader.

*"Thank you for your aid tonight. Please cover our retreat as we withdraw General Mackensen to the rear. Afterwards we'll make sure that Central Command knows how much the International Brigade aided us this night."*

Max considered the implications. *The International Brigade was a communist volunteer army during the Spanish Civil War. How were they fighting with the Imperial German Army? Was this already a world war? What type of situation called for these two groups to be allies?*

Max felt like his understanding of the world was going on a roller coaster ride. He was beat by British soldiers. He was saved by sword wielding German soldiers against gunfire. The International Brigade was their allies during the Spanish civil war.

*How did the volunteer leftist fight alongside the aristocratic imperialists?*

This timeline meant the Kaiser and Lenin would be singing La Marseillaise together during parades. This was some sort of crazy fantasy world.

But he was too badly beaten to care too much. Ideological musings could wait. He had to survive first to be able to complain.

The column moved through the night until it reached the outskirts of Seville.

Behind thick brush Max could see several military trucks that were plainly out of place with everything else he had seen. Bicycles and horses stood next to them accompanied by scouts who kept lookout.

But there was still no time to stop. Max, Mackensen and the other wounded were quickly loaded onto the trucks while the German soldiers and International Brigade got on bicycles and horses.

The engines came to life and the horses neighed as the column moved away from the city.

The truck itself had no seats and Max found himself supporting General Mackensen on cold steel sheets with a little hay to pad it as the truck jumped along the ground.

In the moonlight he stole glances at the general.

Mackensen looked worse for wear with dried blood around his mouth and bruises on his face but he still held his back straight and watched out into the night.

Every so often he glanced towards Max with a different look in his eyes. Not the cold analysis of a senior officer.

Max searched his memories for any connection between them but Mackensen spoke first.

*"Last time I saw you was a ball in Dresden."*

He sounded tired but kept vigilant.

*"You were your grandfather's grandson. He was a man of note in Baden and in the army. I respected him. After him the Zähringen line thinned out. Weak sons, weaker grandsons. By the time I saw you in Dresden you still dressed as Hochadel and still talked your way into every salon but the name was already fading into obscurity. Empty eyes devoid of purpose. I thought the line was finished with you."*

Max paused. Mackensen had known his grandfather and the slow dimming of everything that followed.

*"Tonight you surprised me, more than I could ever expect."*

Mackensen turned to Max. His gaze was serious.

*"I thought a few English punches would have you running your mouth."*

Max smiled wryly but said nothing.

If he said any bit of the truth he was liable to be booted off the truck by the general personally.

Mackensen took the silence as agreement and almost sounded proud.

*"So the blood is still there. Buried under drink and idleness but not extinguished. That does not mean I'll go easy on you."*

The general's tone went flat and angry.

*"The day before our squad left, a domestic telegram found its way to my desk from a remarkably well connected lady."*

He nearly spat the words with contempt.

*"She wanted me to take care of you, place you in the rear and away from danger."*

Mackensen snorted and forced himself up despite the pain and got right up in Max's face.

*"She thinks the army is her personal spa or cafe. I hate people who treat the service as a finishing school and fools who try to meddle in operations with connections. I will not. You will not sit in the rear. You will fight on the front lines. An assault unit in the first wave. You will learn what war is. We will see if the drink has turned your bones to trash."*

The truck lurched and they nearly pressed their faces together but Mackensen pulled back, hissing through his teeth in pain. He didn't mention the turmoil in his heart, how it pained him to see a once great name faded to a last weak heir and to wonder whether anything of his grandfather's stubborn honor had survived.

Max on the other hand wasn't too bothered by a thousand previous lifetimes, his currently deceased family or his spotty memories.

Max did wonder for a moment who was so influential in his life as to try to influence the general. But the ride continued in silence except for the jolts from the road.

To occupy himself, Max looked ahead, watching the shadows in the distance. Large silhouettes hung in the night sky, perhaps airships or barrage balloons.

He realized that if there were people on those, that is how the artillery was being guided.

A long stretch of road later the pace slowed down as they approached camp. It was constructed in a clearing in a wooded area bordering a slope. Tents covered the slope in every direction and German uniforms moved among the tents. Artillery could be seen in the distance.

Chatter made it clear that this was the forward camp of the German advance and the temporary command of the region.

As the trucks stopped, Max jumped down and nearly reached out his hand but saw a look in General Mackensen's eyes that made him stand to attention and let the man come down on his own.

Officers rushed towards him at once but he ignored them all and made his way towards the largest tent in the middle of the camp.

Everyone had things to do and even Max was only briefly treated by a medic who quickly moved on to other soldiers.
