# Chapter 9

**Arc:** Seville Counterattack  
**Date:** 7 February 1914

---

An eerie silence fell over the battlefield. The smoke of battle still lingered and the smell of blood was pungent, but the gunfire and shouts of killing had ceased. German infantrymen and International Brigades soldiers gripped their weapons tightly on the high ground, watching the impending clash of steel below the hillside. On the other side the defeated Royal Army and British infantry stopped and looked back at their trump card. This meat grinder which just moments ago belonged to the infantry had become the exclusive stage for the armored knights.

The armored knights led by the Order of the Garter seemed unconcerned about being surrounded and instead took a step forward. The lead knight raised its right hand and slammed the ornate sword in its hand heavily onto the kite shield on its left arm.

**CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!**

The crisp resounding clang of metal echoed across the battlefield in an ancient declaration of war before a duel, or even more so an undisguised provocation.

*"They really put a lot of thought and ceremony into it."*

Max watched this scene through binoculars from the high ground and couldn't help but note. The next moment the white armored knight was met with a simple and brutal response from the Teutonic Knights.

**BOOM!**

Ludwig fired at the showy British armored knight. Beside Ludwig the eleven other Teutonic Knights armored knights who had gathered aimed their cannons at the enemy in the center after seeing him fire.

**BOOM BOOM BOOM BOOM**

Eleven direct fire cannons opened fire almost simultaneously. Their shells whistled as they instantly covered the area where the Order of the Garter stood. Violent explosions continued unabated. The earth trembled and dust and smoke billowed into the sky, completely engulfing the four white figures.

Seconds later four white armored knights burst out of the smoke and dust. They formed a tight diamond formation, ignoring the shells being fired at them and charged straight toward Ludwig and Wilhelm.

Max fixed his gaze firmly on them. He could clearly see that each of the white armored knights was covered by a shield that was constantly rippling. When the Teutonic Knights' cannonballs hit the shield they exploded in concentric blue ripples. Some kind of protective spell was at work.

But this protection was not invincible and had a certain defense threshold. Under the continuous bombardment of twelve handheld cannons the Order of the Garter armored knight at the forefront experienced increasingly violent ripples around its body. The color of its shield grew increasingly dim. Finally after another shell hit its target the shield crumbled silently. Subsequent shells struck its white armor without hindrance, blasting out charred craters and halting its charge.

The damage that long range firepower could cause was limited to breaking shields. When the distance between the two sides closed to 100 meters Ludwig was the first to throw down his handheld cannon. The other eleven armored knights of the Teutonic Knights also dropped their heavy ranged weapons and drew various melee weapons from their backs or waists. There were traditional greatswords, massive two handed battle axes and menacing flails. More often it was a heavy duty mace specifically designed for armor penetration.

Armored knights with black and white cross paint roared with deep engine noise as they brandished their savage weapons and charged toward the Order of the Garter. At the same time the knights and squires from both sides flanked them and collided violently.

**BOOM**

The moment steel clashed head on all the onlookers felt as if their hearts skipped a beat. The scene before them was truly breathtaking. Giant metal constructs created using magical and industrial techniques collided and slashed at each other in the most primitive and savage way. Every collision sparked dazzling sparks and every swing was accompanied by a whistling sound that tore through the air.

Max noticed the difference between the two. The armored knights of the Order of the Garter had rounded and exquisite external armor designs and their movements were elegant and swift. There was no exhaust pipe device behind them emitting any smoke. Pure magical technology. On the Teutonic Knights' side each armored knight had two exhaust pipes emitting black smoke standing behind it. These iron constructs moved more mechanically with large sweeping movements full of heavy industrial force. A meticulously crafted specialist machine besieged by robust and durable mass produced machines. Magic technology versus magic and internal combustion engine hybrid technology.

Max understood why the Teutonic Knights were fighting this battle with a three fold numerical advantage. There was a visible difference in performance between the armored knights of the two sides. Without the sheer number of weapons and the use of ranged weaponry the situation in this battle would be completely different.

The battle intensified after the two armored knights clashed. The Teutonic Knights abandoned all unnecessary movements and simply hurled their armor piercing melee weapons at their opponents' armor with frenzy. The Order of the Garter, relying on superior equipment and greater mobility, managed to defeat two Teutonic Knights in the initial skirmish. The relatively traditional armed swords in their hands could only be used to attack vital parts and seemed more suitable for unleashing their power in a group of infantry. In the armored knights civil war the armed sword was clearly outmatched when facing weapons like the spiked mace which were designed purely for armor penetration. The slashing blades would only leave white marks on the armored knights' thick armor while the enemy's spiked mace could smash through their outer armor plates leaving terrifying holes.

The balance of power in the battle began to slowly tilt amidst the brutal attrition. An armored knight of the Order of the Garter, after parrying several attacks, had its shield cleaved off its left arm by a battle axe. Immediately afterwards two maces struck its breastplate simultaneously from the left and right.

**CREAK**

The breastplate adorned with intricate emblems was smashed open leaving two holes. The white knight staggered backward. Its movements became noticeably slower.

Ludwig seized the opportunity, ramming his vehicle aside and raising his mace high, aiming it at the cockpit of the damaged Order of the Garter knight before slamming it down.

**CRUNCH**

Ludwig's mace smashed the cockpit of the Order of the Garter knight, completely deforming it. When he pulled the hammer out again he could even see bits of flesh and blood stuck to it. The elegant white armored knight in front of him struggled and swayed twice before finally collapsing to one knee then fell completely silent.

After eliminating one target the Teutonic Knights' offensive became even more ferocious. At the cost of four armored knights falling and nearly half of their knight squires being killed they finally destroyed all the knight squires and overturned another Order of the Garter knight. From Max's telescope the fallen armored knight was quickly surrounded by three Teutonic Knights who used maces and battle axes that shimmered with some kind of magical light to smash its limbs and front armor again and again. A battle between armored knights. A death match where no one holds back.

Only two armored knights of the Order of the Garter remained on the battlefield struggling to hold on. They fought and retreated, clearly having developed the intention to withdraw and began to break through the weakest point in the encirclement.

Ludwig who had already gained the upper hand was not about to give up the opportunity to annihilate them all so easily. He brandished his weapon and charged forward. Seeing his actions the other armored knights pressed their advantage, determined to leave their two last prey behind.

Just as the Teutonic Knights were about to achieve a great victory in this hunt a figure dressed in a mage's robe flew rapidly from the distant sky. The figure stopped abruptly in the air above the battlefield in a way that defied the laws of physics, looking down at everything below.

Upon seeing the person standing in the sky Max felt a sense of danger. Immediately afterwards the system's notification sound rang out, this time a rather piercing alarm.

```
Highland Mage Corps of the British Empire: Instructor — Eldridge
```

This was a Highland Mage who almost made eye contact with Max while he was conducting scouting ahead. Max knew very well that the only reason this person would appear here would be to support the enemy knights.

Max watched Eldridge in the air slowly raise his hands, make a downward pressing motion and chant an obscure incantation. Eldridge pointed towards the ground beneath the feet of the Teutonic Knights who were pursuing them.

```
Fifth Ring Spell — Transmute Rock
```

In an instant a strange change occurred. The solid scorched earth beneath the Teutonic Knights' feet seemed to have turned into a flowing swamp. Large tracts of land quickly softened and liquefied, turning into muddy ground that should never have been there. Those armored knights whose combat weight was over ten tons and their squires who wore power armor immediately lost their balance. Their heavy bodies sank uncontrollably into the mud.

*"Damn it!"*

Ludwig's roar echoed in the cockpit as he desperately steered his vehicle trying to break free from the mud. The frantic roar of the internal combustion engine only resulted in an even faster descent.

The situation on the field was completely reversed in just a few seconds. The two surviving Order of the Garter armored knights were initially hesitant but after Eldridge pointed in the direction of Seville they turned around without hesitation and quickly left the area.

Max looked at the figure in the sky. A sense of powerlessness washed over him.

*Are these the advanced spellcasters of this world?*

With a simple gesture one can alter the terrain and turn the tide of a local battle. With these armored knights around what chance do the infantry have? Cannon fodder.

*No. We can't let him continue casting the spell.*

Max was well aware of the importance of these Teutonic Knights' armored knights to the German infantry. If all these armored knights were to perish here the infantry would be in deep trouble.

Max glanced at the markings on the system map. Next to the mage's special unit badge there was a line of information and a shield symbol.

```
Status: Casting in progress, can be interrupted
Mage Armor — Current Durability: 100%
```

*Exactly. Of course it has weaknesses. Otherwise the game would be broken.*

*Mages have had it too easy for too long. Flying around casting spells like that without consequences. Remember when infantry used to be terrified of mage units?*

Max immediately understood: if mage spellcasting couldn't be interrupted that would be way too overpowered.

*"Third Platoon, ready! All hands, target that mage! Fire! Free fire!"*

Max shouted orders to his soldiers. The three rows of soldiers lying prone on the high ground were stunned at first but they quickly reacted and aimed at the figure more than 300 meters in the air from a prone firing position.

**BANG**

Max fired first. Dozens of Gewehr 98 rifles simultaneously spat fire. The burst of gunfire shattered the brief silence on the high ground. Max's actions were like lighting a fuse.

The International Brigades soldiers around him quickly realized what was happening. Everyone knew they couldn't let the mage wipe out their armored knights so easily. At the command of the bearded commander they all raised their guns and fired. Immediately afterwards Major Thomas the battalion commander of the 1st Battalion and other officers including Captain Hauser also reacted. The remaining 300 plus combat soldiers of the entire 1st Battalion pointed their guns at the sky.

A barrage of fire from hundreds of rifles rained down on Eldridge. Hitting a human sized target at a distance of over 300 meters is challenging for a rifle with iron sights. The hit rate will be affected to some extent. Quantitative changes can lead to qualitative changes. Under such a dense barrage of bullets a considerable number of bullets accurately hit the target.

A transparent shield rippled around Eldridge. The effect of Mage Armor. Eldridge intended to maintain the spell, burying those Teutonic Knights to achieve a tactical victory and then turn that victory into a decisive one.

The continuous attacks of the 7.92 full power rounds caused more and more cracks to appear on the shield. Eldridge's expression turned grim. Mage Armor wasn't invincible. It was essentially just a level 1 spell. The defense threshold of the Mage Armor he cast at his level was much higher than that of other low level mages but there was still an upper limit.

Back when Eldridge was an apprentice studying at the Scottish Wizarding Tower he had heard warnings from the old wizards in class.

*"Although we can easily deal with ordinary soldiers we must never try to fight firearms with our bare hands nor should we think that we can completely ignore firearms attacks by casting protective spells."*

The most famous example of this is the tragedy of a Highland archmage more than 20 years ago who used Mage Armor to face the enemy's artillery fire head on only to be pulverized along with his shield. In Eldridge's previous battles supporting the colonies when he appeared on the battlefield ordinary soldiers would often lose their will to fight and turn and run away in fear, let alone attack him.

Today this perception had been shattered by the attack of German soldiers. Eldridge never expected that these ordinary infantrymen whom he considered ants could threaten him in this way.

Just before the shield was about to collapse Eldridge had to abandon his channeling and begin to dodge in the air. The mud on the ground quickly solidified again but the Teutonic Knights were already half submerged and unable to move.

Eldridge glared fiercely at the infantrymen below who were still firing incessantly. He was just about to use other spells prepared in his spell slots to make these ants regret shooting at him.

The white flares rising from the city of Seville behind him abruptly interrupted his actions. A signal for the Allied forces to retreat.

*"These useless things!"*

Eldridge cursed angrily then cast another Shield in front of himself before flying off towards Seville without looking back.

The mage's figure disappeared into the horizon in a disheveled state instantly shattering the silence on the highlands.

*"We chased him away!"*

Someone shouted first and then a deafening cheer echoed across the entire hillside. The surviving soldiers whether Germans or volunteers from the International Brigades all jumped up from behind their bunkers. They waved their rifles and some even threw their pointed helmets into the air. An action that was immediately stopped by the officers who then kicked them hard in the backside. All the soldiers were expressing their excitement and ecstasy in the most primal way.

The joy of this victory was far greater than that of defeating an infantry battalion of a Northumberland Fusiliers regiment. Max was naturally caught up in the atmosphere and became very excited but he was filled with more doubts than anyone else. Though he could see the shield weakening quickly in the map it didn't seem likely that a mage of that level was forced back with rifle fire alone. It must have something to do with the flares flying over Seville.

Max initially worried that all the high level mages of this world were like Fujitora and could spam meteors but fortunately that didn't seem to be the case. With proper tactical planning it seems that it is actually possible to put up a fight.

*"I hope I'm not just imagining things."*

Max looked at the soldiers around him who were caught up in the revelry. The tension in his heart slowly eased.

*"My God, Lieutenant Maximilian, we actually drove a high level mage away!"*

The bearded commander of the International Brigade who had been lying next to him was so excited that his face turned red. He patted Max's shoulder hard. His voice was filled with disbelief.

*"I've been in so many battles but this is the first time! The first time I've seen ordinary people with guns like us make those high and mighty mages suffer a setback!"*

It was only then that Max belatedly realized how incredible their actions must have seemed. In this world mages have always seemed to occupy a position close to the top of the battlefield food chain. This has led many ordinary soldiers to develop a stereotype that they are invincible.

Today a group of ordinary soldiers on Hill 127 temporarily shattered this stereotype with their rifles.

Just then from the area below the high ground that had been turned into a quagmire by magic came the sound of a metal cockpit canopy opening. The cockpit of the Teutonic Knights armored knight that was stuck the deepest part slowly opened and Ludwig's figure stood up from it. Ludwig took off his helmet revealing a handsome face stained with a little oil. He looked up at the cheering soldiers on the high ground. His gaze searched through the crowd for a moment as if trying to find the person who had fired the first shot. Ludwig abandoned the idea a moment later, raised his right hand high and gave a thumbs up to everyone on the high ground.

This simple yet powerful action instantly propelled the soldiers' emotions to another climax. The cheers grew even louder and some people even began shouting "Long live the Teutonic Knights!"

*"Wow, that's pretty flashy."*

Max looked down at the noble pilot receiving cheers. The Teutonic Knights' armored knights seemed to be battle stars in the German army.

*"Don't just stand there! Go down and help!"*

Major Thomas the battalion commander of the 1st Battalion rang out just in time. He directed his soldiers to rush down the hill to help the armored knights who were still stuck in the mud to get out of trouble.

Max also prepared to follow with his men. At that moment the messenger he had sent to the artillery command post ran back panting.

*"Platoon leader! I couldn't find the artillery command post but I encountered a troop of our cavalry on the way!"*

The messenger still had an excited look on his face.

*"I told them the location of the magic crystal cannon position you marked and the cavalry commander said they would take care of that problem!"*

Max suddenly realized. This must be the German cavalry regiment that had launched the charge earlier. No wonder they left the battlefield in that direction. They were going to carry out a new combat mission. It remains to be seen whether their attack will be successful.

Down the hillside rescue work was in full swing. The Teutonic Knights' logistics trucks and a large number of draft horses quickly arrived at the scene. The soldiers used shovels and spades they had found in the supply corps to dig the soil that had hardened again. Thick cables were attached to the armored knight and the truck and draft horses pulled along. The two armored knights that were stuck relatively shallowly were the first to break free and after the knights' technicians quickly inspected and restarted their internal combustion engines they joined the rescue efforts.

Max stood to the side watching the scene that resembled digging up giant potatoes in a farmland but he still felt uneasy.

*What if at this moment the enemy fires a few more magic crystal cannon shots?*

Fortunately this time his worst fears did not come true. Perhaps the cavalry unit really destroyed the enemy's magic crystal cannon position. In any case all of the kingdom's magic crystal cannons did indeed fall silent afterward.

As the last vehicle, Ludwig's, was successfully pulled out of the mud everyone cheered again. Ludwig jumped out of the cockpit and was immediately surrounded by fanatical soldiers. Even Major Thomas solemnly removed his pointed helmet to pay homage to this noble armored knight commander.

Amid the noisy crowd Max stood at a distance with a polite smile on his face then clapped along with the others. Normally Max who had blended into the crowd would not have attracted attention but his calm demeanor which was clearly out of place with the atmosphere around him quickly caught Ludwig's attention. Ludwig's gaze lingered on Max for a few seconds. His brow furrowed slightly as if he were recalling something.

*"Lieutenant Colonel, it was this Lieutenant Maximilian who was the first to order us to fire on the mages in the sky!"*

When Captain Hauser noticed that the other party had taken notice of them he immediately put his arm around Max's shoulder and introduced him with a proud expression.

Ludwig's confusion instantly turned into surprise. He reexamined Max. His expression was as if he had witnessed something that overturned his understanding.

Ludwig remembered. He had seen this young man at a banquet for nobles before the war began. One of them relying on his meager family fortune and his thick skin frequented various banquets. With his fairly handsome face and smooth talking skills he talked with naive noble ladies everywhere. A somewhat well known idle conversationalist in the Hochadel aristocratic circles.

*Why is he here?*

And he even made that courageous and wise move?

Despite his inner turmoil Ludwig put aside his aristocratic arrogance. He strode up to Max and gave him a solemn military salute.

*"Lieutenant Maximilian, on behalf of the Teutonic Knights and all your comrades whom you saved I extend my sincerest respect to you."*

His voice was loud and sincere.

*"The courage you showed when facing a formidable opponent is admirable."*

*"You're too kind, Lieutenant Colonel. This wasn't achieved by me alone."*

Max glanced at the other man's rank, returned the salute and then continued.

*"Without the combined efforts of everyone in the 1st Battalion and the International Brigade it would have been impossible to force that mage to retreat."*

After the Teutonic Knights escaped their units began collecting the bodies and providing emergency treatment to the wounded. At the same time a messenger rode up on horseback and stopped in front of Major Thomas.

*"Reporting, Major! Regimental orders! The enemy is retreating across the board! We are ordered to pursue them immediately!"*

Upon receiving the order the troops who had just been basking in the joy of victory immediately regrouped. The atmosphere on the battlefield became tense yet orderly once again.

The bearded commander of the International Brigades found Major Thomas and after a brief discussion decided to temporarily join the 1st Battalion and continue advancing towards the city of Seville. Ludwig also stated that the Teutonic Knights were originally there to cooperate in the battle so they would also enter the city first. The attitude of these two men relieved Major Thomas. Otherwise he would have been somewhat panicked to lead the heavily damaged 1st Battalion forward.

During this brief moment Max quickly took stock of the casualties in his platoon. Max looked at the sparse queue in front of him. His heart sank. After a battle to capture the high ground his original full strength 3rd Platoon was reduced to only 48 men. Sergeant Jonah who had fought bravely behind him also fell in the final hand to hand combat and never got up again. The cruelty of war was presented to Max in the most direct way at this moment. This was also the first time Max had directly experienced what it felt like to see his soldiers die in battle.

After leaving behind a platoon that was almost completely decimated waiting for the medics and stretcher bearers to come up and collect the bodies of the fallen.

Major Thomas led his troops in coordination with the International Brigades and the Teutonic Knights in pursuit of the enemy's wreckage towards the city of Seville. Although it was called a pursuit to everyone's surprise they advanced without encountering any obstacles. Major Thomas also had a messenger report the progress to regimental headquarters. Max didn't know the specifics. He only heard Major Thomas say:

*"We're advancing unopposed! Unopposed!"*

They encountered no resistance until the International Brigades sent an advance force of more than ten men onto the cobblestone streets on the outskirts of Seville. The enemy did not simply order a full retreat as stated in the regimental command. They abandoned the entire city outright.

*"Something's not right. This isn't how you fight a war."*

Max looked at the eerily calm city before him. His brow furrowed.

According to his previous reconnaissance the Royal Army and the British had deployed a large force here, built a strong defensive line and were ready to fight the allied forces to the death. How could they abandon the city and flee so easily just because of a single failed attack? This is highly illogical unless it's a trap.

Max wanted to find his direct superior Captain Hauser but he couldn't reach him for a long time. In the end he could only find Major Thomas who was talking to the bearded commander of the International Brigades and express his concerns.

*"Major, I think this is very likely a trap. The enemy is clearly trying to lure us into the city."*

*"Max, your concerns are valid."*

Major Thomas interrupted him.

*"However I think it is more likely that the enemy was not prepared to fight in the city streets. After our defenses were breached there was no point in continuing to defend the city."*

The bearded commander nodded in agreement.

At that moment Ludwig who had his servants and technicians inspect the damage to the armored knights also came over and overheard their conversation.

*"I think their losses are too great."*

Ludwig's voice drew the attention of the three.

*"Leaving aside the casualties of the Kingdom's ragtag troops the Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment of Britain alone had more than half of its battalion decimated by you on the high ground."*

*"More importantly they also lost two armored knights and all their knights' squires, a crushing defeat that the British who have always been very proud of themselves have not suffered in recent years."*

Ludwig paused then continued his analysis.

*"I don't think they'll give up so easily. This retreat is more like a tactical retreat. They will likely regroup outside the city and launch a counterattack against us."*

Max heard Ludwig's words. He frowned and asked in return.

*"Would you willingly relinquish your defensive advantage and then at the cost of even greater casualties launch a counterattack?"*

*"This shows that the other side is confident in doing so and they have grasped a point that we cannot refuse."*

*"Seville's importance to us makes it impossible for us to retreat as easily as they have."*

Major Thomas realized what was happening. He picked up the conversation and looked up at Max.

*"This also means that we're stuck here, Lieutenant Maximilian. Your concerns are indeed valid."*

*"It seems we must remain vigilant. There may be even tougher battles to come."*

Ludwig concluded.

Soon the National Army, the International Brigade and the follow up troops of the 16th Infantry Brigade entered the city and carried out a thorough mopping up operation. After some time it was finally confirmed that the enemy had indeed completely withdrawn. Once the city was deemed safe the supply troops, field hospital and field kitchen quickly followed suit, setting up camp on an open area on the outskirts of the city.

Max first ordered several soldiers in his platoon who were seriously injured but had only received emergency treatment to be sent to the field hospital. This reduced the number of men under his command who could move to 42.

Max called Sergeant Klaus aside.

*"Klaus, take stock of the ammunition consumption of each squad then take a few men to the company quartermaster and replenish the ammunition."*

*"Yes, Platoon Commander!"*

*"Especially hand grenades. Try to get more of them. If we are going to face urban warfare these things will be more useful than anything else."*

Sergeant Klaus quickly accepted the order and left.

*"Corporal Baumann!"*

*"Yes!"*

*"You are responsible for compiling a detailed list of all the dead and wounded in the platoon then submitting it to the company clerk."*

Max looked at the young corporal in front of him. His tone became unusually serious.

*"You must be careful and not miss a single name, not even a single letter can be wrong. This is related to whether the fallen soldiers can receive their compensation and it is related to the future lives of their families far away. Do you understand?"*

*"I understand! Don't worry, sir!"*

Corporal Baumann nodded emphatically. The soldiers around him including some International Brigades volunteers who were listening intently nearby all looked at Max with a subtle change in their gaze.

After dealing with these trivial matters Klaus returned with ammunition and two grenades. After Baumann and the others rejoined the team an enticing aroma wafted from the field kitchen not far away. The hunger that surged in the soldiers' stomachs quickly overwhelmed their grief over the loss of their comrades. Cruel but indeed a reflection of the battlefield. If people cry and wail and can't eat whenever a comrade dies then there's no point in fighting this war.

Max looked at the three platoons of still alive starving soldiers. He finally waved his hand dismissively.

*"Third Platoon, everyone! Let's eat!"*

After a whole morning of fierce fighting both Max and his soldiers were starving. Today's meal was more lavish than anyone had imagined.

The soup made with Erbswurst sausage and potato chunks became even thicker after a long simmer. The staple foods black bread and hot coffee were also plentiful.

In addition to these three staples the field kitchen also lavishly prepared fried sausages for everyone and each person even got a whole one. At this time the German Empire was clearly not in a period of material shortage so the supply of military supplies was also very abundant. Unlike the Second German Empire in another timeline where soldiers on the front lines were forced to eat bread mixed with large amounts of sawdust after air routes were blocked and supplies became scarce German sausages were not affected right now.

The chefs placed the whole sausage directly into the pan and fried it in butter until it sizzled and the skin was golden brown and crispy. This method locks in the juices and oils inside the sausage to the greatest extent possible ensuring the purest flavor.

As a Foodie King who had quickly become famous throughout the company Maximilian couldn't wait to find a place to sit down after getting his food. He blew on the steam rising from the sausage and then took a big bite without waiting.

**CRACK**

*"Hiss ha."*

A little hot but once he bit through the crispy outer layer the scalding hot juices mixed with the rich aroma of meat burst in his mouth. In that instant Max felt his tired body and mind completely healed by this unparalleled deliciousness. He quickly recognized it as a classic Thuringian sausage.

The soldiers around them couldn't help but swallow hard as they watched their platoon leader eat with such a blissful expression. They felt their appetites were completely whetted and they all buried their heads and began to eat heartily.

Max ate a sausage with some soaked black bread then ate a lot of thoroughly stewed potatoes and finally had a cup of lighter brewed acorn coffee with a nice nutty aroma.

*"Ahh."*

Max felt like he had come back to life. The unease in his heart never dissipated.

Max wiped his mouth and found Sergeant Klaus and his orderly Hans.

*"Come on, come with me somewhere."*

Max along with the two others borrowed three bicycles from the supply train and rode straight towards the only cathedral in the city. That is also the highest point in the entire city. He planned to climb the bell tower so he could see for himself what was happening outside the city.

The church unaffected by the previous battles appeared solemn and dignified. After explaining their purpose to a somewhat nervous priest at the door the priest who spoke German hesitated for a moment before leading them to the bottom of the clock tower stairs.

*"Klaus, Hans, you two talk to this priest and get a general idea of the situation in the city. See if there's anything unusual."*

After giving a brief instruction Max climbed the spiral staircase leading to the top of the clock tower alone. The steps were narrow and steep and the light was dim. When he pushed open the small door leading to the top floor he found that someone was already there.

Lieutenant Colonel Ludwig of the Teutonic Knights and another armored knight pilot he did not recognize beside him. The former was holding a telescope seemingly observing the movements outside the city.

Seeing Max come up Ludwig put down his binoculars and said something to his companion beside him. The pilot Wilhelm glanced at Max with some surprise then nodded and turned to go down the stairs. At the top of the clock tower only Max and Ludwig remained.

Max didn't speak. He saluted Ludwig and walked to the wooden railing, raising his binoculars and looking towards the northeast of Seville. Cities of this era lacked skyscrapers but church bell towers situated at the highest points offered unparalleled panoramic views. The plains, forests and roads outside the city were clearly visible to him.

Max saw no signs of a large scale mobilization of the Kingdom's army. Everything seemed too calm.

*"Their retreat was very orderly with no signs of chaos."*

Ludwig's voice rang out from the side.

*"It doesn't feel like a rout but more like they're gathering themselves, preparing to deliver an even heavier blow. Your concerns are certainly valid."*

Max put down his binoculars and turned to look at him. Ludwig was also looking at him. His eyes filled with inquiry and curiosity.

*"To be honest I really find it hard to connect the current you with the Max I saw at the party in Stuttgart."*

Upon hearing what the other person said Max was taken aback.

*Is this another acquaintance?*

*"You seem surprised?"*

Seeing that Max looked completely unfamiliar Ludwig smiled.

*"That's not surprising, after all you're always surrounded by noble ladies at the party so you wouldn't be paying attention to me."*

*"I'm really sorry, Lieutenant Colonel Ludwig. I'll definitely be more careful next time."*

Ludwig was genuinely surprised by Max's response and swallowed back all his words. He still stared intently at Max and continued.

*"But I really can't understand how someone like that could display such bravery and sharpness on the battlefield."*

*"I apologize sir. A few days ago I was beaten into idiocy by the British in Seville and haven't quite recalled my past."*

Ludwig didn't seem to expect a reply. He ignored Max's frankly unbelievable words and leaned against the railing and started talking to himself.

*"But that's fine too. The Empire's army needs warriors like you, not useless bums who only know how to boast at parties."*

*"Oh and Ms. Cecilia too. I wonder what her reaction will be when she finds out you've transformed into this."*

*"I heard she sent a telegram directly to General Mackensen's headquarters which infuriated the old general."*

Ludwig kept talking on and on completely ignoring whether Max responded. Max was surprised that Lieutenant Colonel Ludwig seemed to know quite a lot.

Max didn't respond. He just listened silently, memorizing all the information about the original owner. The most important thing is that they know the noble lady who delivered the telegram is a woman named Cecilia but it is not yet clear what the original owner's relationship with this woman is.

*"By the way it seems that my self important sister has misjudged you this time too."*

*"You have a younger sister?"*

Ludwig's expression shifted several times before finally settling down.

*"I can't tell if you are telling the truth or not or pretending not to remember being rejected by Patricia."*

Upon hearing Patricia's name a blurry image of a blonde girl flashed through Max's mind just as Ludwig had said, someone with whom he seemed to have some connection.

Ludwig did not continue on this topic and soon chatted with Max about other things which made Max realize that this noble pilot was a chatterbox. The two stayed on the bell tower for a long time until they confirmed that there was no unusual activity outside the city before leaving one after the other.

When Max returned to the outskirts of the city where his troops were stationed he found that the scene had changed drastically.

After confirming that the people entering the city were the National Army and their allies the residents of Seville came out of their homes. It is evident that the National Army and the International Brigades enjoy considerable popularity among the people of the Kingdom of Spain. That's true at least in Seville.

The enthusiastic citizens spontaneously brought out the bread, cheese and wine they didn't have much of at home to reward the soldiers who had liberated them from the Kingdom's army. Consequently these allies of the German Empire also benefited. Soldiers from the National Army and the International Brigades accepted food offered by the citizens with restraint. Their faces beamed with simple smiles. The entire square was filled with laughter and joy creating a peaceful and happy atmosphere.

The German soldiers were still somewhat wary. After all this was another country to them and the soldiers of the kingdom they had fought against before were essentially composed of civilians from the Kingdom of Spain. Max understood their reaction. In his previous life he had studied military history and learned that after the German army invaded Belgium during World War I they were in a state of paranoia due to the attacks by guerrillas and some Belgian civilians.

Coupled with the pressure from several setbacks in the early stages of the war some German troops attacked civilians and violated military discipline after entering towns. Just as Max was pondering whether he should pay attention to maintaining team discipline to prevent similar situations from occurring his fears came true.

A street in the distance suddenly erupted in commotion. Civilians screamed and fled while soldiers rushed over. When Max arrived at the scene he wondered if he had gained powers to influence his surroundings. Otherwise how could what he feared come true? Several German soldiers were seen standing in front of a civilian house, guns raised, confronting the National Army and International Brigades soldiers around them.

They had cheese and cured meat strung on ropes hanging from their bodies clearly retrieved from the house. Listening to the intermittent shouts from both sides Max had already pieced together most of the story in his mind.

These troublemaking German soldiers like him were soldiers of the 33rd Infantry Regiment except they came from the 2nd Battalion. Like the 1st Battalion the 2nd Battalion suffered heavy casualties in the previous offensive battles, not only from the barrage of magic crystal cannons but also from attacks by enemy forces with superior numbers. Their battalion commander, two company commanders and a large number of officers were killed in action. Now the highest ranking officer left in the entire battalion is a captain.

Lacking officer supervision and having just experienced a bloody battle these soldiers were under immense mental stress just as Max had previously analyzed. In addition the three field kitchens of the 2nd Battalion were slow to serve meals which ultimately led these starving soldiers to commit the act of robbing civilian houses. The soldiers of the National Army and the International Brigades were apparently summoned by the civilians who had escaped.

Upon their arrival they caught the German soldiers red handed as they were giving themselves a five finger discount. Because the soldiers on both sides did not fully understand each other's languages and were still hot tempered from the battlefield they pointed their guns at each other in a moment of excitement.

Now more and more people are gathering to watch and a considerable number of German soldiers have also come after hearing the commotion. When everyone saw this they didn't know what to do. As tensions between the two sides escalated and their fingers were about to land on the trigger Max knew he couldn't wait any longer.

The battle in Seville isn't over yet. If they start fighting amongst themselves here there's no point in continuing. No one wants their side to lose a battle especially since he is now an officer of the German Empire and should be in charge of disciplining his soldiers. So with the other officers not yet arrived he had to find a way to calm the situation and the way he handled it had to be impeccable.

They must not allow the conflict between the two sides to escalate further but they also need to give these local civilians an explanation. Should they just kill these German soldiers? This simple and crude method is the quickest way to quell the situation. In the long run this will only exacerbate the conflict, making German soldiers feel bullied by their allies and affecting morale.

Max needed to change his perspective. With that thought in mind Max no longer hesitated, pushed through the crowd and strode to the middle of the two dark gun barrels on either side.

When the soldiers of the 2nd Battalion saw a German officer approaching their faces lit up with joy, thinking that their saviors had arrived. To everyone's surprise Max didn't say a word but immediately pushed aside the leading soldier's rifle. His eyes looked so terrifying that he seemed to want to devour people.

*"What the hell are you doing?!"*

Max stared at the leading soldier. His eyes glinted dangerously. The colors deepened as he unconsciously used Wild Shape. The air getting colder. The soldier's previous fierce fighting spirit vanished instantly. The National Army soldiers were stunned. The International Brigades soldiers were stunned. The German soldiers watching from the sidelines were also stunned. Even Major Thomas and Lieutenant Colonel Ludwig who rushed over with their men after hearing the commotion were stunned when they saw this scene.

The soldier who had been stared down stood rigid looking incredulous. He hadn't expected that Max didn't seem to be there to protect his own.

*"What are you looking at?!"*

Max turned his predatory gaze on the others. The temperature around them dropped perceptibly.

*"And you all! Put down your guns!"*

The terrifying pressure completely stunned the rioting soldiers. None of them put up any resistance extinguishing the anger in their hearts. They instinctively lowered their rifles. The National Army and International Brigades soldiers who witnessed this also lowered their guns.

Max breathed a slight sigh of relief. At least the worst case scenario wouldn't happen for the time being.

Maintaining a dignified expression Max stood before the soldiers and spoke.

*"I ask you, why have you traveled thousands of miles from the Empire to fight in the Kingdom of Spain?"*

The soldiers lowered their heads not daring to speak.

*"Is it to steal a few pieces of cheese? A few strips of bacon? Is it to point guns at the civilians who saw us as liberators, at the allies who were just shedding blood with us?"*

Max's voice suddenly rose, pointing to the food hanging on their bodies and then to the National Army and International Brigades soldiers opposite them with complicated expressions.

*"When your parents, wives and children saw you off on the train didn't they hope you would become heroes and win glory for the empire? Didn't they long for you to return home safely, adorned with medals?!"*

*"But what are you doing now?! You are tarnishing your military uniforms! You are tarnishing the Black Eagle flag of the Empire! Do you think what you are doing is worthy of the expectations of your families far away? Do you think you are worthy of the comrades who died on the battlefield?!"*

Every word was like a knife to the heart. Max didn't preach any grand principles. His words weren't just for the few soldiers who had made mistakes but for all the German soldiers around him.

Max knew very well that there were many more German soldiers with negative emotions than just these few. They were just the first to express them so he took this opportunity to suppress the emotions of the others. Those soldiers who caused the trouble acted impulsively out of fear and hunger. At this moment after hearing what Max said their mental defenses completely collapsed.

One of the youngest German soldiers his eyes reddened burst into tears.

*"Sir, we were wrong. We were just too hungry."*

*"We will never dare to do it again."*

They hurriedly untied the cheese and bacon strung on their bodies and placed them neatly at the door of the house then bowed their heads like a group of children who had done something wrong. Seeing this Max's anger subsided considerably.

Max turned around, faced the soldiers of the International Brigades and the National Army and solemnly saluted them.

*"My allies I apologize to you and the affected residents of Seville for the actions of these young soldiers."*

He spoke fluent Spanish so everyone around him could understand him.

*"The German army will deal with them seriously in accordance with relevant military discipline. Please believe that this is just an isolated case. I assure you that we will discipline our troops and never allow similar situations to happen again."*

*"I believe these soldiers didn't do these things intentionally. Just now these young men also charged at the enemy's position with bayonets at the ready. Their unit suffered heavy losses and almost all of their officers were wiped out. This led them to take some extreme actions after the battle."*

*"I assure you all once again that we will definitely keep our troops under control from now on."*

The anger on the faces of the National Army and International Brigades soldiers around them gradually dissipated. They were all simple and honest people. Seeing that Max, a German officer, did not protect these soldiers but instead treated them with such sincerity and considering that the German soldiers had indeed suffered heavy casualties their anger had long since subsided.

At that moment Major Thomas and Lieutenant Colonel Ludwig also walked over at just the right time. Major Thomas nodded to Max then loudly announced to everyone.

*"Just as Lieutenant Maximilian said these soldiers will be dealt with according to military discipline! But I also hope that everyone will give them a chance to redeem themselves!"*

*"And as Lieutenant Maximilian just emphasized any violation of German Army discipline or any behavior that undermines our relations with our allies will be punished most severely!"*

Max then translated his words into Spanish and repeated them.

Ludwig added in fluent Spanish.

*"The German Empire, the Spanish National Army and the International Brigades are staunch allies. Our common enemy is the Kingdom's army and their British masters. Anyone who tries to undermine this friendship is our enemy."*

The statements from the two senior military officers brought this turmoil to a complete close. The men from the 2nd Battalion quickly arrived and took the dejected soldiers away. These soldiers wouldn't be executed but they would certainly be punished.

The owner of the house was also asked to come out and after accepting some gold marks as compensation he expressed his forgiveness. A crisis that could have triggered internal strife was defused by Max's few words.

After the crowd dispersed Major Thomas patted Max on the shoulder. His eyes full of approval. Ludwig's gaze toward Max became even more complex as if he were getting to know Max all over again. In addition to inquiry and curiosity there seemed to be a hint of genuine approval in his eyes.

Even the officers of the International Brigades and the National Army looked at Max differently. They all understood that being able to quickly identify the key to the problem in such a chaotic situation and resolve it in the most effective way was something that an ordinary young officer could not do.

A Teutonic Knights messenger approached Ludwig and spoke quietly to him. Ludwig nodded then turned to Max and Major Thomas.

*"Duty calls. Technical issues with the armored knights that require my attention."*

He gave a slight nod and left with the messenger.
