# Chapter 7

Arc: Seville

Date: 7 February 1914, past midnight

Max woke up feeling much better than earlier. He could have slept longer but his stomach was not allowing him to. Max opened his eyes. The tent was pitch black with only the faint light from an oil lamp hanging outside stubbornly penetrating the room.

"*Platoon leader, you're awake?*"

A hushed voice sounded at the tent entrance. Hans was his orderly.

"*Hans? It does not seem like it has been very long. What time is it?*"

"*It is just past midnight. Would you like to have something to eat?*"

"*Hmm, yes. That would be nice.*"

Max got up from his cot and stretched lightly. Max felt how comfortable his body felt.

Hans saw that Max had woken up. Hans immediately handed him a Kochgeschirr that had been kept warm by the embers of the fire and a piece of black bread.

Max took the still warm Kochgeschirr and opened it. A rich aroma of meat broth immediately filled the air.

*The battlefield kitchen is having a special dinner tonight. They have even added some tinned beef to the pea soup.*

Max had not eaten anything properly all day except for the meal before the reconnaissance. He did not care about his appearance at this moment. Max disregarded the serving lid and ate straight from the main container. He practically inhaled the two liters of soup with the loaf of bread Hans had requisitioned. Hans watched his platoon leader eat and felt his impression deepen.

"*By the way, Platoon Leader, the messenger from the company headquarters came an hour ago and told us to break camp at 4:30 in the morning sharp.*"

"*What is the matter?*"

Max looked up in surprise and wiped his mouth.

"*Why did you not wake me when the messenger came?*"

Hans scratched his head and looked a little embarrassed.

"*The sergeant said you were too tired and needed to rest. The messenger also said that Captain Hauser specifically instructed that if you were still sleeping you should rest well and not be disturbed.*"

Max paused and held the empty Kochgeschirr. He realized that in this army which was still unfamiliar to him something different from discipline and orders seemed to be quietly growing.

"*Thank you, but since I am the military commander of the third platoon you must wake me up next time a messenger comes. Understand?*"

"*Yes, Platoon Commander!*"

Max finished his meal. He did not rest any longer but got up and walked out of the tent. Max first walked around the temporary encampment of the third platoon and checked the sentries' posts. He confirmed that several soldiers including Corporal Baumann who had been with him during the day were resting well in their tents before he felt relieved enough to return to his own tent to rest as well.

Sergeant Klaus appeared outside his tent at 3:50 in the morning on time. Max just got up from his cot. The two exchanged a few words. Klaus and Max blew the assembly whistle together at 4:10 and woke the soldiers who were still asleep to pack their belongings and dismantle their tents.

The third platoon assembled on time at the designated location of the company at 4:30.

The entire company of soldiers merged into the massive marching column of the 1st Battalion soon after. The column flowed silently towards the designated attack position under the cover of night.

Hours passed in darkness.

Eventually the sky turned white.

The Royal Army's reveille broke the silence of dawn on a hill outside Seville. The soldiers on the magic crystal cannon position grumbled as they crawled out of their makeshift shacks and began another day of maintenance work.

"*Damn it. This thing is harder to take care of than my wife.*"

A seasoned veteran complained as he carefully wiped the intricate runes on the cannon with a soft cloth soaked in a special liquid. The other person was inspecting and wiping the core magic crystal to ensure it was not contaminated.

These soldiers were completely unaware that a German howitzer battery had been targeting them from behind a slightly elevated woodland about 4 kilometers away.

The captain of the German field artillery company stood atop a 2 meter high observation vehicle on a makeshift earthen mound. He conducted final rangefinding with a scissor type artillery scope. The captain obtained rough distance and location information. He instructed the messenger beside him to transmit the information to the howitzer firing position using a makeshift short telephone line.

Six 105mm howitzers were lined up at the firing position. An artillery sergeant responsible for directing the firing was there. Another messenger responsible for receiving information from the observation positions was also present.

The artillery sergeant gave the company commander the bearing and distance information. The gunner of the first howitzer on the far left immediately looked at the dial sight used for indirect fire and started turning the elevation and traverse mechanisms.

Time ticked by. The appointed moment arrived. The artillery sergeant forcefully lowered the red flag in his hand.

"*Cannon number one, test fire!*"

**BOOM**

The howitzer on the far left of the artillery column roared. The loud bang of the shell shattered the morning tranquility.

A few seconds later a small cloud of dust rose from the distant mountaintop. The Kingdom's magic crystal cannon position was thrown into chaos. Through the observation lens the soldiers looked like a swarm of ants suddenly set into motion.

"*Over! Deflection remains the same. Drop one hundred!*"

The company commander shouted without looking up from his observation scope. The corrected information was quickly transmitted back. The gunners swiftly adjusted the elevation and reloaded the shells. The second shot followed immediately.

The shells landed in front of the magic crystal cannon position this time.

"*Bracket!*"

The artillery company commander on the observation vehicle grinned. Two rounds of test firing had determined the target's precise range within 100 meters in front and behind.

"*Deflection the same. Add fifty! Battery fire for effect!*"

All six 105mm howitzers opened fire simultaneously shortly after the order was given.

The shells whistling with death pierced the still damp morning air and landed precisely on the Kingdom Army's magic crystal cannon position where the soldiers were still complaining. Dirt, rubble and human remains were all thrown into the air amidst the violent explosion.

The meticulously maintained magic crystal cannon at the very front of the position was completely blasted into twisted scrap metal in the first salvo.

The company commander saw through the observation scope that the average point of impact was slightly forward. He made a final adjustment.

"*Battery, add twenty five! Three rounds rapid fire!*"

**BOOM BOOM BOOM BOOM**

The entire high ground was completely engulfed by fire and thick smoke this time.

The surviving Royal Army's magic crystal artillerymen completely collapsed. They abandoned their weapons and ignored the precious magic crystal cannons. The artillerymen fled towards the rear of the position crying and completely losing their order.

This was merely the prelude to the entire battle.

All the magic crystal cannon positions marked by Max and other reconnaissance teams suffered devastating blows from two 77mm field cannon battalions and three 105mm howitzer batteries on the outskirts of Seville. The German artillery proved that truth lies only within the range of artillery fire.

Britain's magic crystal cannons may indeed be very powerful. They still lagged behind the German Empire in terms of range and artillery tactics. The royal artillery trained in Britain was unable to mount any effective counterattack in the first round of attacks specifically targeting the magic crystal cannon positions as a result.

Several large observation balloons were launched near those artillery positions with limited visibility on the German Empire's side. The 105mm howitzer positions with excellent visibility like this one had observation vehicles that could directly guide artillery fire.

These behemoths suspended nearly 300 meters in the air not only provided precise target guidance for the German artillery but also allowed the observers in the gondolas to overlook the entire battlefield. The Royal Army troops on the outskirts of Seville were like ants on a hot pan at this moment in their eyes. The troops ran around in panic and disorder.

An observer turned the telescope to a more outward direction. A vast gray arc appeared across farmland, woods and winding country lanes. The arc was formed by soldiers in gray military uniforms and mixed color uniforms.

The arc was slowly but irresistibly closing in on Seville at this moment like a noose.

General José Sanjurjo, commander of the 24th Division, is a man who sleeps very well. Cannons roared outside Seville while he remained fast asleep in his boudoir.

Sanjurjo's chief of staff rushed into his privately requisitioned detached house and roughly woke him from his mistress's arms. The division commander of the Royal Army realized the enemy had launched an attack only then.

Sanjurjo learned from his chief of staff that 70% of the artillery positions outside the city had been hit by precise artillery fire. He completely lost his mind. Sanjurjo shoved his mistress off the bed and jumped up abruptly. He looked utterly incredulous.

"*What did you say? Seventy percent of the magic crystal cannon positions are destroyed?!*"

"*Yes, General. The enemy's artillery fire is too accurate. For some reason they have a very good grasp of the location of our magic crystal cannon positions.*"

Sanjurjo felt a sudden dizziness. He had planned to force the enemy's main force to gather and launch an attack by retreating back to Seville. Sanjurjo would then inflict heavy casualties on them by utilizing the powerful anti crowd capability of the magic crystal cannon. This tactic had achieved very good results against the National Army and the International Brigades on other fronts.

The British also stated that even German infantry would suffer heavy losses when bombarded by magic crystal cannons. What the British did not tell Sanjurjo was that the German artillery would not be suppressed however.

The battle had barely begun when their most important heavy firepower had been severely damaged. Sanjurjo felt unsure of what to do next. He forced himself to calm down and practically roared at the chief of staff.

"*Pass on my order! All units immediately move into their designated positions! Organize the defense according to the original plan! Under no circumstances should the enemy be allowed to approach Seville!*"

The chief of staff nodded and was about to say something when the door to the room was suddenly pushed open again. A messenger rushed in stumbling and crawling. His voice trembled with tears.

"*General! Something terrible has happened! The enemy's main ground forces are launching a full scale attack on our southeastern defense line!*"

The whistling of the last round of shells faded into the distance. The sharp sound of the charge whistle shattered the brief silence on the battlefield.

"*All company, attack!*"

Captain Hauser's roar echoed across the 3rd Company's position. A large number of figures in field gray uniforms leaped up from behind the earthen mounds and shell craters where they were hiding then.

The three companies of the 1st Battalion spread out in multiple skirmish lines with each soldier spaced 4 to 5 steps apart. The entire column resembled a thin gray line surging towards the high ground that was still belching black smoke.

A total of 2 divisions participated in the offensive on this front outside Seville. The German Expeditionary Force, the International Brigades, three infantry brigades and two cavalry regiments of the National Army were included. The 1st Battalion of the 32nd Regiment where Max was stationed was just one of the tactical units in this large scale offensive.

The entire horizon was filled with soldiers rushing forward looking out. Their shouts of battle, officers' commands and heavy footsteps all mixed together.

This is a symphony unique to war.

Max was also a note in this symphony. This was the first time he had personally participated in such a grand charge.

Max gripped the rifle tightly and felt his heart pounding wildly in his chest. He felt deeply excited.

Countless blue arrows representing the 16th Infantry Brigade and friendly forces resembled a giant comb on the system map in the upper left corner of the view. The arrows brushed across the land outside Seville. The 32nd Infantry Regiment was located on the far left flank of the entire brigade. Their 1st Battalion was on the far left flank of the regiment.

The 1st Battalion having already suffered casualties was now in charge of a magic crystal cannon position that had been attacked by artillery. The effectiveness of the 105mm field howitzer was obvious. The battalion encountered almost no decent resistance during its ascent of the high ground.

Twisted corpses of Royal Army soldiers and weapons blown to pieces were everywhere on the shattered artillery positions. The air was filled with the pungent smell of gunpowder, dirt and blood.

Max led the 3rd Platoon and stepped on the soft scorched earth. They quickly occupied the territory that had just belonged to the enemy. Those magic crystal cannons that once posed a challenge to everyone have now turned into piles of twisted metal emitting black smoke and no longer posed a threat.

Max cautiously approached the high ground on the side closest to Seville. The view suddenly opened up there. Max could clearly see the overall situation of the battlefield with the guidance of the system map. This was the first time he had experienced what it was like for an army of 2 divisions to launch a frontal attack.

The three infantry regiments of the German Army's 16th Infantry Brigade were the main attacking force in the center. They were lined up like a sharp dagger and had already pierced directly into the Royal Army's defenses.

Another unit was launching an equally fierce attack about 600 meters to the left of the 1st Battalion meanwhile. Those were soldiers from the International Brigades.

Their clothing was a motley collection. Their equipment consisted of outdated rifles provided by the German Empire with no heavy machine guns or mortars to provide fire support. Their fighting spirit was frighteningly tenacious yet.

The International Brigades faced the Royal Army's defenses and practically pushed forward with their bare flesh and blood. They launched a fearless charge after only a brief exchange of fire. The brigades used bayonets and shovels in close quarters combat to drive back the numerically superior enemy.

"*Attention, all companies! Establish a defensive line on the spot! Hold this high ground!*"

Major Thomas's order was quickly relayed to every company by messengers just as Max was getting excited about the International Brigades' attack. This is a decision that is entirely in line with current German military manuals.

This high ground offers an excellent view overlooking a large area and makes it a superb artillery observation post and fire support point. They could even deploy artillery directly from here and use artillery fire to cover the entire Seville. The high ground must be firmly under their control therefore.

The soldiers immediately sprang into action and used existing shell craters and crates and sandbags abandoned by the enemy to quickly construct makeshift shelters.

*This is not the best place to set up.*

Max knew very well that the previous reconnaissance was unlikely to have marked all of the enemy's artillery positions. No matter how well the counter battery operation was executed it is highly unlikely that all of the enemy's long range firepower points were destroyed therefore.

This area was previously the location of the Royal Army's magic crystal cannons. The enemy must have the precise coordinates and geographical information of this high ground since this was the case. The Germans have occupied this area now. The surviving enemy artillerymen barring any idiots will inevitably target this place in their next bombardment.

*The 1st Battalion has now concentrated almost all of its forces in this small area. It is like they have a sign that says come and bomb me written all over them.*

"*Sergeant! Order all squads to disperse immediately! Squads must maintain a distance of at least 20 meters from each other! Soldiers must also maintain distance from each other! Hurry! Hurry! Hurry!*"

Max sensed the danger and roared an order. He waved his hands to make the surrounding soldiers disperse. Sergeant Klaus did not understand why. Klaus already had an almost blind trust in Max however. He immediately blew his whistle and loudly relayed the order.

The soldiers of the 3rd Platoon moved quickly. They swiftly formed a loose defensive formation under the puzzled gazes of the soldiers from other companies.

Max thought of having Major Thomas disperse the other troops as well after giving orders to his own troops. Time was of the essence. Max did not have time to think much. He immediately found Captain Hauser who was directing the soldiers to prepare for defense.

"*Captain! We cannot gather here!*"

Max's voice was very urgent at that moment.

"*The enemy must know the coordinates of this hill. Their surviving artillery could launch a barrage of fire at any time!*"

Captain Hauser paused for a moment and glanced at the busy soldiers around him. He looked at Max's serious face then.

"*Your concerns are valid, but we do need to prepare for defense now. This high ground is very important. The enemy will definitely counterattack!*"

"*I understand! But I hope the troops can at least spread out a bit. Even if they are attacked by enemy artillery the casualties will not be too heavy then.*"

Captain Hauser hesitated for less than two seconds before nodding heavily.

"*Alright! I will go and remind the major right away. You still need to make sure the troops are prepared to defend against an enemy counterattack however!*"

Max returned to his position in the 3rd Platoon after reminding Captain Hauser. Max saw that his soldiers had dispersed as instructed. He found a shell crater and lay down.

Max could feel the danger in the air. His hunch was right this time unfortunately.

The soldiers of the 1st Battalion had finished building simple defensive fortifications using the shell craters and were about to catch their breath just then. A strange shriek came from the distant horizon.

The sound was completely different from the whistling of ordinary shells. The sound was sharper and more piercing like the cry of some strange bird.

A dazzling blue sphere of light appeared out of thin air in the sky above the highlands immediately afterwards. The sphere suddenly exploded then.

There was no earth shattering roar and only a muffled popping sound.

**BOOM**

An invisible terrifying force spread outwards from the center of the sphere of light. Countless fragments of unknown material gleaming with a cold light rained down from the sky and instantly covered a large area in the center of the highlands.

**BOOM**

Two more identical explosions rang out. Two identical blue orbs of light exploded one after another in the sky above different locations on the high ground.

Max felt his ears ringing. He lay tightly in the crater. Max could clearly hear the shrapnel splattering into the surrounding soil. He did not know if he had been hit by shrapnel. Max only knew that he was still alive for the time being.

This highland instantly turned into a living hell after the three airbursts.

The piercing screams and wails rose and fell and replaced all the previous sounds.

Max struggled to his feet and felt dizzy. Max confirmed that he was not injured. He looked at the 1st and 2nd Companies located in the center of the high ground after that.

The high ground was littered with mutilated corpses and wounded soldiers groaning in pain. The blood had stained the scorched earth dark red. The scene of devastation indicated that the two companies were almost completely engulfed by the hail of bullets.

The final explosion affected the 3rd Company and its headquarters.

The blue soldier's badge representing the 1st Battalion was flashing wildly on the system map. Max knew that this meant the battalion was under attack and suppression even without looking at the system's annotations.

Max quickly realized that this must be an attack from the magic crystal cannon. This magical weapon from Britain surprised Max greatly both in terms of the power of its airburst and its accuracy.

*They did not even take time for calibration. They just opened fire on this hill and every shot hit its mark. With the airburst and first shot hit I now understand why the magic crystal cannon can go toe to toe with the field howitzer.*

Preparations for the next round of firing began at a camouflaged magic crystal cannon position 3 kilometers away just as Max was marveling at the absurd accuracy of the attack. A large number of Royal Army artillerymen and magic technicians were carefully maintaining the magic crystal cannons that had just finished firing. They checked the runes and crystals on the cannons for any damage. A figure dressed in the robes of a Highland mage floated silently above this magic crystal cannon position at an altitude of about 200 meters.

"*High ground 127 successfully suppressed. Preparing to switch guidance targets.*"

"*Received. Temporary maintenance on the magic crystal cannon is complete. Please provide attack information.*"

"*Hill 125. Target rebel infantry group. Use fragmentation magic crystal shells. Begin guidance after loading.*"

The mage apprentice from the Highland Mage Guild stared coldly at the location he had chosen as his next target. That was not just an enemy for him but also his ticket to leaving his apprenticeship and becoming a full fledged mage. The mage apprentice was already an apprentice in the Highland Mage Order. He understood that only by completing the promotion would he be qualified to be called a Highland Mage. He also thanked the Royal Army's incompetent artillerymen. The mage apprentice would not have been able to unleash the full power of the magic crystal cannons if they had not been so bad at it because of this. High ranking mentor Eldridge would not allow his mage apprentices to come and assist the enemy in guiding the artillery barrage otherwise.

The bracelet on the left wrist vibrated and indicated that the wearer of another bracelet on the ground was sending a message. Highland mages can send magical messages at will without exhausting their reserves. The issue is that receiving those magical messages requires prohibitively expensive enchanted equipment that only other trained mages can afford or create. This short range magical communication bracelet set has become an indispensable part of the magic crystal cannon's guidance because the artillery crews below cannot receive magical messages without it therefore.

"*Fragmentation crystal shells are loaded!*"

"*Understood. Beginning guidance.*"

The mage apprentice locked his gaze on the 125 high ground and opened his right hand and raised it. He used the space between his thumb and forefinger as a crosshair. A wave of magical energy appeared at the space between his thumb and forefinger then.

The magic crystal cannons on the ground roared again soon. The magic crystal projectiles that streaked across the sky began to fall after passing their highest point. An unseen force began to correct the trajectory of these shells then. The force caused them to fall straight toward Hill 125 and finally complete the airburst at a height of less than 10 meters.

Max was surprised by the magic crystal cannon on Hill 127 on the other side. He also knew that in this world with magic and magical technology many things were indeed inexplicable.

The system also deduced the approximate location of the magic crystal cannon position based on the trajectory of the attack and marked it at this moment. Max immediately took out his binoculars and looked in that direction. He quickly found the fish that slipped through the net.

It was a camouflaged position hidden in the farm. The camouflage should be quite good judging from the large number of dead branches and ears of wheat scattered around the magic crystal cannon. No wonder the previous reconnaissance failed to find it.

The system map also marked the exact location of the position after Max saw it however.

"*Messenger!*"

Max waved to a soldier who was also covered in dirt and grime not far away. Max quickly pulled out a notebook and pencil from his pocket and scribbled down the detailed coordinates and terrain description of the hidden magic crystal cannon position on the paper as the other party ran over.

"*Immediately send this to the artillery command post at the rear as fast as possible! Tell them this is an enemy magic crystal cannon position that has not been destroyed!*"

The young messenger took the note and nodded heavily. He rushed off towards the rear without hesitation then.

"*Organize defenses! Prepare for battle!*"

The surviving officers on the high ground roared as they reorganized the surviving but terrified soldiers meanwhile. A dark mass of royalist soldiers had launched a counterattack below the high ground because of this.

"*Platoon! Report casualties!*"

Max shouted.

Sergeant Klaus and several corporals quickly tallied the results. Their faces were filled with grief.

"*Platoon leader! The 2nd Squad is gone! All of them are gone!*"

"*1st Squad and 4th Squad each lost four men! 3rd Squad had two slightly wounded! 5th Squad had one seriously wounded and two slightly wounded!*"

"*The other squads were further away so there have been no other casualties so far!*"

The soldiers in his platoon had only been with Max for less than 48 hours. Max's heart still bled when he heard about the casualties. Max knew this was not the time for grief however. He ordered his squads to treat the wounded. Max rushed to the edge of the high ground and set up his binoculars again after that.

At least one battalion of Royal Army troops was surging toward the high ground in dense formation through the somewhat blurry field of vision of the telescope.

Max glanced at his own positions. The 1st and 2nd Companies had already suffered heavy losses the day before. The recent artillery bombardment had only made matters worse. Countless people were killed instantly by the shrapnel from the magic crystal cannon. Most of the surviving soldiers were also wounded.

The stretcher teams organized by the company's medical sergeant were simply not enough. The morale of the entire battalion was extremely low.

Major Thomas and three company commanders were loudly encouraging the others. The soldiers gradually climbed to the edge of the high ground and raised their guns under the officers' urging. Max knew very well that given the current state of the 1st Battalion it was virtually impossible to hold off an enemy battalion's charge head on however.

The only hope is to inflict enough damage on the enemy and repel their attack in the first wave. This high ground will definitely not be able to be held today otherwise.

Max's gaze swept back and forth along the enemy's attack route. A bold idea quickly took shape in his mind.

"*Klaus! Sergeant Jonah!*"

Max roared.

"*Yes sir!*"

"*You two along with the heavy machine gun team take the rest of the 1st and 4th Squads and come with me!*"

Max pointed to the relatively complex slope on the left side of the high ground.

"*Let us flank them from the side and give them a good beating!*"

Sergeant Klaus and Corporal Jonah immediately called for the designated soldiers to follow without hesitation. The Maxim machine gun team is currently assigned five soldiers. Three of them worked together to lift the already assembled machine gun and advance. Another person picked up two ammunition boxes with both hands. The last person picked up the heavy coolant tank.

Max's soldiers had also looted less than 500 rounds of ammunition from the second floor of the farmhouse when they captured the machine gun. This amount of ammunition was not much for a heavy machine gun that needed to maintain continuous firepower. The rifles used by the Royal Army are the same .303 British caliber. Royal Army soldiers carry approximately 150 rounds each. Max and his team had found plenty of loose ammunition however. They had not had the time or supplies to load more belts. Max could not care less about that now.

"*Baumann, Fischer, Müller! You take the rest of the men and stay here! Help Captain Hauser and the others hold the front! Hold off the enemy as much as possible before we open fire!*"

"*Understood, Platoon Leader!*"

Max did not delay any longer after giving instructions to the other three corporals. He led two squads and a heavy machine gun team and quickly slipped down the left slope of the hill.

Their actions had to be swift and covert. The undulating gentle slopes and the crater filled terrain provided them with natural cover fortunately.

The gray uniforms of the soldiers provided decent cover on the smoke filled battlefields after autumn arrived. They were better than the French wearing red trousers at least.

Max ran in the lead. His brain worked at lightning speed. The system map in his field of vision kept adjusting and planning the safest and fastest route for him.

This operation was fraught with danger. Their small squad would be found and surrounded if they were discovered by the enemy. Their fate would be predictable then.

The Royal Army was so focused on assaulting the high ground that no one noticed the small force quietly flanking them fortunately.

Max led everyone to the designated location about a minute later. A huge depression created by artillery shells formed a perfect natural machine gun position. One can clearly see the long undefended flank of the Royal Army's attacking force struggling to advance up the slope from here.

Normally a commander would consider this attack order carefully before issuing it. The Royal Army was eager to recapture these high grounds however. Their commander did not think much of it and simply ordered the Royal Army's forces to charge forward first.

"*Quickly! Set up the machine gun!*"

Max ordered the machine gun team in a low voice. Several soldiers deftly set up the heavy Maxim machine gun. The loader less skilled loaded the canvas ammunition belts.

"*Everyone else. Establish a circular defensive perimeter around the machine gun. Prepare for battle.*"

The soldiers quickly spread out and lay prone on the ground. They pulled back the bolts of their rifles. The dark muzzles pointed at the attacking Royal Army and were wary of any potential threat.

Max lay prone next to the machine gun and raised his binoculars. The Royal Army's first wave of attacks has begun.

The Royal Army soldiers also formed three relatively dense lines and charged towards the top of the hill with shouts just like the German soldiers' attack that Max had seen before. Soldiers from another company in a close knit column were behind these three ranks of soldiers.

The soldiers of the Royal Army in the front row were less than 200 meters away from the front line guarded by the 1st Battalion at this moment. Sporadic gunfire rang out from the high ground. It was the soldiers of the 1st Battalion firing their rifles with precision and attempting to slow the enemy's advance.

"*Platoon leader, can we fire now?*"

The machine gunner licked his chapped lips. A hint of excitement was in his voice. It was his first time firing although. He seemed to have anticipated how much damage an attack in this direction would inflict on the enemy.

"*Hold on.*"

Max's voice remained calm as he placed his hand on the machine gunner's shoulder.

"*Let them charge forward a bit more and then attack when they cannot retreat easily!*"

Time seemed to stretch on endlessly at that moment. Every second felt like an eternity. Max could hear the heavy breathing of himself and the soldiers around him.

150 meters.

120 meters.

100 meters!

Now!

"*Fire.*"

Max suddenly patted the shooter on the shoulder.

**TA TA TA TA TA**

The Maxim heavy machine gun roared and spat out a long tongue of fire. The scorching bullets swept fiercely towards the unsuspecting flank of the Royal Army like the scythe of death.

The Royal Army's soldiers who were charging forward with all their might never dreamed that such a deadly firing point would suddenly appear on their flank. They were stuck in an awkward position now. The .303 bullets easily tore through their packed formation then.

Many soldiers did not even have time to react before being hit by bullets and falling to the ground. The attack was from the flank. The machine gunners perceived the enemy's attacking line as a column because of this. He does not need to adjust the angle much to bring an entire squad of enemies into firing range.

The roar of machine guns and the cries of the Royal Army's soldiers instantly echoed across the battlefield.

"*Fire. Fire. Shoot their officers first.*"

Max also loudly gave the order to attack to the riflemen around him after the machine guns opened fire.

More than 20 rifles opened fire simultaneously. The precise bullets fired by the Gewehr 98 continuously killed the Royal Army's officers who were trying to find cover or organize a counterattack.

The uniforms of the Royal Army's officers are clearly different from those of ordinary soldiers to highlight the officers' status and make it easier for soldiers to find them during combat. They even added silver thread decorations to their black clothes to highlight their status. This made them easy targets for Max and the other soldiers.

The entire Royal Army's offensive formation collapsed completely at that moment.

A flank attack by a heavy machine gun is devastating against densely packed infantry. They were completely exposed to the machine gun's firing range. Advancing meant death. Retreating meant death. Lying still meant death as well.

The slope in front of the high ground had become a veritable slaughterhouse.

Sergeant Klaus and Sergeant Jonah were filled with shock and fervor. They mechanically pulled the bolt, aimed and fired. They sent bullets into the enemy's body. The positions gave them perfect overlapping fields of fire. Klaus had drilled fire discipline into his men for two years. Seeing it work under actual contact was different from the training ground however. Jonah felt his breath come easier as the pattern held. The squads knew their business. Platoon Leader Max had placed them well. The corporals were managing their sections without needing orders shouted down the line. This was doctrine executed cleanly and it was working.

The regimental machine gun companies existed to anchor defensive lines and break enemy attacks from prepared emplacements. Carrying a water cooled Maxim forward into an assault was lunacy by conventional thinking. The weight alone argued against it. Five men required just to move the gun, tripod and ammunition. Max had turned that conventional wisdom on its head. He used the enemy's own doctrine against them. The Royal Army expected machine guns to sit on the ridge firing frontally. They never anticipated one repositioning to rake their exposed flank mid assault.

The Royal Army's first charge ended in utter defeat after more than 70% of the first three ranks of soldiers were killed on the spot. The dense column following behind these three ranks of soldiers even attempted to launch a charge against Max and his men. Their denser formation made it easier for the machine gunners to fire however. A single .303 bullet could penetrate two enemies in succession at this range.

The entire dense column completely collapsed just seconds after the machine gun fire turned around.

Max even saw a sinister smile gradually emerge on the face of the machine gunner who had seemed somewhat nervous and inexperienced before firing. That was a smile unique to suppressing firepower.

The surviving soldiers of the Royal Army completely collapsed faced with the relentless unstoppable machine gun fire. They cried out and dropped their weapons. They fled backwards in a panic. Their morale completely collapsed.

Several officers who still wanted to hold out kept raising their sabers in an attempt to stop the retreat. Officers of all ranks in the Royal Army still maintained the tradition of carrying sabers with them yes. This conspicuous action on the battlefield was quickly thwarted by the precise shooting of Sergeant Klaus and his men however.

The Royal Army's soldiers who had surged up like a tide retreated from the high ground just as the tide receded.

Max breathed a sigh of relief seeing this. He stopped the machine gunner from continuing to fire then.

"*Alright, alright. Conserve your ammunition! We still have a lot to fight!*"

Max looked around at everyone after he finished speaking.

"*Check everyone for injuries and then check the ammunition!*"

Everyone completed the confirmation that no one was injured soon. The Royal Army had not been able to launch an effective counterattack at all or rather. Everyone had used up nearly one sixth of their ammunition however. The Maxim machine gun had almost emptied its first ammunition belt.

"*The regimental machine gun company should be deployed to the front lines. There are already too few of them and now they are not being used.*"

Max lay face down on the ground and tried to relax his tense body a little. His mind turned to equipment and doctrine. Better portable machine guns would help. Something air cooled that two men could carry instead of five. The Pickelhaube offered no protection against shrapnel or debris. A proper steel helmet covering the temples and back of the skull would save lives. The Maxim worked but moving it was an ordeal. Artillery observation needed improvement too. Better optics, better communication between spotters and batteries.

"*Platoon leader, what is the next step?*"

Sergeant Klaus approached Max with his eyes shining and asked.

"*Let us prepare to move to a different position. We have just exposed our firepower. The enemy's next attack might target us.*"

Max got up from the ground and focused his attention on the system map. He prepared to find another temporary machine gun position. Max actually found a more suitable position then.

The spot was a bit far from the top of the hill. Several large trees that had been knocked down by artillery fire lay there haphazardly however. They served as natural cover.

Max immediately sent a soldier back to the top of the hill to check on the situation of the rest of the camp and to relay his plans to Captain Hauser and his men.

Max led the remaining men and carried the heavy Maxim machine gun and quietly began their relocation after doing all that. The defeated Royal Army soldiers were still fleeing down the hillside. No one noticed the movements of their small detachment. The entire transfer went surprisingly smoothly.

Max immediately ordered everyone to disperse and find cover and do a final check of their weapons once the group arrived at the new temporary position.

"*Drink some water and catch your breath. There is probably going to be a tough battle later.*"

Max unscrewed the canteen and took a sip. He took advantage of this rare break to immerse himself completely in the system map in the upper left corner of his field of vision.

The fog of war that had shrouded Seville was largely dispelled as friendly forces advanced across the board. The fog revealed the situation along the entire front. Red soldier badges representing the Royal Army units were flashing and disappearing one by one.

The main offensive force centered on the 16th Infantry Brigade has captured all the high ground with a wide field of view southeast of Seville. Most of those high grounds with open views have now changed hands.

The battalions of two 77mm field artillery battalions are also slowly moving forward and preparing to occupy new positions and provide closer fire support for the upcoming offensive. The three 105mm howitzer batteries which were not so mobile continued their attack from their original positions.

The overall situation seemed to be very favorable. It seems only a matter of time before the Germans completely breach Seville's outer defenses if this trend continues.

Max knew this would not last long however. He clearly remembered that the intelligence he brought back specifically mentioned several elite enemy units. The Highland Mages, the armored knights of the Order of the Garter and the Northumberland Fusilier Regiment were mentioned.

The enemy who captured Max when he arrived in this world was also from the 4th Battalion of the Northumberland Fusiliers.

None of these elite British units have appeared on the map to this day however.

*Is the enemy really planning to abandon the outer positions and bet everything on urban warfare?*

The thought flashed through Max's mind. He immediately dismissed it however. This is completely contrary to the mainstream military thinking of this era. Relying on strong city defenses to fight street battles would be a choice made only by a huge disadvantage.

A new situation arose before Max could figure things out however.

"*Platoon leader! They are coming up again!*"

Sergeant Klaus who was lying behind the fallen tree trunk on guard duty lowered his voice and said this.

"*The routed soldiers have been stopped by their officers and are regrouping!*"

Another new force appeared in Max's view below the high ground almost at the same time as Klaus's report. Their uniforms were khaki. This was a stark contrast to the black uniforms of the Royal Army.

A light and rhythmic trumpet sound mixed with the beat of a military drum drifted from the foot of the hill. The melody was all too familiar to Max. It was the Grenadier March.

Max's heart skipped a beat. He rushed to Klaus's side and raised his binoculars.

The newly appeared British troops wore khaki field service dress and soft peaked caps. At the very front of their ranks stood rows of heavily armored soldiers.

Some wielded enormous two handed swords. Others held a kite shield in one hand and a single handed sword in the other. All of them exuded a chilling aura that kept strangers at bay.

The system map then displayed detailed information about this unit.

```
British Empire - Northumberland Fusiliers Regiment - 4th Battalion

Officers: 30

Soldiers and Non-commissioned Officers: 997
```

Max felt a sense of unease upon seeing this unit to be honest. Max looked at this reinforced battalion of nearly 1,000 men. A British battalion carried more strength than most German regiments. The 1st Battalion which had already suffered heavy losses on the high ground will now face an enemy several times their size. An elite force at that.

The early tactic of armies deploying entire battalions at once has now become the most deadly numerical advantage.

They had played all their cards. The Maxim machine gun only had one ammunition belt left. This was simply not enough to reverse such a huge disparity in troop strength.

*How to do this?*

Max's mind raced. He gritted his teeth after a few seconds of deliberation.

"*Turn the machine gun around!*"

Max pointed to the British troops that were assembling.

"*Point your guns at those British! As for the Royal Army's soldiers leave them to the riflemen! And trust our allies on the high ground!*"

The machine gun team immediately began frantically adjusting the direction of their machine guns upon receiving Max's order. The drumbeats of the Grenadier's March grew increasingly intense. The two enemy forces below the hillside completed their assembly. The dark mass of people began to slowly move upwards.

"*Listen up!*"

Max's voice was unusually serious as he instructed the group.

"*Once the machine gun runs out of ammo we will retreat from the flank and rear of the hill! Do not be stupid and charge up the hill. That is just making us easy targets for the enemy!*"

"*Yes, Platoon Commander!*"

The reorganized Royal Army soldiers were clearly still shaken by the flanking attack they had just suffered down the hillside. Max could clearly see that as they advanced upwards they would occasionally cast wary glances toward the machine gun position where they had previously been.

They never dreamed that the machine gun that had claimed the lives of so many of their comrades had quietly moved to a different location and was now aiming at their allies from a different angle however.

Max instructed everyone to keep their bodies low to the ground and use the fallen tree trunks and craters to perfectly conceal themselves.

The enemy's two armies are getting closer and closer. Max's heartbeat quickened with the rhythm of the military drums.

Max was not really sure himself. He witnessed his own heavily armored units taking direct hits from pistol bullets at close range when he was captured. Max was somewhat worried about how much damage the Maxim's .303 bullets would actually do to those British tin cans therefore.

The drumbeats of the military band suddenly quickened. The two enemy ranks began to jog.

The remaining soldiers of the 1st Battalion had begun to fire sporadically in an attempt to halt the enemy's offensive at the top of the hill. The soldiers of the Royal Army also sparsely raised their guns to return fire.

The time has come.

Max no longer hesitated.

"*Fire!*"

The silent temporary position was instantly engulfed in gunfire at a single command.

**TA TA TA TA TA**

The remaining ammunition of the Maxim heavy machine gun was unleashed without reservation and turned into a deadly whip of fire that lashed fiercely at the ranks of the Northumberland Fusilier Regiment. Sergeant Klaus and his men also opened fire with their 20 odd Gewehr 98 rifles on the other side. Max fired alongside them.

Accurate bullets flew less than 200 meters and struck the Royal Army soldiers who were charging upwards. The position made it impossible not to get a few kills.

With his first shot Max watched three soldiers tumble to the ground and could not stop a smile from reaching his face. He knew it was insensitive to feel joy at their demise but he could not help but whisper to himself.

*Oh baby a triple.*

As Max continued to fire he could feel his body getting lighter and his senses sharper. His gun felt more controllable in his hand and the recoil more manageable.

Ignoring the notifications that continued to tick up Max could vaguely feel something changing in his body much like it did with the natural recovery druid circle. He did not have time to experiment however.

The Royal Army which had just been decimated by the flanking fire of heavy machine guns suffered a familiar blow once again. This time there were only about 20 rifles although. The killing efficiency was far less than before. The attacks from the flanks that could not be effectively avoided still threw their attack formation into disarray however.

The soldiers at the front fell one after another. Those behind instinctively sought cover while this happened. The entire charge stalled because of this.

A burst of gunfire erupted from the top of the hill at the same time. This clearly indicated that the other soldiers of the 1st Battalion had begun to consciously coordinate with Max and his men to form a crossfire.

Max watched the notifications tick up with every kill but he was more concerned about the situation on the other side however.

The machine gun bullets struck the armor of the heavily armored soldiers in the front ranks of the Northumberland Fusilier Regiment and scattered a series of dense sparks. The bullets made a crisp clanging sound and caused the heavily armored soldiers to pause in their movements.

It works.

Max was pleased. He was thoughtful again soon however. The killing efficiency is too low because of the armor.

Those heavily armored units basically only staggered and fell down after being hit directly by multiple machine gun bullets. Max could even clearly see a soldier wielding a two handed greatsword whose chest armor withstood 5 or 6 bullets before finally shattering and deforming through his binoculars. Machine gun bullets fired at him could penetrate his body only then.

This level of casualties is negligible for a force of nearly 1,000 men.

The soldiers of the Northumberland Fusiliers did not retreat after a brief commotion. They quickened their pace of the charge instead. The khaki colored wave was rushing toward Max and his group with unstoppable force.

"*Machine gunner! Stop firing indiscriminately! Target them one by one! Focus on those armored units!*"

The machine gunner immediately stopped his sweeping fire and began aiming at the heavily armored soldiers one by one upon hearing Max's words. The rapidly decreasing ammunition supply also means that this attack cannot last much longer however.

The turning point of the battle appeared in an unexpected way at this moment of despair however.

Max noticed that the troop badge representing the 1st Battalion, 2nd Regiment of the International Brigade was rapidly approaching his location on the system map out of the corner of his eye. The two sides were not far apart and only about 600 meters separated them. These hundreds of soldiers from the International Brigades appeared on the flank and rear of the Northumberland Fusiliers in the blink of an eye because of this.

A loud horn sounded from that direction immediately afterwards.

"*Come on!*"

"*Ula!*"

A variety of deafening shouts of battle followed.

This sudden turn of events clearly caught the British who were charging forward somewhat off guard. They had to divert some of their forces and hastily turn around to try and engage this unexpected surprise attack.

The resounding bugle call not only ignited the fighting spirit of the International Brigades volunteers but also made Max's blood boil.

**TA TA TA TA. CLICK**

The machine gunner finally fired the last bullet from the canvas ammunition belt.

"*Lieutenant, should we retreat?*"

Sergeant Klaus asked the crucial question. All the soldiers including him had already guessed the answer however. Their hands reached for the scabbards at their waists.

"*Bayonets ready!*"

Max practically roared the order and deftly drew his bayonet and mounted it on his rifle. The cold touch from his palm sharpened his mind.

The International Brigades soldiers had come to rescue Max and his men clearly. Max could not bring himself to abandon them and retreat under these circumstances.

The battle has come to this point. There is no choice but to fight to the death now.

"*Charge!*"
