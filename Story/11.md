# Chapter 11

Arc: Seville Counterattack

Date: Late January 1914

---

While waiting for the first batch of castings to cool the tense work finally had a brief pause.

The temporary workshop was thick with the smell of metal and sweat. Everyone was exhausted. Each person found a place to sit and rest.

Max took a canteen and several pieces of black bread from his orderly Hans and distributed them to the chemistry professor and the workers.

*"Eat something first, gentlemen. There is still much work ahead."*

The workers were not polite. They took the food and bit into it with large bites.

The professor thanked him gracefully and sipped his water in small mouthfuls.

The bearded International Brigade commander also came over and sat down beside Max with a heavy thump. He pulled out a battered metal flask from his coat.

*"Want some? Liquor from my homeland. Clears the head."*

Max smiled and waved his hand.

*"Thank you but I will pass. I never drink before battle."*

*"You really are a disciplined German."*

The bearded commander took a large gulp himself and breathed out a strong alcoholic vapor.

Max looked at the busy International Brigade volunteers in the distance. He could not help asking a question he had held for a long time.

*"Andrei, I have always wanted to ask you. Why did you come here? Traveling thousands of miles to Spain to fight a war that has nothing to do with your own country."*

Andrei blew out a smoke ring. His eyes became somewhat profound.

*"For ideals, Second Lieutenant Max. For a world without oppression where all technology serves all people."*

He turned his head to look at Max. His tone revealed a kind of indignant anger.

*"Look at those British. They rely on their monopoly of magical energy resources to dominate the entire world. They treat other countries as their mines and marketplaces for dumping their commodities!"*

*"They enjoy the benefits brought by technological progress but are unwilling to let others share even a sip! If we let them control the Spanish king the consequences would be unthinkable! We came here to break this monopoly."*

*"We believe that technology and knowledge should be the wealth of all humanity! Only by letting every country develop and improving the quality of life for ordinary people will this world become better!"*

Hearing Andrei's words Max fell silent.

He had not expected that although this world's International Brigade had some differences in starting point from the International Brigade he knew they were surprisingly grand in their ultimate ideals.

Soon Max asked another question that had puzzled him all along.

*"But as international soldiers like yourselves why would you choose to ally with an autocratic empire like Germany?"*

*"Ha ha ha ha!"*

Hearing Max's words Andrei suddenly burst out laughing. However his laughter was filled with helplessness.

*"Because looking at the whole world only the German Empire's emperor and generals are willing and dare to challenge British hegemony. Only you are willing to provide us with weapons, supplies and transportation support."*

*"Then this is actually a transaction."*

*"That is correct, Second Lieutenant Max. Each of us is very clear that this is a transaction."*

The bearded Andrei's eyes became sharp. Then he continued speaking.

*"Your German Empire wants to pull us together to weaken your old rival the British Empire and expand your influence on the continent. And we need an opportunity and a battlefield where we can put our ideals into practice."*

*"Our enemy is common. That is the old order established by the British based on their monopoly of magical technology. We want to break it and let the fruits of technological progress be shared by the whole world. We want to let ordinary people in every country live better lives."*

His words were filled with passion and strength. They infected the International Brigade soldiers resting nearby.

Everyone nodded. Their eyes flashed with the light of idealism. A young man even said excitedly.

*"We will build a new world without oppression and without exploitation!"*

Max looked at these soldiers filled with ideals. He could not help asking the next question.

*"I understand your ideals."*

Max organized his words trying to make his question sound less offensive.

*"But even if you help the Crown Prince win this war Spain will ultimately still be a kingdom, a monarchical country. This seems somewhat inconsistent with the new world you pursue?"*

Hearing this Andrei laughed again. Then he patted Max on the shoulder.

*"My friend, revolution is not a feast accomplished overnight but a long and winding expedition. We never thought we could eat ourselves fat in one go."*

He took another gulp of liquor and continued speaking.

*"You speak of the kingdom but you miss the point. The old king Alfonso XIII made promises once. He did not keep them. Instead he allied with the British to maintain his grip and crush anyone who wanted change."*

*"His son is different. The Crown Prince studied in Germany. He saw what progress looks like. He promises real reforms, improved livelihoods and most importantly he is willing to accept new ideas and new technologies."*

*"Under his rule the Spanish people can at least live a hundred times better than they do now. They can eat their fill, have jobs and enjoy the conveniences brought by technological progress."*

*"That is enough. At least at this stage it is sufficient."*

*"Revolution is a tall ladder, not an easy leap."*

A voice came from the side. It was the chemistry professor from Western Russia. He adjusted his glasses and added calmly.

*"We must climb it step by step. First overthrow the most reactionary and most oppressive rule and establish a relatively enlightened order. Then step by step move toward the ultimate ideal."*

Max fell silent. He found himself unable to refute this pragmatic theory of staged revolution for the moment.

He could feel the sincerity and ideals of these people.

But to him as a transmigrator behind these beautiful visions lurked enormous historical paradoxes.

The spread of new technology would indeed bring development of productive forces but it would also quickly bring market saturation followed by almost inevitable inflation and economic crisis.

In this world background similar to the early 20th century before his transmigration what would happen after a global economic crisis was obvious.

At that time what would rise would probably not be more beautiful ideals but more extreme nationalism and fascism.

These International Brigade soldiers were now using blood and lives to ignite the spark of technological revolution for this world.

But they might not realize that this spark could equally detonate an unprecedented powder keg.

Thinking of these things Max felt an inexplicable heaviness.

He decided not to continue this heavy topic. Instead he asked about another question he had been somewhat curious about.

*"Professor, Andrei, you just mentioned that you come from the Western Russian Provisional National Government. To be honest this is the first time I have heard this name."*

The bearded commander shrugged.

*"Not surprising. Our government is currently just a makeshift operation. You cannot even find it on a map. It is normal that you have not heard of it, Second Lieutenant Max."*

*"I indeed have not heard of it. In my understanding your area should seemingly be..."*

Max had originally wanted to say Tsarist Russia but he was not sure for a moment whether it was called that in this world. So he paused.

*"Second Lieutenant Max, you mean the Great Russian Empire?"*

Mentioning this name both the bearded man and the professor showed complex expressions on their faces. There was disgust and also emotion.

And in Max's ears the long absent prompt tone sounded.

```
Current information collection: 20%

New entry unlocked in Intelligence
```

*"The Great Russian Empire? That corrupt, reactionary prison finished a year ago!"*

Andrei's tone rarely carried a trace of satisfaction.

*"Those vampires of the Romanov family were mostly sent to the gallows by angry people and got the end they deserved."*

Max's heart jumped.

This world's Tsarist Russia had already collapsed a year ago and it sounded like the Romanov family's fate was no better than in his previous life.

*"Then what about Russia now?"*

*"Russia now?"*

The chemistry professor sighed. Then he took over the conversation.

*"Russia now is just a battlefield hell engulfed in flames of war."*

*"After the Romanov family fell the entire country shattered and split into large and small forces. I do not even know how many different factions are fighting each other tooth and nail."*

*"There are those like us who hope to establish a communist country. There are also royalists who want to restore tsarist rule. There are various ethnic groups seeking independence and carving out territory. And of course there are places controlled by lawless warlords."*

*"So currently who has the strongest power?"*

*"It is the Russian Empire established by former Imperial Navy Admiral Kolchak and those royalist nobles. They have British support, excellent weapons and strong forces. They are our most powerful enemy."*

Hmmm. The White Army.

In Andrei's subsequent description Max's ears kept prompting him that more information was being collected. He himself also roughly understood Russia's current situation.

Simply put it had entered an all Russian battle royale that had started more than ten years early.

Currently two warlord forces established by former Navy Admiral Kolchak and former Army General Denikin were the most powerful.

Moreover these two major forces supported by the British had been trying to eliminate other forces blocking them in order to achieve unification.

Talking to this point Max thought of a person who could not be avoided.

*"Then Andrei have you heard of a famous proletarian revolutionary in Russia?"*

*"Revolutionary? We have plenty of revolutionaries over there. One pops up here today and another pops up there tomorrow."*

*"Vladimir Ilyich Ulyanov? Does this name ring a bell?"*

*"No."*

*"What about Lenin?"*

*"Also no. Why are you asking about this?"*

Andrei looked at Max somewhat puzzled. He did not know why this German Empire second lieutenant was suddenly so interested in Russian affairs.

*"Because you just said you want to establish a communist country. I wanted to understand what kind of person your leader actually is."*

*"Oh, you want to ask about this? Hey, you German are quite concerned about this."*

Andrei suddenly understood. He paused as if pondering something. Then he continued speaking.

*"The initial leadership group basically all sacrificed themselves in the revolution that overthrew the Romanov dynasty because of Kolchak's double dealing and the intervention of the royalist party. The current leader is someone called... Kerensky."*

*"However this person clearly does not know how to govern a country so our economy, production and such are all a complete mess now."*

The conversation with Andrei once again increased the system's information collection rate to 23%.

It also made Max increasingly curious about what would happen when this thing reached 100% collection. Would he become omniscient and omnipotent?

At the same time the system map was also greatly updated. After zooming it could be seen that many areas on the Russian side had also broken free from the fog of war. Max could see quite a few large and small forces.

The pattern and revolutionary ecology of this world were far more complex than Max had anticipated.

That man who was called the Teacher in the world before his transmigration seemed to have already exited the stage early.

Although the Romanov dynasty had ended that red country had also failed to be established. The only force that came close had Kerensky as its leader.

All of Russia had become a chaotic mess under external interference. From the current situation no side seemed capable of quickly ending this civil war.

So Andrei and the others chose to take an alternative path. If with German help they could slow down the British elsewhere or even inflict heavy damage on them the British would have less energy and resources to spare for the Russian side.

This also meant that the Kingdom of Spain was only one stop in their long journey.

*"Is this world too dark? Is this some DC universe?"*

Max thought to himself while trying to look at the familiar East on the map. Besides the tightly covered fog of war there was also a line of small text.

```
Come explore this area later
```

*"Damn it."*

Just as Max was still digesting this information a worker master suddenly shouted excitedly.

*"Done! The hollow cone is cast!"*

Everyone's eyes were instantly attracted over.

Max and Andrei immediately stopped talking and quickly walked over.

A copper hollow cone had completed cooling and was placed before everyone.

Its surface was not very smooth but the cone angle was perfect. All data also conformed to the results calculated by Max and the chemistry professor.

On the other side the artillerymen had also separated the TNT from more than ten shells. They were neatly stacked in wooden boxes.

A worker who had also worked part time as a watchmaker brought over an exquisitely structured metal gadget composed of springs, firing pins and other components. He indicated that he had also completed his work.

All the parts were ready.

*"Begin final assembly!"*

Max issued the final order.

The following steps tested skill and courage even more.

Under Max's guidance the veteran artillerymen first installed the exquisitely structured manually triggered fuze at the deepest part of the 105 millimeter shell casing.

Then they began to first add a layer of fabric to insulate the metal into the hollow shell casing. Then they refilled it with explosives.

But this time they did not fill it completely. Instead they left a precisely calculated hollow cavity at the front of the warhead.

Finally the most critical step.

They carefully installed the completely cooled copper cone into the cavity at the front end of the shell casing. The tip of the cone faced inward directly toward the explosive at the bottom of the casing.

When the last component was secured a shell with a strange appearance that looked somewhat incongruous was born.

It had the thick body of a 105 millimeter shell but the warhead was a funnel that curved inward.

The tail end had a mechanically triggered fuze. The extending structure was fixed to several cavalry lances bundled together after their spearheads were cut off.

Max glanced at the time. From start to finish it had taken one hour and thirty minutes.

This matched his impression of German army engineers in another world repairing artillery through similar methods on the battlefield.

Ludwig walked forward. He looked curiously at this freshly baked weapon. No matter how he looked at it he felt this thing seemed somewhat unreliable.

*"Max, is this what you call a can opener?"*

*"That is correct."*

Max nodded.

*"To be precise it is a shaped charge armor piercing round."*

*"Shaped charge armor piercing round. So are we supposed to stuff this thing into a gun barrel and aim and fire like other ammunition?"*

Ludwig asked his question while circling this thing curiously and vigilantly.

*"No. We need your armored knights to carry this and charge to close range to detonate it. Otherwise why do you think I added a pole to the back?"*

*"Wait."*

Ludwig froze.

*"Did you not just say this was some kind of shell? Why does it need to be manually detonated?"*

*"Because I currently have not found another reliable delivery method."*

Max shrugged. Then suddenly as if having an epiphany he looked at Ludwig with a shocked expression.

*"My goodness, this lord of the Teutonic Knights, you surely were not thinking of having infantry use this thing with their flesh and blood bodies?"*

*"What? I did not. I was not."*

*"My lord, with the explosive charge of this thing the blast alone would shake them half to death. That would be a suicide attack. I am not as cruel as you."*

*"Alright alright alright. Our armored knights will use it. Now tell me how to use it and let us do a test."*

Ludwig finally raised his hands in surrender. Then he nimbly ran to the other side and climbed onto his mount. He planned to personally conduct the test.

Before entering the cockpit, he waved his hand. He had several knight squires bring over the thickest chest main armor plate removed from the wreckage of a destroyed Order of the Garter armored knight.

This armor plate was leaned diagonally against a broken wall. It still bore exquisite heraldry and traces left by battle.

Max went over for a look. From the cross section it did not have a thickness of 60 to 70 millimeters. At most it was around 35 to 40 millimeters.

In other words through special craftsmanship and enchantment this armor plate could obtain nearly 50% defense improvement.

*"This magic really has something to it."*

Max was still clicking his tongue in wonder when Ludwig's Siegfried Type 1 armored knight moved forward.

It carefully picked up the weapon Max called a close-range shaped charge armor piercing round.

*"Just release the safety then activate it against the target?"*

Ludwig's voice came from somewhere on the armored knight. It still had some echo effects.

*"That is correct. To ensure penetration rate I suggest controlling the distance between the warhead and target at around 20 centimeters."*

*"Alright, I understand. Everyone stand back!"*

Ludwig said loudly. After everyone retreated to safe positions he raised this shaped charge armor piercing round toward the target like raising a lance.

*Could this thing really work?*

Ludwig's heart was full of doubt.

This thing looked like the product of third rate craftsmen in a rural workshop after getting drunk. It was full of wildness and uncertainty.

But he still chose to trust Max. Then he activated the armor piercing round.

**BOOM!**

A dazzling flash of fire erupted from the front of that strange shell. It instantly illuminated the entire courtyard.

Powerful force spread outward. Sand fell from the sandbag walls with a rustling sound.

Everyone instinctively pulled their heads back.

After a brief roar the courtyard fell into dead silence. Only the idle engine sound of Ludwig's armored knight remained. A pungent smell filled the air.

Several seconds later people cautiously poked their heads out from behind cover again.

Through the smoke they saw that huge white armor plate. It still stood there intact.

Failure?

This thought appeared simultaneously in the minds of most people.

*"I knew it."*

An artillery officer shook his head in disappointment.

*"Pure fantasy."*

Even the International Brigade's Andrei, the chemistry professor and those workers showed traces of regret on their faces.

Only Max showed no disappointment on his face. Instead he wore a confident smile.

Because a familiar prompt sounded in his ears.

```
Technology entry unlocked. Please check promptly.
```

When Max opened the interface according to the prompt he found that on the left sidebar a tab that had previously shown a question mark had become Technology.

However after opening it there was temporarily only one major category of Military Technology. Under this category there was currently only one technology: shaped charge armor piercing round.

Below it was a line of small text: can be improved.

*"Colonel, please go take a closer look."*

Max's voice finally broke the silence.

Ludwig in the cockpit also thought it had failed.

He was about to throw away this useless lump of metal. Hearing Max's words he was puzzled but still opened the cockpit and jumped down.

However when the smoke gradually dispersed and he saw the armor plate's full appearance he completely froze.

In the center of that apparently intact armor plate appeared a small, inconspicuous hole.

That hole was only the size of an Imperial copper pfennig. The edges were incredibly smooth. The surrounding metal showed a state of having been melted by high temperature and then solidified. It still faintly glowed red.

This... this is...

Ludwig felt his brain completely blank.

His body could not help slowly squatting down. He used his gloved finger to lightly touch the edge of that hole.

A scorching temperature transmitted clearly through the glove to his fingertip.

*"Colonel, careful it is hot!"*

Ludwig could no longer care about listening to Max's words. Because he saw the other side of the hole.

A beam of light penetrated straight through from that small hole.

*"Holy hell, it actually penetrated."*

Colonel Ludwig, who had always behaved elegantly and was held up by many as a model nobleman, could not help bursting out a curse.

This main armor plate from a Garter Knights main armored knight was just cleanly penetrated from the front by a crude handmade product with a strange shape?

How was this possible?

Ludwig suddenly turned his head. He stared at Max in the distance with eyes looking at a monster.

His lips trembled. He wanted to say something but found his throat seemed blocked by something. He could not say a single word.

All the people present now also saw clearly the wound on that armor plate.

Shock, wild joy, disbelief...

Various complex emotions appeared on everyone's faces.

Immediately after they erupted in deafening cheers!

*"Success! We succeeded!"*

*"My God! It really penetrated!"*

Those several workers and old artillerymen embraced each other excitedly. They cried and laughed.

That Western Russian chemistry teacher held his glasses and muttered to himself.

*"Incredible. Truly learning has no end. Learning has no end."*

In the midst of noise and wild joy Ludwig finally found his voice back.

He strode to Max's front. His hands grabbed his shoulders and shook forcefully.

*"I swear by the name of the Empire's Emperor. Max! What magic is this? Are you a hidden archmage?!"*

As a nobleman born into nobility who received the most orthodox knight education Ludwig's world was constructed of clear rules.

On one side was mortal steel and courage. On the other side was mysterious magic controlled by mages requiring talent and inheritance.

Between the two was an insurmountable chasm.

But today Max and that group of people he temporarily summoned used a pile of scrap metal to manufacture a weapon capable of threatening the most advanced magical constructs.

This completely destroyed his understanding.

Max did not answer the question. His gaze passed over Ludwig's shoulder and looked toward those ordinary people from different countries and different classes who were cheering. German artillerymen, International Brigade workers, Western Russian intellectuals.

On their faces bloomed the same pure joy and hope.

They came from different places with different identities holding different beliefs.

But at this moment they made no distinctions. All their faces showed the same emotion. Hope.

Max suddenly smiled.

It was a smile from the heart.

He met Ludwig's bright eyes and slowly shook his head.

*"No, Colonel."*

*"This is not magic."*

Max's voice was not loud but it clearly suppressed all the noise on site.

He broke free of Ludwig's hands. He turned. He raised his hand and pointed at that group of people with complex compositions who were surrounding the armor plate and clicking their tongues in wonder.

*"This is not magic."*

Max repeated.

*"This is his knowledge."*

He pointed to that chemistry professor wearing glasses.

*"This is their skill."*

He pointed to those several workers with callused hands.

*"This is their rigor and experience."*

He pointed again to those several relieved old artillerymen.

Finally his gaze returned to Ludwig's face. His face bore a smile more brilliant than ever before.

*"And you, Colonel. Your courage to bear risk."*

*"This is science, Colonel. A force that does not rely on mysterious bloodlines and does not need divine gifts. A force that any ordinary person can grasp as long as they are willing to learn and practice."*

He paused to let everyone present have time to digest the subversive meaning in his words.

He looked at those people. Those workers in ragged clothes. Those soldiers covered in grease. Those intellectuals full of scholarly air. On their faces was emerging a light called epiphany.

*"However in a certain sense I think it truly is a kind of magic. But it is magic that belongs to every one of us!"*

This sentence was like dropping a boulder into a calm lake. It stirred up enormous waves in everyone's hearts.

Ludwig slowly released his hands. The way he looked at Max had completely changed.

From initial curiosity to later appreciation to recent shock...

Finally settling into a complex emotion mixed with admiration and even a trace of barely detectable fear.

He completely understood the meaning behind Max's words.

This thing was not merely a weapon.

It would change war and change everything in this world.

*"Ha ha ha ha! Well said!"*

A hearty and loud laugh broke this solemn moment.

The International Brigade's Andrei strode over. His palm like a fan slapped Max's back hard enough to make him stagger.

*"What excellent magic belonging to everyone! Too well said, Comrade Max! I love this expression!"*

His laughter ignited the atmosphere on site.

Oppressive silence was completely broken. Replacing it were mountain calling and sea roaring cheers and shouts!

Soldiers and workers lifted Max high and threw him into the air.

Max smiled wryly.

*Even if it is magic for everyone, it is still a blade.*

*"Be thoughtful about it. Do not share it too easily."*

*"I would rather not die to my own invention so soon."*

A hearty laugh rolled through the crowd and heads nodded all around. Despairing emotions were swept away completely. In everyone's eyes hope's flame reignited. Moreover it burned more fiercely than ever before.

*"Quick! Mass production! How many shells do we have left? Disassemble them all!"*

*"Molds! Make more molds! Everyone move!"*

This temporarily built handicraft workshop once again became a frantically operating workshop. A simple but efficient assembly line was rapidly organized.

With successful experience their speed became faster and faster.

However the time left for them by the Royal Army and the British was not much.

As everyone was enthusiastically investing themselves in production warning whistles sounded from various parts of the city.

This represented that the enemy had new movements.

Hearing the whistles Max took a deep breath. He took one last look at that group of workers and artillerymen still enthusiastically rushing their work. He shouted loudly to Andrei and Ludwig.

*"I leave this to you! Try to make as much equipment as possible for the remaining armored knights!"*

Ludwig nodded heavily. Then he patted his chest.

*"Rest assured. We will let those British tin cans taste what power is!"*

Max delayed no longer. He turned and sprinted toward his 3rd Platoon's defensive zone.

Along the way he was amazed to discover that in just under two hours since he left most of Seville's urban area had changed appearance.

The potential that people burst out with when pushed to desperation was astonishing.

While he and Ludwig were conducting their can opener experiment German soldiers, International Brigade fighters and Spanish National Army troops had already put Max's street fighting theory into practice under the organization of their respective officers.

Many originally wide streets were now piled with various debris. Overturned carts, damaged furniture, door panels and stones removed from buildings. They formed uneven barricades.

On some major thoroughfares dense barbed wire was stretched. It glinted with cold light in the sunlight.

Large numbers of civilians dragged their families under the guidance of International Brigade and National Army soldiers who were more friendly to the people. They evacuated toward the relatively safe southern city and suburbs.

A few elderly who were unwilling to leave their homeland hid in their own cellars under neighbors' persuasion. They used heavy wooden boards to block the entrances.

Although due to time constraints many details Max emphasized such as internal building connections and camouflaged firing points were not yet completely in place.

But the current Seville already had the initial form of a street fighting meat grinder.

Max's heart settled slightly. This set of tactics was deadly enough for soldiers who had not yet experienced the baptism of brutal street fighting. Especially for the attacking side.

However he actually still felt somewhat uncertain. Although he had the support of street fighting theory the soldiers had not undergone targeted training after all. For them this was also the first time conducting street fighting.

So what it would ultimately turn into Max also did not know.

He glanced at several lines of prompts he had pinned on the system map.

```
German Empire Air Force - Zeppelin class armored airship - L30

Status: Approaching destination

Estimated arrival time: 3 hours 48 minutes
```

*"Less than four hours left. Will we be able to hold out?"*

When Max ran back to the 3rd Platoon's temporary station gasping for breath Sergeant Klaus immediately came up to meet him.

*"Platoon leader, you are back."*

Klaus's voice was still so steady. This platoon sergeant had already arranged the defensive zone the 3rd Platoon was responsible for in perfect order during Max's absence.

*"Battalion headquarters just issued orders. Several platoons from 1st and 2nd Companies that suffered relatively heavy losses were all broken up and supplemented into platoons like ours whose organization is still relatively intact."*

Hearing this Max's spirits lifted. This was good news. After all after the previous battle the 3rd Platoon's losses were also not small.

*"How many people were we allocated?"*

*"Quite a few. Not only did we fill our missing personnel but we also gained two extra infantry squads! The regimental heavy machine gun company also split off an MG 08 heavy machine gun team to us!"*

Hearing this Max was happy. It was just what he needed.

He immediately spread out the map. He pointed at the 3rd Platoon's defensive zone marked on it and began issuing orders.

Perhaps Major Thomas and the brigade's high command placed special value on Max and his platoon's combat capability. The defensive zone they were allocated was one of the few still relatively wide streets in northern Seville.

This street was called Sun Avenue. It was the most likely main attack direction the enemy would choose after entering the city.

At this moment quite a bit of barbed wire had already been laid in sections along this avenue. At the end of the street three main barricades were also constructed using sandbags and bricks.

All the alleys leading to both sides were also blocked tightly with various debris. Only a few narrow passages were left for friendly personnel to pass through.

This way the enemy's attack direction became very narrow. They could only advance straight along this street.

*"Klaus, you and the other NCOs each take one or two squads. Wait in the standby area first. After the enemy infantry comes up then enter the second and third floors of these buildings I marked out to form crossfire on this street. Remember, block all the doors and windows on the first floor for me. Enter from the back."*

*"Yes, platoon leader!"*

*"The two newly supplemented squads, have them serve as reserves and also wait in the standby area for orders. Pay attention to concealment. Do not expose yourselves easily."*

After a while there was more good news. A 77 millimeter field gun and a 105 millimeter howitzer were also allocated to this defensive zone.

Max immediately hid them behind the heavy machine gun position. The loaded shells were fragmentation and high explosive respectively.

After deploying the main forces Max kept a ten person team with himself. This included two messengers.

In this era without individual radios they would deliver his orders to every corner during this inevitably chaotic battle.

Having done all this he finally relaxed slightly. He opened the system map.

On the map red enemy markers had already appeared densely on the city outskirts. Moreover they were slowly encircling Seville.

From the information displayed on those markers the enemy had indeed received reinforcements of at least two regiments. Moreover the British Northumberland Fusiliers also had an entirely new battalion.

Friendly observation posts deployed outside the city were constantly retreating. The areas they lit up were also becoming smaller and smaller.

However one unit's icon was particularly eye catching. That was the 52nd Cavalry Regiment that had previously launched the critical rear charge on the heights.

Their marker information showed that this roaming unit currently had only 64% strength remaining.

But the cavalry regiment had not retreated. Like a pack of tireless wolves they still constantly prowled the periphery attempting to find high value targets like enemy headquarters or artillery positions.

*"You must be careful, friends."*

Max silently prayed for them in his heart.

Several minutes later the outermost red markers on the map had already reached Seville's outskirts.

Perhaps because the magic crystal cannon positions were severely damaged before this round of attack began the Royal Army surprisingly had no artillery preparation phase.

Before long the Royal Army and British vanguard troops carefully stepped into this city about to be baptized by fire.

---

Major John, the British officer responsible for forward observation, was full of doubt.

*"What is going on? Why is there no resistance?"*

He asked the Royal Army liaison officer beside him.

In his view since those Germans chose to hold fast they should concentrate all forces in the suburbs and rely on fortifications to conduct a proper decisive battle with them.

This conformed with the German Imperial military's consistent style. At least in previous colonial conflicts overseas it had always been so.

But now the suburbs were deserted. Quiet to the point of being eerie.

*"Could it be they gave up defending the city?"*

The Royal Army liaison officer was also somewhat uncertain.

*"Directly handing the city over to us?"*

This idea sounded tempting but was also too unreasonable.

Major John frowned and waved his hand.

*"Have the soldiers from the Royalist Army go in first. We follow behind. Make sure to stay alert!"*

The first batch of Royal Army soldiers boldly walked into Seville's streets.

They walked very slowly, very carefully. Gun muzzles nervously aimed at every corner that might hide people.

However after walking several dozen meters besides some scattered debris there was nothing on the outer streets.

Their courage gradually grew. Officers also came to both sides to restore the somewhat loose formation.

However when they arrived at the center of a main thoroughfare according to the map they discovered the road ahead was blocked by various messy obstacles and barbed wire. There was not even a place to step.

*"Damn it, what are these Germans doing?"*

A Royal Army officer cursed and shouted.

*"Send several people up. Cut open that barbed wire!"*

Several soldiers responded. They took huge cutting pliers and ran toward the barbed wire.

They had just run to the barbed wire front and squatted down.

**Bang!**

A crisp gunshot broke this dead silence.

A soldier holding cutting pliers had his head explode in a spray of blood. He fell straight down.

Immediately after as if it were a signal.

**Bang! Bang! Bang bang bang!**

**Da da da da da!**

All of Seville came alive.
