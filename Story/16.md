# Chapter 16

Arc: Seville Counterattack

Date: Late January 1914

---

Blood soaked the broken paving.

Max crouched in the rubble where Hauser had thrown him. His nails cut his palms and no sound came out of his throat.

Wilhelm's Siegfried Type 1 was the only German armored knight still on its feet. He drove into the panicked British infantry and cut a path with his war axe.

His target was clear. He meant to kill the wounded mage Eldridge where he lay.

Briefing files said even a high instructor had a finite number of spells each day.

Eldridge had already spent high rings in the fight including *Transmute Rock* and *Mordenkainen's Sword*.

At his level he should have no slot left that could threaten an armored knight unless he broke another precious scroll.

High scrolls were handmade by archmages and scarce even in Holy Britannian stores.

This might be the hour to end Eldridge.

Wilhelm's hate sharpened.

He walked the Siegfried Type 1 through the British cluster and cleared the space with each swing of the axe.

Soon nothing stood between him and Eldridge struggling on the ground.

He raised the weapon for the killing stroke.

Eldridge pushed himself upright on a splintered staff with blood still on his mouth.

He looked at the axe above him and smiled with pain and madness.

His shaking hand drew another plain scroll from the stained robes.

He tore the seal with his teeth.

A thin green ray left the parchment without sound and struck Wilhelm's Siegfried Type 1.

*Disintegrate*, a sixth ring spell.

The green ray brought no blast and no flash.

It spread across the Siegfried Type 1 like a ray of sun on the snow.

Steel plate and the machinery beneath it broke apart wherever the light touched.

Wilhelm had no time to cry out in the cockpit before the glow swallowed him and his machine together.

A few seconds later the light faded.

The machine had already leaned into its attack. The whole upper hull, cockpit and both arms, was gone.

Two ruined legs stood alone for a breath then toppled with a heavy clang.

The street went quiet again. No one could count how many times that had happened today.

Every man on the street froze at the sight.

Eldridge had paid in blood and a priceless scroll to erase the last unit on the field that could still threaten him at close range.

He coughed hard and spat blood again. He rose on a cracked staff and swayed on his feet.

His robes were torn and stained. His fine curls hung in soot and dust. He looked ruined.

A sick smile stayed on his pale face.

He looked around at the stunned German riflemen and the British soldiers who had also gone rigid.

He bent slowly and lifted a dress sword some British officer had dropped in the rubble.

He pointed it toward the German line and screamed through a raw throat.

*"The enemy has no soldiers left to fight! Attack! For the glory of Holy Britannia! Attack!"*

His voice broke the stillness.

British and Royal Army men woke from the shock and roared.

The German armored knights that had torn through their ranks were gone. Victory seemed within reach.

*"Charge!"*

*"Kill the German swine!"*

Officers led the line forward in a last rush on the municipal hall.

Royal Army men who had been left to clear the field marched behind them under watch from their own officers.

The street filled with running men.

German soldiers on the line needed no new order. Rifles cracked and two machine guns drew long lines through the charge.

Men in the front rank fell and the ranks behind kept coming. They fired from behind rubble and broken carts as they advanced.

The Northumberland Fusiliers believed that if they could close to the German posts the blade would finish what the guns had started.

Max gathered the survivors in the buildings along the flank and prepared to hold from the rooms while men tried to pull Ludwig free and fall back toward the main line.

The system tone rang in his head without warning.

```
German Imperial Air Force — Zeppelin-class armored airship — L30 arrived
Prepare attack guidance
```

Max lifted his head by reflex and searched the sky.

Smoke had stained the clouds a thick yellow gray. Nothing showed at first.

A new placard had appeared on the small map. The mark looked like the heaviest strategic unit the interface could show.

Over the noise of the street another sound built from above the cloud deck.

**Woooooom.**

The note drew nearer until the ground under his boots trembled.

British and Royal Army soldiers in the charge and German men on the wall all looked up toward the sound.

Even Eldridge in his frenzy frowned and his grin stiffened on his face.

Then the cloud mass split as if torn from within.

A shape too large to take in at once pushed through the gap.

A rigid airship hung over the city.

The hull matched the Zeppelins Max remembered from his old world but the envelope looked even larger.

The difference lay under the gas bag. The craft below was not three small gondolas with separate tasks.

One long structure ran under most of the envelope, more deck than car.

Four twin turrets sat on that deck, two forward and two aft. The barrels looked like naval rifles and the bore had to be heavy.

Smaller gun turrets and bomb bays studded the rest of the platform.

A black eagle with spread wings covered one side of the white envelope in paint. The mark belonged to the German Empire.

*"Our airship!"*

*"Zeppelin! It is the Zeppelin!"*

German positions erupted in a roar of disbelief and joy.

Hope had come when the line had already written itself off as lost.

*"No. That cannot be."*

Eldridge stared up at the apparition and the color drained from his face.

As a senior instructor he knew what German armored airships could do.

He had not expected one here.

*"Intelligence said every German airship was on the border facing France!"*

*"Did anyone collect a report worth reading?"*

Max was still staring at the war machine when Ludwig's cockpit opened on the fallen Siegfried Type 1.

Max sent the others to cover the street and scrambled down the rubble to the hull. He hauled the colonel clear of the wreck.

*"Colonel! Look. Our armored airship!"*

Max clapped Ludwig's shoulder hard. Relief had slipped past his control.

*"Cough. I see it. Easy."*

Ludwig leaned on Max and sheltered behind the dead machine while he pulled air through his teeth.

Several ribs were likely broken. He would not be moving fast for a while.

Once he knew he could not stand and fight he gripped Max's hand.

*"Max, I need you to finish the attack guidance."*

*"Attack guidance?"*

*"Yes. The knight was meant to guide the fire. You can see that this hull will not walk again. The cockpit locker holds a flare pistol and signal rounds for emergency marking. Take them and put a round on the British heads."*

Max understood at once.

The airship carried heavy guns but this world's methods made air support hard in a street fight where friend and foe shared the same blocks.

A misplaced salvo would kill Germans as easily as British.

The flare was probably the key to aim.

Max climbed the fallen Siegfried Type 1 at once.

The cockpit stank of oil and other fumes he could not name. There was no wheel or stick like he had imagined.

Strange gauges and crystal blocks lined the walls. Two glove rigs hung beside the seat and metal posts along the centerline looked meant to lock into a pilot suit.

He dug through the locker under the seat and found a heavy leather case.

Inside lay a flare pistol and several special rounds.

The pistol broke open like any signal gun he remembered from his old life. The rounds were another matter. Fine work covered the brass and the heads were etched with dense runes.

Max snapped the barrel open, seated one round and closed the breech.

He peered around the wreck with care. The street below had turned into a slaughter pen.

British and Royal Army soldiers charged in waves while German machine guns licked along the front. Men fell every second and the ranks behind closed the gaps at once.

*"Max, put the round where their mass is thickest."*

Ludwig braced against the hull and spoke through clenched teeth.

Max nodded, raised the muzzle and fired.

**Bang!**

The round arced up and burst above the thickest block of the British advance.

No blinding white flare followed. Pale blue sparks hung in the air for a moment and faded.

*"Report to the captain. Teutonic attack marker in view."*

An observer on the L30 pressed his face to a great lens in the transparent blister and called his sighting down.

On the glass a blue magical circle locked over one smoke choked block of the city.

*"Compute firing data. Send it to every turret. Weapons free."*

The captain in the command chair gave the order without raising his voice.

Orders ran through brass speaking tubes to the gun decks and to a small room set apart from the rest.

Blue uniformed magitech technicians crowded a complex table and worked the marker into range and bearing.

Runes flowed across a crystal face and settled into numbers that jumped to each turret.

*"Firing solution set. High explosive loaded."*

*"Open fire."*

**Boom! Boom! Boom!**

The guns spoke from the sky like distant thunder.

Eight 203 millimeter barrels in four twin turrets spat flame from the deck.

Shells screamed down the street Max had marked and struck the packed British column.

Men in the charge had no time to understand what had killed them.

Blast swallowed the street. The ground shook and brick groaned.

Heat, splinters and stone swept the street and tore apart everything in the open.

Bodies broke under fire meant for ships and walls.

One salvo stopped the rush. The street became a field of blood and fire.

That was only the first pass.

The next salvos walked deeper into the attackers while the airship slowed and dropped height.

It held a few hundred meters above the street. Rows of bomb bays along the lower deck opened.

Heavy bombs the size of gas canisters fell with mechanical clanks into the smoke below.

**Boom!**

The chain of blasts shook the whole city. A tower of black smoke and flame rose over the ruins.

Facades on both sides of the street collapsed in blocks and buried men who could not run clear.

After the guns and bombs the hemispherical MG08 turrets on the deck began to fire.

Machine gun fire lashed the ground and cut down anything that still moved.

British soldiers who had lived through the first minute broke completely.

They could not hear their officers. Survival came first and the whole mass ran rearward with screams.

Max held Ludwig behind the dead knight and stared up at the machine still pouring fire into the street.

Max had not expected them to put this much metal and spellwork into one hull.

The L30 was more heavily armed than the airships he expected but still fell far short of the airborne weaponry he had seen.

The lift and the armored deck under the hull alone spoke of tonnage far beyond any Zeppelin he had known from history, before counting four twin 203 millimeter turrets and the bomb stowage.

Up close the envelope itself rippled with shield energy.

He could see how Germany managed to keep up with Holy Britannia while British mages still led the world in raw craft.

While the airship kept height and mages could not reach it, ground troops had no solutions.

The appearance of the L30 broke what was left of British and Royal Army will to press the city.

Every attack line that could see the hull faltered.

German posts took heart in turn. Loudspeakers on the airship played imperial march music while the guns worked.

Counterattacks rose on every front still held and the attackers ran from the streets they had bled to take.

Yellow banners bearing the black eagle climbed again to the high points of Seville.

Max watched the flags rise and finally muttered to himself.

*Imperial eagle, nice.*

The enemy in front of the municipal hall line melted away after leaving the street carpeted with dead.

The airship checked its heavy fire and circled with machine guns only to pick off stragglers.

Max led a little more than twenty survivors out of the rubble.

He sent one runner to the rear for medics and stretchers for Ludwig and the other wounded.

Then he took Klaus, Baumann and the rest forward in short rushes to clear the ground the shells had plowed.

They needed to know whether any British or Royal Army men still hid in the wreckage.

They needed to know whether Eldridge had lived through the storm.

Veterans who had fought all day turned sick at the sight.

The street had lost its shape. Craters and fallen walls replaced the old stone.

British and Royal Army dead lay in layers. The airship barrage had left almost nothing whole.

Blood, powder and the stink of burned flesh mixed into one nauseating breath.

*"Platoon leader. Here."*

A rifleman called from a heap of charred brick.

Max and Klaus crossed the rubble at a jog.

They pulled charred brick aside and found Eldridge in the wreckage.

Half his body was gone. What remained still wore scorched robe with traces of rich pattern at the shoulder.

Eldridge was still alive.

He gripped a small glass vial in his one whole hand and worked at the cork with his teeth.

The cork would not come out.

*"That is him."*

Klaus said it without raising his voice.

Eldridge tried again and could not break the seal.

Max knelt beside him and held the vial steady.

Delirious hope swam in Eldridge's eyes when the glass stopped shaking in his grip.

He worked at the cork with his teeth while Max kept the bottle still.

Max twisted the bayonet off his rifle.

*"You are lucky. This is the easy way out."*

Max said it against Eldridge's ear while the mage still clung to the vial.

He drove the blade into Eldridge's chest.

Eldridge thrashed against the rubble with what was left of his body.

Pain and refusal twisted his face and he tried to claw Max away with a ruined arm.

Max bore down on the bayonet and ground the serrations along the bone until the thrashing stopped.

The senior instructor who had bent the battle with his own power died in the rubble with the vial still stoppered.

The relief in the group was plain. The man had been too dangerous to leave at large.

Max was about to pull the party back when another soldier ran up with a smoke black notebook in his hand.

*"Platoon leader. This was in the rubble with more robe scraps. Look."*
